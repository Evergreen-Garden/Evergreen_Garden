# Chat Rules and Etiquette

Chat rules exist to make the Garden a safe and cosy environment. We want this community to be a place where everyone can feel welcomed and wanted.
We would like you to read the following rules before joining the conversations on the server.

## Rules of decency
This set of rules is aimed at keeping conversations polite and respectful. Rules of decency are less strict than other rules, and breaking them does not entail immediate penalties. Instead, staff members will politely ask you to correct your behaviour. However, if one is to systematically ignore warnings from staff, moderators can penalise them with a 🟨 yellow card and a chat mute.

1. Don’t use hurtful language towards other members and respect their boundaries. If a person asks you to stop bothering them, respect it. Same goes for provoking conflicts;
2. Don’t insult or generalise groups of people with blanket statements. Saying “All X are Y” or “Screw all X” is not nice;
3. Respect Alderon and mod teams. We don’t allow bad-mouthing or slander towards hard-working developers and creators! Instead of saying “X is a terrible mod”, try saying “I personally don’t like this mod”;
4. Please include trigger warnings and spoiler tags for any content that can be disturbing or triggering for other people;
5. Please don’t spam the chat with an excess of memes, images, GIFs or copy-pasted and gibberish text. We have dedicated channels for memes and pictures, post them there instead of clogging general. No more than 3 GIFs, memes or distracting reaction images should be posted in a row, all images should be gone from the screen before any more may be posted;
6. Please avoid posting AI-generated images. If you have to post them, use spoiler tags and warnings. Not everyone wants to see these types of images.
7. Do not privately message any staff member about issues to do within the server or in game, please direct them into tickets
8. Show all Garden Staff respect when in conversations, especially when asked to drop a conversation or topic. Remember, our staff are all volunteers who spend their time in helping the server!
9. No mini-modding or vigilantism: do not assume a role of server moderator if you are not on Garden Staff. We can handle it ourselves!

## Taboos
Taboos are a set of strict rules that govern sensitive topics. Taboos are set in place to protect the Garden from malicious and hateful people. If one is to break a taboo, staff can immediately issue up to two 🟥 🟥 red cards depending on the severity and mute them.

1. No bigotry or discrimination : no sexism, no homophobia, no transphobia, no racism, no nationalism, no ableism, no religious fanaticism;
2. Respect science: no anti-scientific statements or pseudoscience, including young earth creationism, anti-vaxx, Covid or AIDS denialism;
3. Do not post explicit mature content or fetish art. Tasteful depictions of human form (like classical art and sculpture or anatomical illustrations) are allowed, but should __always__ come with a disclaimer and a spoiler tag;
4. No advertisement;
5. No politics;
6. Do not take other people’s private or personal information out of the Garden. This is a safe space;
7. Telling people that they should harm themselves in real life in any shape or form is not allowed (including threats of self-harm);
8. Don’t make light of historical or current human tragedies and don’t act approving or apologetic about atrocities or acts of cruelty;
9. Do not request staff assistance and then try killing said staff member.
