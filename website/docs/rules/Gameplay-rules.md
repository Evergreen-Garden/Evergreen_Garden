# Gameplay Rules

Gameplay rules are the core set of laws for all players of the Evergreen Garden. They are set in place to provide a fair and enjoyable experience in the vein of Speculative Realism.
If you encounter a rulebreak during your gameplay session, you can create a thread in this channel so that staff could help you: https://discord.com/channels/1287029235759714387/1363214367964795041
Breaking any of these rules will result in a player getting a verbal warning or a 🟨 yellow card. Staff is allowed to reprimand chronic rulebreaking in short succession by turning your dinosaur into a hatchling or kicking from the server!

## 1. Essential rules
- Do not spam-call;
- Do not friendly-bait or attack group members without consent;
- Do not revenge-kill;
- Do not defend your own body if you quick-log;
- Do not disclose the location of *other* players in chat;
- Do not log out from the game during combat;
- Do not deliberately target specific players or groups;
- Do not send random group or baby invites;
- Profiles take priority over general rules;
- Do not fully mesh and clip into walls to escape combat;
- Do not t-bag dead or living dinosaurs or simulate any inappropriate motions with your dinosaurs. Please. Just don’t.

## 2. Combat rules
- Detailed conflict rules are outlined here: https://discord.com/channels/1287029235759714387/1324766958796804139
- Carnivores can hunt or challenge other animals to a fight;
- Herbivores can challenge other animals to a fight with aggressive calls (with some exceptions);
- Any animal may defend its personal space without warning __but may not deliberately invade another animal’s personal space to exploit this__
- Interfering in an ongoing fight as a 3-rd party is not allowed (with some exceptions);
- When one of the participants dies, combat ends (with some exceptions);
- Any animal with a claimed body receives protection from being hunted;
- Please follow your animal’s conflict limitations outlined in the profile;
- Please give at least *2 minutes* of grace before engaging a party that you saw exit combat;
- Please give at least *2 minutes* of grace before attacking a player that you witnessed logging into the game and appearing in the world;
- Grouping up during combat is not allowed, and any additional group members cannot join the fight after 2 minutes! 
- Sleeping during any conflicts is now strictly prohibited;
- You have to wait at least 10 minutes before attempting to hunt the same party again;
- **All combat stops at 1 minute before server restart**.

## 3. Claiming bodies
- Any animal can claim a dead body to receive protection from being hunted;
- A carnivore may only leave a claimed body once it decomposes by at least 50%, and herbivores can leave whenever they want;
- If you witnessed a body down, you must wait 2 minutes before contesting (with the exception of corpse pirates);
- A carnivore may briefly leave the body to drink or waystone a group member;
- You **have** to claim a body obtained during a hunt, however you don’t have to claim a body obtained through self-defence or a challenge;
- If a carnivore stumbles upon a random vacant body, it may eat without claiming it;
- You are **not** allowed to contest claimed bodies of your fallen group-mates;
- You may reclaim the body if you leave and return to it being vacant;
- Some carnivores can scavenge and steal meat chunks from claimed bodies, while others have to challenge for them;
- Any carnivore can attack without warning to defend its claimed body;
- Herbivores can be contested off bodies by carnivores and have a right to defend the carcass and not allow the carnivore to feed;
- *Scavengers* can eat or steal from claimed bodies as long as the owner isn’t actively eating- that includes trophies as well!
- Chunking bodies to dispose of them quicker **IS NOT ALLOWED**;
- Switching dinosaurs to contest your own body is **not allowed**;
- Any body of a colossal animal counts as a “**Whalefall**”: carnivores don’t have to wait until it reaches 50% to leave, and *multiple* groups of carnivores can claim a *Whalefall* and feed from it together without having to challenge one-another! You may announce the whalefall location in global chat!

## 4. Body denial
- During conflicts, land animals are prohibited from deliberately drowning themselves or trying to die in way that will prevent the other party from accessing their carcass;
- Same rule applies to aquatic animals if they try to put their body too far onto land;
- This rule exists so that the victor could access either meat or the trophy that they earned in the conflict. Nobody likes getting no rewards for hard work.

## 5. Relationships between groups
- Herbivores and carnivores may coexist in peace, but they never work together or defend each other;
- Separate groups may occupy the same space and mingle briefly, but for the most part should stay separated to clearly indicate who’s part of the group and who’s not to any observing party;
- An exception from this rule are *Super-herds*, which can contain several families of herbivores travelling or resting together. However, the super-herd should disperse once predators strike, with only group members being able to come to the rescue of the attacked individual;
- “Cuddle-puddles” are **not** allowed. They are severely immersion breaking!
- Please don’t stay close to an ongoing conflict. Spectate from a distance, give the fighting parties space.

## 6. Mixed species groups
- Be sure to group only with species that are indicated in your animals’s profile!
- Most carnivores have rigid rules and can mix only with a few other species;
- Most herbivores have relaxed rules and can mix freely within 12 group slots;
- This rule exists to make the game have a more natural feel. In real life some organisms enjoy mutualistic relationships, but seeing a ragtag party in the style of “Land Before Time” may prove immersion-breaking.

## 7. Growth and development
- Animals go through 5 growth stages: hatchling, juvenile, adolescent, subadult and adult;
- Hatchlings, juveniles and adolescents are considered *immature* individuals;
- Subadults and adults are considered *mature* individuals;
- Immature animals count as belonging to **small** size category regardless of species, so watch out! They can be identified by the presence of a specific “baby skin” that completely fades out once the animal reaches subadult stage;
- Mature animals get a size category indicated in their profile.

## 8. Territorial behaviour
- An animal or a group can claim a source of water, food or a **small** area their personal territory and challenge anyone who tries to utilise the space or resources;
- *Giant land carnivores* automatically claim any area that they reside in and **have** to challenge off any other giant carnivores that try to set house near them. Giant predators are very sensitive to competition!
- Any nest counts as a personal territory, and any animal may attack without warning to defend its nest from intruders!

## 9. Sanctuaries
- *Sanctuaries* are safe zones where no conflicts can start. Predator and prey and deadly rivals should ignore each other when together in a sanctuary. You cannot run into a sanctuary zone to escape an ongoing conflict. Sanctuaries include:
- **Waystones**: the mystical energies surrounding the ancient standing stones of Gondwa radiate a calming presence that pacifies the visiting animals.
