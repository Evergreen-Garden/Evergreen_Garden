# Penalty system

This channel details the server's rulebreak penalty system.  If you end up breaking any rules set by this server, don’t panic!  Our moderation team uses a forgiving *card* system which ensures that minor transgressions are appropriately recorded and able to be learned from, while chronic rulebreaking activity is stopped in its tracks.

⭐  Staff keeps track of individual account status behind-the-scenes.<br/>
⭐  You can **ALWAYS** make a ticket inquiring about your account status and card count!<br/>
[🎫 Make a ticket here!](discord://-/channels/1287029235759714387/1363214367964795041/1472286026327265444?fallbackUrl=https://discord.com/channels/1287029235759714387/1363214367964795041/1472286026327265444&ofl=https://discord.com/channels/1287029235759714387/1363214367964795041/1472286026327265444)

## How Cards Work
### 🟨 Yellow & 🟥 Red Cards
Minor rulebreaks result in a single *yellow* card; a small reminder to respect the rules set by this community.  These may be added to your account status when a moderator witnesses a rulebreak 'live' in-game or in Discord chat channels, or after receiving and processing reports from tickets.  

If you collect 2 active yellow cards, they transform into 1 *red* card. These take longer to decay:

* A yellow card is 'active' for 14 days.
* A red card is 'active' for 30 days.

After 30 days, the red card 'decays' into a single active yellow card.  This 'new' yellow card begins its own decay process: after 14 days, it will disappear.  Any further yellow cards received during this process will cause a *new* red card to be created, which will begin its own 30 day decay process.

⚠️  If your account status reaches **2 reds cards**, moderators will attempt to send you an in-game warning via personal announcement.
⚠️  If your account status reaches **3 red cards**, moderators will attempt to send you an in-game personal announcement, and then [ban](https://discord.com/channels/1287029235759714387/1483488459963236412/1483859719738364045) your account from the game server. 
❗ If we notice suspicious behaviour (for example: multiple decayed cards, then a period of new rulebreaks) you might be pulled into a ticket to speak with staff.

### ⬛  Black Cards
In cases of rulebreaks involving a [Chat Etiquette Taboo](https://discord.com/channels/1287029235759714387/1324769355136897044), a black card may be added to your record at admin-level staff's discretion. 
* Unlike yellow or red cards, black cards ***never* decay**.
* Breaking of a Taboo rule may result in an **instant** ban, regardless of account status.

## How Bans Work
An in-game ban is put into place when a player's file reaches **three red cards**: the equivalent of six *active* rulebreaks on record. Severe etiquette rulebreaks or acts of harm against the server, its players, or its moderation team may result in an **immediate** ban.
### 📚 Ban Appeal Process
If you find that you have been banned and would like to appeal for a second chance, follow these steps:
1. Create a Ban Appeal ticket thread in this channel:
:::info
Ask the admins for the channel.
:::
2. In your ticket thread, provide the following information:
    - Ping an admin.
    - Your in-game username and Alderon ID. Example: `dinoguy [111-222-333]`
    - Provide us any information you're *aware of* about your ban! Ideally, you were sent an in-game personal announcement with information.
3. Then, admin-level staff will talk directly with you in your ticket thread. This process does *not* guarantee an un-ban.

### 📗  Ban Probation Process
After you have spoken with staff in your ban appeal ticket, your account will be placed on either *cooldown* or *probation*: 

**⏸️ Cooldown:**
This is a period of time where your account will remain banned from the in-game server. 
* This may range from 2 weeks to 1.5 months depending on the type or severity of your rulebreaks, at admin discretion.
* During this time, we encourage you to become more familiar with the server ruleset and the community of the Discord server. Interact, ask questions, and learn. 
* After your given cooldown date, **make another appeal ticket**. From there staff will grant your account...

**✅ Probation:**
* You'll be allowed into the server to play again
* Your card count will be reset to **2 red cards**, which will begin the normal decay process.
* If your account reaches **3 red cards** again, your account will be permanently banned.
