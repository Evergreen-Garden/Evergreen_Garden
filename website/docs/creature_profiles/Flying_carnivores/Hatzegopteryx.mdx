# Hatzegopteryx

## Known species
- *H. thambema*: +10% flight stamina;
- *H. philadelphiae*: +5% armor;
- *H. gigantea*: +3% attack damage;

**Size category**: large;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, noxious amphibians, animals remains, fish;<br/>
**Nest materials**: bones, rock, mud;

## In real life
*Hatzegopteryx* was a giant azhdarchid pterosaur that lived in the Late Cretaceous Romania. Unlike other azhdarchids, that were light and spindly built,  *Hatzegopteryx* had short and robust neck vertebrae and a massive skull, features that would suggest that this pterosaur was adapted for macropredation. During the Late Cretaceous, the Hatzeg Basin where it was found was an isolated Hatzeg Island inhabited by dwarf dinosaurs. *Hatzegopteryx* was the largest carnivore on Hatzeg, and likely was the apex predator.
*Hatzegopteryx* was possibly the heaviest animal to ever take to the wing.

## In the Garden
On the island of Gondwa, *Hatzegopteryx* is a true giant of the skies. It dwarfs every other flying animal, and even its closest cousin *Quetzalcoatlus* has to make way. This animal pushes the limits of physics for airborne creatures. *Hatzegopteryx* is a mecacing hunter and a scavenger, using its bulk to either overpower prey on the ground or bully any other pterosaur away from a carcass. Its neck is more flexible and stress-resistant than in any other azhdarchid, and its beak looks more like a skull-splitting war glaive than a pair of chopsticks. The tradeoff to this robustness is a very heavy manner of flying that takes skill and experience to master for any growing *Hatzegopteryx*.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Is significantly meaner and more aggressive about its personal space than *Quetzalcoatlus*;
- Prefers to nest either on high cliffs or on islands;
- When hungry can visit the coastline to scavenge carcasses of beached sharks;
- *Hatzegopteryx* is a very heavy flyer. It doesn’t usually stay in the air for long, mainly using its wings to escape danger and travel the island. It spends most of its time on the ground, looking for prey on foot; Nevertheless, it can cross vast distances on wing;
- Prefers not to fly for long in bad weather;
- Nesting pairs court each-other by gifting dead critters and trophies from hunted dinosaurs;
- Nesting *Hatzegopteryx* are sometimes known to construct enormous decorated eyries using large roots and branches to impress their mate;
- Parents are sometimes known to throw their flaplings from high places to teach them flight;
- Their agility on the ground lets them hunt in any terrestrial habitats from deserts to woodlands.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have up to 3 mature individuals in a group, called a murder;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called flaplings;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhycnus*, which often frock around the giant pterosaur as a swarm of scavengers.

## Conflicts
- Azhdarchids peck first, ask questions later. This means that they could attempt to hunt anything they lay eyes upon;
- Can contest any carnivore for a carcass;
- Can contest any other pterosaur for territory, including their kind;
- Can contest any small or medium carnivore for territory, and are quite mean at that; 
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated flaplings as a quick snack;
- **Corpse bully**: can ignore 2-minute grace timer when challenging other animals for carcasses.


## Other interactions
- Do not tolerate *Quetzalcoatlus* at their kills, viciously chasing them off;
- Dominate all other scavengers and make them wait until *Hatzegopteryx* fills up;
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed bodies as long as the owner isn’t actively eating.

## 👹 Deviant profile *the Raven Lord*
- Sometimes old solitary *Hatzegopteryx* become a significant and recognised presence in pterosaur colonies: they inspire fear and respect, and lesser winged creatures flock to their side and fly in their shadow;
- Such individuals are known as *Raven Lords*, and are followed by an entourage of smaller pterosaurs that herald their coming and swarm their enemies;
- A *Raven Lord* can freely group with *Thalassodromeus* and *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- There can only be a single *Raven Lord* in a group;
- This pterosaur grouping is known as a *Raven Court* and can hunt animals of any size;
- The *Raven Court* can still use *Corpse Bully* rule;
- This is a mutualistic relationship, but all participants still follow mostly egotistical interests, trying to snatch a bite for themselves first and foremost.

## Survival tips
- Because of its great mass, *Hatzegopteryx* needs a long run-up before it could launch itself into the air. Be careful not to get caught while you take off!
- *Hatzegopteryx* is not much of a swimmer, so don’t fall into the water!
- *Hatzegopteryx* has a very strong momentum while flying. This means that it is able to reach incredible speed in the air, but at the same time is hard to control;
- Grounded *Hatzegopteryx* can swiftly spin in place to face incoming opponents;
- Common nickname: hatz;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
