# Quetzalcoatlus

## Known species
- *Q. northropi*;

**Size category**: large;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, animal remains, mollusks;<br/>
**Supplementary food**: fish;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, mud, roots;

## In real life
*Quetzalcoatlus* was a giant pterosaur from the Late Cretaceous of North America. It belonged to the family *Azhdarchidae*, which included the largest flying animals known to science. They were tall, long-necked and long-billed animals with a towering presence. *Quetzalcoatlus* itself was a terrestrial stalker of small prey, foraging on the ground with its long thin beak. Despite its enormous size, it was still a capable and efficient flyer.

## In the Garden
On the island of Gondwa, the shadow of *Quetzalcoatlus* wings strikes fear into the hearts of all creatures small enough to fit into its enormous beak. This giant pterosaurian menace is a masterful thermal soarer and is even more adept at moving on the ground. Standing taller than a *Tyrannosaurus*, it roams the grasslands in search of small prey to terrorise. It is a curious and intelligent generalist that uses every opportunity to get a snack, be it a young dinosaur, a carcass or a delicious critter.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Sexual dimorphism: males often sport a larger head crest or a stockier build than females. This is not a hard rule, but merely a trend;
- Often slowly soar above open areas like grasslands and deserts in search of food;
- Often visit freshwater habitats to probe silt for mollusks;
- Don’t shy away from spending a lot of time on the ground, taking graceful strides across the fields and being inquisitive;
- Prefer to nest on rocks and cliffs;
- Especially likes to nest near waterfalls;
- Is an incredibly efficient flyer and can cruise in the air for long periods of time;
- Prefers to avoid taking extensive flights in bad weather and at night when thermal updrafts can’t help them fly;
- Nesting pairs court each-other by stretching their wings and gifting small objects;
- Are better parents than most other pterosaurs. 

## Friendly interactions 
- Can have 2 mature individuals in a group;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called flaplings;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhycnus*, often employing the small pterosaur’s services to preen their coats and eat bloodsucking parasites from their wing membranes;

## Conflicts
- Azhdarchids peck first, ask questions later. This means that they could attempt to hunt anything they lay eyes upon;
- Can contest any carnivore for a carcass;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated flaplings as a quick snack.

## Other interactions
- Can attempt to steal meat chunks from kills made by other carnivores at their own risk;
- Often circle above active hunts like vultures waiting for a body to drop to get a bite;
- Communal feeders: can tolerate unrelated *Quetzalcoatlus* and other pterosaurs at their carcasses to butcher the meat more easily;
- Highly tolerant of other *Quetzalcoatlus*, with unrelated individuals and pairs often resting and foraging together;
- Often congregate around *Argentinosaurus*, using the giant sauropods as a protective presence and a landmark to gather and socialise.


## Survival tips
- Your best way to escape other flying carnivores is to use your superior air mobility and aerial attacks;
- You can easily damage yourself when colliding with objects, so fly carefully;
- You are bad at swimming, so try not to land in the water!
- Common nickname: quetz;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
