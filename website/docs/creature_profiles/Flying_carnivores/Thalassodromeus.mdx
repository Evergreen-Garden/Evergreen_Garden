## 🇧🇷 In real life
*Thalassodromeus* was a large pterosaur that lived in the Early Cretaceous of what is now Brazil. This flying reptile possessed a gigantic head crest, one of the largest known among pterosaurs. This structure was likely used for display and may have been colourful in life. The crest made *Thalassodromeus* as tall as a human person.
Although upon discovery it was first proposed to be a seagoing fisher, it is now accepted that *Thalassodromeus* was a generalist land hunter that used its massive beak to gobble up small terrestrial animals. *Thalassodromeus* possessed one of the proportionally highest bite forces known among pterosaurs.

## In the Garden
Cutting through the skies of the Garden, *Thalassodromeus* is a blade-beaked hunter.
Its gigantic crest may appear unwieldy, but it is light and durable, serving both for flamboyant display, and as a reinforced helmet.
Travelling upon the stormy gales, this predatory pterosaur appears where trouble strikes. A wounded animal. A nest left without parents. A marine giant stranded helplessly upon the shore. Some may call these disasters: for *Thalassodromeus* they are… divestment opportunities.
On the ground, long limbs allow *Thalassodromeus* to run down and swallow any creature small enough to fit in its mouth. In the air, it is a deadly acrobat, diving, dodging and crashing into its enemies at high speed.


## 🏝️ Habitat preference
- **Coastal**: *Thalassodromeus* can often be found running along tidal flats and coasts in search of small animals to eat;
- **Wetland**: marshy habitats provide countless opportunities for all-terrain hunters;
- **Scrubland**: often patrol dry brush in search of prey.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- *Thalassodromeus* are bold generalist hunters that don’t shy away from most feeding opportunities and harass their prey with vicious determination;
- *Thalassodromeus* are very nimble on the ground and can spend a lot of time patrolling on foot;
- *Thalassodromeus* can be quite noisy when gathering in large numbers;
- Are not afraid to bully larger pterosaurs when needed;
- Curious by nature;
- Often circle around large gatherings of dinosaurs looking for… opportunities;
- During courtship gift sticks, shells and crystals to one-another and perform flashy dances;
- Often gather around fresh kills of other predators and patiently wait until the hunter finished its meal, only to swarm the body the moment it becomes safe to feed;
- Petty bickering and establishing a pecking order is common in large gatherings.


## 💛 Grouping
- A group of 2-4 mature individuals is called a squabble;
- A group of 5-6 mature individuals is called a bazaar;
- A group of *Thalassodromeus* counts as a *large* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can freely group with *Rhamphorhynchus*, flying together;
- Can include a single *Raven Lord Hatzegopteryx* in a group.

## 🪺 Reproduction
- **Nest materials**: branches, reeds;
- Prefer to nest on high rocks and steep cliffs that are out of reach for predators;
- Often extravagantly decorate their nests to impress potential mates;
- Young pterosaurs are called flaplings;
- *Thalassodromeus* are unfocused parents, and often just shove food into a pile of screaming flaplings, paying no attention towards who actually survives as long as someone gets fed;
- Unlike neonate birds, *Thalassodromeus* flaplings become independent and flight-capable very shortly after hatching;
- **Colonial nesters**: group members prefer to nest in close proximity, forming noisy nesting colonies;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.


## 🍖 Hunting
- A lone *Thalassodromeus* may hunt animals of up to *huge* size;
- A squabble (2-4) may hunt animals of any size;
- A bazaar (5-6) may hunt animals of *large* size and above together: for lesser prey only a squabble must engage;
- Don’t hunt their own species;
- *Raven Lord* groups can hunt targets of large size and above together.

## 🦴 Player carcasses
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner isn’t actively eating;
- Can challenge animals within hunting limits for claimed carcasses;
- May freely eat from *Thalassodromeus* corpses.


## 🚩 Claiming territory
- A group (2+) of *Thalassodromeus* may claim a nesting site as their *Rookery*;
- Rookeries are often located at cliff edges and in natural cavities;
- The group owning the Rookery may warn any creatures to stay away and attack the intruders if they ignore multiple warnings;
- *Thalassodromeus* can be especially aggressive towards other groups of their own species, as prime nesting estate is limited: intraspecific dogfighting is not uncommon.


### ☂️ Survival tips:
- *Thalassodromeus* can specialise into a ground-hunting or dogfighting style!
- Air diving from big heights gives you great speed and momentum - as well as collision damage to anything that you ram into;
- *Thalassodromeus* can soar for short periods without losing much stamina;
- You can use the surface of the water to rest and regain stamina;
- *Thalassodromeus* can dive, but is not very mobile in the water;
- Common nickname: thal, thalasso;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️ (easy)
