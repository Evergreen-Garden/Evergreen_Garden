# Rhamphorhynchus

## Known species
- *R. longicaudus*: +20% faster cooldowns;
- *R. etchesi*: x2 health stealing;
- *R. muensteri*: +10% swim speed;

**Size category**: small;<br/>
**Diet**: fish, meat;<br/>
**Supplementary food**: blood;<br/>
**Nest materials**: branches, reeds;<br/>

## In real life
*Rhamphorhynchus* was a small long-tailed pterosaur that soared above the waves of what is now Germany during the Late Jurassic, when most of Europe was covered in sea. It was most likely diving and swimming to catch small fish, while sometimes becoming prey to larger fish itself. Evidence suggests that *Rhamphorhynchus* was adapted for a nocturnal lifestyle, likely feeding on deepwater fish that migrated to the surface at night.

## In the Garden
In the skies of Gondwa, *Rhamphorhynchus* represents one of the smallest pterosaurs. Feeding of fish and small critters, it is completely harmless to most larger animals. However, this noisy winged trickster is known to form alliances with many deadly predators, guiding them towards prey in exchange for being able to peck at the kill.

## Roleplaying guidelines
- By default, *Rhamphorhynchus* are fishers that use their spiny teeth to expertly catch slippery aquatic prey. They can be found congregating both around freshwater and at sea;
- Prefer to nest on rocks and ledges near riverbanks and seashores close to their fishing spots;
- However, some of them have learned that sticking near bigger carnivores can win them a free meal. They follow other predators around like an entourage, helping them with locating and even harassing prey to eat the spoils afterwards;
- Such mutualistic rhamphs are even known to sometimes build nests near their chosen carnivore’s nest and even play with their young to build tolerance between the species and mutual disease immunity;
- Some *Rhamphorhynchus* are known to serve as asymptomatic vectors for dangerous zoonotic diseases;
- Are especially vocal and active under the light of the Moon, being nocturnally inclined.


## Friendly interactions
- Can have up to 12 mature individuals within a group, called a swarm;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called flaplings;
- Can group with any carnivores, forming a mutualistic relationship with them.

## Conflicts
- Can help grouped carnivores in hunts, but the death of a *Rhamphorhynchus* in a mixed group __does not__ end conflict if the rhamph wasn’t specifically the intended target;
- Hurt and sick individuals can exhibit parasitic behaviour by sucking blood from larger animals to replenish strength: blood offers little nutritional value, but contains components that help them heal;
- If not grouped with other species can interfere with any ongoing conflicts as a 3-rd party, spreading chaos and mayhem;
- Can hunt land critters if feeling bold!

## Other interactions
- Can scavenge, attempt to share a meal with random carnivores or steal meat chunks at their own risk;
- Can attempt to perch on herbivores at their own risk.

## Survival tips
- Latching onto a target doesn’t drain stamina unless you are getting bucked off;
- *Rhamphorhynchus* recovers stamina 25% faster and drains hunger slower at night;
- “*Lucky feather*” can save you from a lethal blow;
- You can regain stamina while resting on the water’s surface;
- Common nickname: rhamph;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️ (easy)

## Roleplaying inspiration
- Seagulls and terns for a fishing rhamph;
- Oxpeckers, honeyguides and ravens for mutualistic rhamph;
- Bats can also be a point of inspiration.

## Etiquette
- We kindly ask you not to spam the “*Rat Out*” ability. It has the potential to be rather irritating and immersion-breaking if someone makes the whole POI scream without end. Use it with wisdom and temperance.
