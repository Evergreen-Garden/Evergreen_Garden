# Halszkaraptor

- *H. anasilliei*: +25% defense;

**🦆 Size category**: small;
**🐟 Diet**: fish, amphibians (even poisonous), insects, mollusks, berries, fruit, ground vegetation, fungi;
**🌐 Range**: Gondwa, Beringia.

## 🇲🇳 In real life
*Halszkaraptor* was a small bird-like dinosaur that lived in what is now Mongolia during the Lake Cretaceous. It belonged to the famous “raptor” family - *Dromaeosauridae*, and possessed a typical sickle claw on its second toe. Unlike many other dromaeosaurs, it was not built like a ground hawk. Instead, it was built like a duck! It had a long neck and a spoon-like snout filled with small sharp teeth. It is debated if *Halszkaraptor* was a semi-aquatic animal or not, and most current evidence points towards it being fully terrestrial, but one thing is certain: it was a very unique raptor.

## In the Garden
In the lakes and rivers of The Garden, *Halszkaraptor* is a tiny semi-aquatic omnivore. In fact, it is one of the smallest dinosaurs you can find here! Being the size of a literal mallard duck, it leads a humble life under the feet of giants. Mostly found in freshwater habitats, this miniature fluffy swimmer fills the niche of a nimble fish eater. It can hold its breath to dive underwater and chase after small aquatic prey. Unlike most of its raptor relatives, *Halszkaraptor* also consumes a wide variety of plants and can sometimes be found grazing in the company of larger wetland herbivores. The important thing is not to get squished under foot!


## 🪷 Habitat
- Mostly inhabit freshwater lakes and rivers;
- Rare marine populations exist as well, hunting for fish in ocean waters;
- Some individuals lead a fully terrestrial lifestyle, making home in fields and forests and feeding mostly on insects;
- Prefer to nest in the waterside reeds. A single raptor can build a spectacularly large nest for its size, often protected by logs, roots and more reeds;
- This giant circular nest serves as a dancing stage to perform courtship display, as safe a house, and later - as a little playground for the neonates;
- Often nest on lake and river islands if they are available;

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Dart around in the water hunting for fish;
- Often perch and rest on fallen trees, logs or rocks near the water's edge, preening their feathers;
- Can rest while floating on the water surface;
- *Halszkaraptor* is a social and noisy animal! They are fond of fooling around and playing;
- Have a large repertoire of calls and dances for social signalling and courtship: when courting, two raptors will mirror each-other’s moves;
- Fish and amphibians represent the bulk of their diet, but as omnivores, *Halszkaraptors* also like to go for sweet berries and flowers;
- *Halszkaraptor’s* tiny body has a very high metabolism, so it demands food almost constantly;
- *Halszkaraptors* are a curious bunch and enjoy spectating large animals;
- Feel safe in the company of larger semi-aquatic herbivores and love to follow them around;
- Sometimes live inside *Lisowicia* burrows;
- Can be found cleaning the teeth of basking crocodilomorphs.


## 🩵 Grouping
- Can have up to 12 mature individuals in a group, called a waddle or a flock;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fits in a group, called ducklings.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- A family of *Halszkaraptors* can adopt a single baby *Deinocheirus* as their ugly duckling.

## ⚡Aggression
- *Halszkaraptors* may exhibit a misplaced confidence and can try and challenge animals of up to… medium size (*Wow!!*)! Results may prove sad;
- Have a heightened aggression around their nest and ducklings, and may chase off small and medium animals with hissing and scratching. It does not pursue animals that choose to leave;
- Members of a flock can duel for prime nesting spots, biting and kicking;
- If *Halszkaraptors* get accidentally killed in a mixed group whilst not being a target of a hunt, they don’t count as a body down.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Halszkaraptor* is not a fighter. Nor is it useful in any capacity. It exists. It is cute. That’s all it does;
- *Halszkaraptor* has moderate water drain while in the water, but loses thirst at a faster rate while on land!
- Your incredibly tiny size means that it is very easy to hide and disappear into the vegetation;
- The duck raptor possesses very high mobility in the water and can swim circles around most aquatic predators;
- Remember that most creatures can kill you in one blow;
- Most critters are larger than you and pose significant threat;
- “*Omnivore*” diet lets you eat mushrooms to quickly regenerate health!
- You can double jump! Flap those tiny wings!
- Common nicknames: halsz, halszka, duck, duckling;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️ (easy)
