# Deinocheirus

## 🔎 Known species
- *D. mirificus*: +25% venom and bleed healing while dry;
- *D. grandis*: +10% health recovery;
- *D. hirsutae*: +5% damage while wet;

🦛 **Size category**: giant;
🕑 **Passive growth time**: 3 hours 20 minutes;
🔷 **Group size**: 2

## 🍽️ Diet
- **Herbivore**: leaves, fruit;
- **Fisher**: fish, mollusks.


## 🇲🇳 In real life
*Deinocheirus* was a highly unusual theropod dinosaur from the Late Cretaceous of what is now Mongolia. Although it was a relative of swift ornithomimids, *Deinocheirus* itself was massive and bulky, with a tall hump on its back. It was initially known from just the giant clawed arms, and the true appearance of *Deinocheirus* remained an enigma for many decades.
The body plan and environment of this animal indicates that *Deinocheirus* inhabited the ancient wetlands, and was neighbours with such animals as *Therizinosaurus*, *Barsboldia* and the mighty *Tarbosaurus* that possibly left some bite marks on the belly ribs of one of the known *Deinocheirus* specimens. It had a highly pneumatised skeleton with lots of hollow spaces. Both gastroliths for grinding plant matter and fish scales were found in association with its belly ribs.

## In the Garden
In lakes and swamps of The Garden, *Deinocheirus* is a gargantuan wetland omnivore. A most bizarre of beasts with a mismatched appearance, it feels at home both on land and in the water. Somewhat oblivious and unpredictable, sometimes it acts dangerously hectic for such a gigantic creature, resulting in a multi-ton animal stomping around with awkward jumpiness of a small waterfowl. Tall enough to look a *Tyrannosaurus* in the eye and with arms that could break trees, an anxious fit from such a creature may have lethal results. If an animal like this feels endangered, you need to get out of the way…
There is also a certain animated curiosity present in the duck-billed giant’s eyes. As it clumsily wades through the wetlands, *Deinocheirus* adds just a little bit of whimsy to the water ecosystems of the Garden.


## 🪷 Habitat
- **Waterside**: *Deinocheirus* mainly inhabits the shores of lakes and rivers, foraging on local plants and fish;
- **Wetland**: *Deinocheirus* finds swamps and marshlands very hospitable;
- **Woodland**: *Deinocheirus* often visit lush forests to browse and feed.

## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- Big eater, and enjoys a varied diet that includes both animal protein and leafy greens;
- Prefers to rest and socialise in and around water, but is no stranger to going a bit farther onto land if needed;
- Use the sandy banks as the stage for attracting a mate using loud vocalisations and sometimes spouting fountains of water as a show;
- Love to run around, honk and play during the rain;
- Enjoy pushing over decaying trees;
- Can carry large amounts of water in its crop to spit at both enemies and friends;
- React nervously and jumpy to sudden sounds, and easily goes into panicked aggression;
- When there’s no danger, act relaxed, either sunbathing or jollily jogging around with earth-shaking steps;
- Possess a curious character and love to inspect new things and creatures. Often carry curious objects around in their beak;
- Enjoy *Hibbertopterus* cleaning their shaggy feathers;
- Often coexist peacefully with other semi-aquatic herbivores and even smaller carnivores that live in their lake;
- Generally tolerate other *Deinocheirus* individuals in their waters and like to socialise.


## 🩵 Grouping
- Can have a maximum of 2 mature individuals in a group, called a pair;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed-species grouping
- Can freely group with any *small-sized* herbivores;
- **Theropod Flock**: can freely group with *Citipati*;
- **Wetland crew**: can freely group with *Desmatosuchus*, *Lurdusaurus* and *Agathaumas*;
- Can include 1 deviant *Troubadour Lambeosaurus* in a group;
- May get adopted by a family of *Halzskaraptors*.

## 🪺 Reproduction
- **Nest materials**: roots, mud, branches;
- Prefer to construct their nests on lake and river islands, often covering the nest with large branches and carefully weaved grass;
- Young *Deinocheirus* are known as goslings;
- *Deinocheirus* is a protective parent and looks over its nest and offspring like a hawk;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.

## 🌳 Claiming food
- If under 75% hunger, may challenge any feeding herbivores away from a berry bush. Once the defeated party flees the vicinity of the bush, the contest is over, and the victor must eat up.


## 🚩 Claiming territory
- A *Deinocheirus* may choose to claim any body of water as its *Duckpond*;
- While wading in its *Duckpond*, a *Deinocheirus* may challenge any semi-aquatic or aquatic creatures to leave the body of water;
- Additionally, it can challenge any terrestrial creature that comes too close to the shoreline, however, it **does not** pursue them inland once they choose to leave;
- May use splashing and spitting water as a warning for a challenge;
- **Dry season rage**: when water quality drops below 25%, *Deinocheirus* becomes a lot more irritable and may attack any unwanted visitors **unprovoked**;
- At least *10 minutes* must pass between each unprovoked attack. However, there exists no cooldown on normal challenges.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Deinocheirus* is an ultimate survivor, being able to get land predators off its tail by jumping into water, and avoid aquatic carnivores by running onto dry land! Its flexibility and ability to utilise so many resources make this dinosaur very adaptable;
- You can build your *Deinocheirus* as a shoreline-based combatant that soaks enemies and delivers heavy wet blows;
- Alternatively, you can build your *Deinocheirus* as a primarily land-based fighter that shakes off any water like a duck and inflicts grievous bleeding woulds;
- You can also use an assortment of utility-based abilities that allow you to send your opponents flying with knockback, be faster while swimming or locating fish;
- Common nicknames: cherry, duck, goose;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

## 🫎 Roleplaying inspirations
