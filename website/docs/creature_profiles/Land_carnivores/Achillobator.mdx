# Achillobator

## Known species
- *A. giganticus*: +20% bleed damage;
- *A. calvus*: +50% acceleration speed;
- *A. barbatus*: +10% health recovery;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, noxious amphibians, animal remains;<br/>
**Supplementary food**: fish;<br/>
**Nest materials**: bones, rock, mud;

## In real life
*Achillobator* was a giant dromaeosaurid(raptor) from the Late Cretaceous of Mongolia. It was unusually heavily built, with a stocky frame unlike most other raptors. This indicates that it was not a particularly fast runner, but instead relied on brute force to ambush and overpower large prey. Like all dromaeosaurids, it had a great sickle claw on its second toe.
The animal represented in game is oversized compared to real life, but we like to see it as a stand in for the truly gigantic speculative “Bissekty dromaeosaur” known from extremely fragmentary remains from Uzbekistan.

## In the Garden
*Achillobator* is by far the largest raptor on the isle of *Gondwa*. It is robust and heavily-built for its size, an ambush predator outfitted with deadly sickle-shaped talons. It is an eerily intelligent hunter that often carefully tracks and studies its prey before a strike. And while one raptor is a deadly menace for things its own size, they often come in a family. Utilising teamwork and their claws uniquely adapted for latching, they can pounce the sides of giant dinosaurs and strike with deadly precision, making them specialised slayers of giants.
*Achillobator* is a king among raptors that makes even the top-order carnivores of the island shiver with fear.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Often prowl obscured habitats like rocky areas and woodlands, avoiding spending too much time in open terrain, as speed isn’t its strongest quality;
- Prefers to nest on protected rocks and cliffs and in crevices, where it can build a large comfortable eyrie or a lair;
- Have an uncanny fancy for carefully decorating their nests with morbid trophies taken from their kills, which almost takes the form of art. This also serves as a means of displaying fitness for potential mates;
- Each raptor can have a distinct personality and individual habits;
- Lone raptors are elusive and stealthy and like to set up a perfect ambush;
- Family groups are very well coordinated and socially organised. Such convocations specialise in hunting giant animals, taking turns latching onto their sides and targeting their weak spots;
- Communicate using elaborate feather displays and iris flicking;
- Prefer to rest on hight rocks inaccessible for most animals, watching their hunting grounds.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have a maximum of 4 mature individuals in a group called a convocation;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called eaglets;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus* and often deliberately attract them with food;
- Can substitute 2 of their group members with a *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*.

## Conflicts
- As a specialist in taking down large prey, *Achillobator* can hunt or challenge animals of any size.
- Often absolutely hate *Utahraptors*, bullying them for territory at any opportunity.

## Other interactions
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner isn’t actively eating.


## Deviant profile *Mob Boss*
- In times of scarcity, female *Achillobators* switch to brood parasitism and stealthily plant their eggs into the nests of *Deinonychus* and *Letanivenatrix*. The parasitic eaglet grows among its new brood learning the social signals of smaller raptor species, and slowly begins to dominate its adoptive family;
- When such changelings grow up, they become *Mob Bosses*;
- A *Mob Boss* is an *Achillobator* that can group with a maximum of 5 small raptors and command them as minions;
- The resulting pack can hunt and challenge anything;
- Immature *Achillobator* can also get adopted into nests of small raptors to represent brood parasitism.

## Survival tips
- *Achillobator* can only latch onto very large targets. This includes __all__ *giant* and *colossal* animals and the heaviest of *large* animals (*Lisowicia*, *Stegosaurus*, *Suchomimus*, *Sarcosuchus*);
- When latching onto a target, avoid pouncing the areas that have weapons nearby, like ceratopsian shoulders or stegosaurian hips, as you can still be hit;
- You are slower than some large carnivores that can easily kill you, so always have a plan of retreat in mind, for example, sticking to advantageous terrain;
- Remember that you can jump higher than most other medium carnivores!
- Common nickname: achillo;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (challenging)
