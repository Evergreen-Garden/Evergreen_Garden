## 🇺🇸 In real life
*Tyrannosaurus rex* is the largest land carnivore known to science. This beast of legendary status struck fear into Late Cretaceous North America. It is known from the Hell Creek formation from the time at the very tail end of the Mesozoic. This King saw the twilight and the end of the dinosaurs.
As the largest member of *Tyrannosauridae*, *T. rex* was a top order carnivore with a bone-crushing bite and immensely robust frame. It is one of the most well studied dinosaurs, with extensive information known about its growth, ecology and life history thanks to many intricately preserved fossils. Massive herbivores such as *Edmontosaurus* and *Triceratops* served as game for the royal feast. *T. rex* is known to have exhibited violent intraspecific hostility, with several skulls bearing bite marks from fellow *Tyrannosaurs*.

## In the Garden:
A great shadow silently moves through the trees. A pair of bright, front-facing eyes pierces the darkness with eery intelligence. A barely audible, but immensely powerful rumble vibrates the ground. The Tyrant King of the dinosaurs is on the hunt, and someone will die tonight.
An oddity among the megatheropods of the Garden, it grew to be larger, stronger and smarter than any other one of them. Where carnosaurs rely on bloodloss and prolonged attrition, *Tyrannosaurus* simply pulverises prey between its jaws with a force of a hydraulic press. The attack is always shockingly fast, brutal and calculated: it is usually over in a matter of seconds. And once the king’s hunger is satisfied, *Tyrannosaurus* disappears back into the woods…
The greatest horror is not witnessing a roaring monstrosity. True dread looks like a pair of watchful eyes and sounds like silence.
And then a branch snaps.


## ⛰️ Habitat preference
- **Woodland**: *Tyrannosaurus* is an ambush predator that enjoys the shadowy veil of deep forest;
- **Wetland**: *Tyrannosaurus* prefers wet habitats with high water availability, such as marshlands and flooded forests;
- **Coastal**: a surprisingly good swimmer, *Tyrannosaurus* often travels from island to island in search of food;
- **Desert**: in contrast, *Tarbosaurus* can be found wandering deep inland interiors, often venturing into dry and inhospitable environments.


## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- Does not like being seen. *Tyrannosaurus* lurks in the shadows observing its kingdom;
- Tend to be silent and communicate mostly with deep infrasonic rumbles, but let out a loud and terrible roar when broadcasting over their territory or trying to intimidate rivals;
- Careful and strategic hunters;
- When hunting, *Tyrannosaurus* prefers a swift and efficient kill over a drawn-out fight, and are known to abandon hunts that take too much of their time;
- Choose well protected lairs and caves for sleep and rest;
- Get confused and irritated when caught in the open;
- *Tyrannosaurus imperiosus*, although a very close relative of *T. rex*, possesses some notable differences. It is slightly smaller in stature, but is more heavily built. It has more generalised feeding habits and can subsist on poisonous and rotten meat that usually repulses other *Tyrannosaurus* species. Most of these traits likely evolved as adaptations for a colder climate. Both species are known to successfully interbreed where their ranges meet;
- *Tyrannosaurus* is a beast of habit that enjoys comforts of a routine. When travelling, they often visit several favourite spots for bathing and resting that attracted their fancy over the years;
- *Tyrannosaurus* possesses great memory;
- Often form monogamous pairs, sometimes even mating for life. The partners usually take great care of each other;
- Gifting a bloodied trophy to a potential mate greatly increases the chances of winning their heart.


## ♥️ Grouping
- A group can consist of 1-2 mature individuals;
- **Minor group**: 1 individual is called a loner;
- **Family group**: 2 mature individuals are called a pair.
- A pair of *Tyrannosaurus* is considered a *colossal* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Both official and modded species of *Tyrannosaurus* can group with each-other;
- Can freely group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- Can have up to 2 *Royal Adjutant Deinonychus* in a group;
- A lone *Tyrannosaurus* can group with up to 2 deviant *Royal Marquis Alioramus*;
- *Adjutant Deinonychus* and *Marquis Alioramus* are incompatible with one-another!

## 🪺 Reproduction
- Nest materials: bones, rock, mud;
- Prefer to build nests in dense shadowed woodlands and inside spacious caverns;
- *Tyrannosaurus* nests are often littered with bones that the youngsters use as chewing toys;
- Young *Tyrannosaurus* are known as princelings;
- Adults usually exhibit great parental care over hatchlings, but the growing tyrants rather quickly strike into the world on their own;
- Parents often gently carry the young in their jaws;
- Pairs usually temporarily separate to raise young, with only one parent being present with the clutch most of the time;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.


## 🍖 Hunting
- **Minor group**: a lone (1) *Tyrannosaurus* may hunt animals of any size: nothing is off the king’s menu;
- **Family group**: A pair (2) may only go for targets of *giant* size and above together. For hunting smaller prey, only one of a pair may engage. Small prey is not a worthy meal for a royal couple;
- A group consisting of only immature individuals counts as a **Minor group** for hunting restrictions;
- **Infanticidal**: can hunt immature *Tyrannosaurus*;
- Do not hunt other mature *Tyrannosaurus* (although devouring fallen territorial rivals is very common).

## 🦴 Carcass interactions
- Can challenge any creature for a claimed carcass;
- Can freely feed from *Tyrannosaurus* carcasses;
- Immature individuals count as **scavengers**.


## 🚩 Claiming a territory
- **Hyper-territorial**: automatically claim any area they reside in and **have** to contest any other giant land carnivores they come across, otherwise they must leave;
- Any area of residence of *Tyrannosaurus* automatically becomes its *Kingdom*;
- Have to contest any other giant land carnivores or leave the territory;
- Optionally, may view any *huge* land carnivores as competition as well and challenge them;
- After being exiled from territory, must wait a minimum of 15 minutes before returning;
-  *Tyrannosaurus* is very possessive over the land it lords over and shows special hostility to any other *Tyrannosaurus* that show up in their domain;
- May consume the rival *Tyrannosaurus* that fell during a territory contest;
- Immature individuals don’t participate in territorial behaviour, and can be tolerated by adults.

### ☂️ Survival tips:
- *Tyrannosaurus* is the slowest out of giant land carnivores, but makes up for it with high damage, fracture and ambushing capabilities;
- *Tyrannosaurus* can utilise fracture effect to slow down prey or increase damage output. Use it strategically!
- *Tyrannosaurus* moves very quietly and very fast while crouching. Use it for stealth ambushes!
- Most medium and small animals can be grabbed in your jaws;
- Swarms of smaller animals are one of the biggest threats to the bulky and cumbersome *Tyrannosaurus*. Try to avoid getting surrounded and find a defensive position!
- Common nickname: rex;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️(very challenging)
