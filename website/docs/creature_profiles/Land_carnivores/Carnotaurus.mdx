# Carnotaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *Carnotaurus sastrei*.

🐯 **Size category**: large<br/>
🥩 **Diet**: meat, noxious meat, carrion, bones.

## 🦴 In real life
*Carnotaurus* is a horned carnivorous dinosaur from the Late Cretaceous of Argentina. It had tiny utterly useless arms, but its legs were built for fast sprinting! The horns are hypothesised to be a sexual display feature, but the true purpose of the devilish headgear is still unknown.
*Carnotaurus* also preserved with patches of fossilised skin, revealing the exact appearance of this theropod’s scaly covering.
It was one of the largest members of its family, *Abelisauridae*, second only to the Brazilian *Pycnonemosaurus*.

## In the Garden
*Carnotaurus* is the speed demon of the Garden. With menacing horns and a hot temper, this troublemaker races across the fields ready to bash and pummel. *Carnotaurus* is an active and swift hunter, and is always on the move, almost never still.
There is no escape from the horned strider, for its legs are fast as the prairie wind.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Inhabits subtropical and tropical climate;
- Thrives in open areas such as prairies and deserts;
- Prefers to nest in ground scrub near a nice open mating display “dancefloor”;
- Sometimes comes down to coastal areas to scavenge dead sharks;
- Often stalk forest edges.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- **Sexual dimorphism**: on average males tend to have larger horns as a sexually selected feature, while females can afford more modest headgear. This is not a hard rule, but merely a trend;
- Additionally, males tend to be larger in size on average;
- Love to select clear open patches as a “dancefloor” to impress potential partners and intimidate rivals with display. This behaviour is more common among males;
- If a dance-off doesn’t work, competitors are ready to bash heads for territory. Such duels are rarely lethal, but they tend to leave one bruised and pummelled;
- Love to carry around sticks and bones, and chew on them for hours;
- Love to observe their hunting grounds from atop a hill or from under the shade of a lone tree;
- Habitually bump their head into inanimate objects to leave territorial marks… or just seemingly for fun;
- Highly nomadic.


## ♥️ Grouping
- A group of 2 mature individuals is called a pair;
- A group of 3 mature individuals is called a dash;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fits in a group;
- A group of *Carnotaurus* counts as a *huge* target.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can freely group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- Can freely group with *Pycnonemosaurus* (see deviant profile).

## 🍖 Hunting
- A lone *Carnotaurus* may hunt animals of up to *huge* size;
- A pair may hunt animals of any size;
- A dash may hunt animals of *giant* size and above together: for smaller prey only a pair should engage;
- **Partial cannibals**: may see immature and dead individuals as food.

## ⚡️ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of *medium*, *large* and *huge* size for territory;
- A solo can challenge animals of up to *huge* size a carcass;
- A group can challenge animals of any size for a carcass;
- A group of *Carnotaurus* may challenge and defend themselves together;
- Upon encountering each-other, *Carnotaurus* often bullfight for territory, but maintain non-lethality most of the time.

## 🍗 Other interactions
- **Scavenger**: may eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner is not actively eating.


## 👹 Deviant profile *Romeo* and *Juliette*
- Sometimes a *Carnotaurus* mistakes its larger cousin *Pycnonemosaurus* for a large member of their own kind and tries to court it, being lovestruck by the tall stranger…
- Such misguided hopeless individuals are known as *Romeos* or *Juliettes*. A forbidden love between rival species;
- Many *Pycnonemosaurus* reject such advances, but there are some that reciprocate this romantic endeavour. The resulting mixed-species grouping is called a *Hustle*;
- A *Hustle* can include any number of *Carnotaurus* and *Pycnonemosaurus* within group slot limits: love knows no bounds!
- A *Hustle* may adopt any baby abelisaurs of both species they come across;
- There exists evidence that on rare occasions this union may produce viable offspring: it is now believed that horned species of *Pycnonemosaurus* carry a significant *Carnotaurus* admixture as a result of hybridisation;
- A *Hustle* of 2 individuals may hunt prey of any size;
- A polycule *Hustle* of 3-4 individuals may only hunt prey of *giant* size and above together: for smaller prey only a pair may engage.

## ☂️ Survival tips
- Keep running! Your momentum makes your attacks stronger. Always stay light on your feet;
- You are not very agile and lack rear defences! Beware butt-biters;
- Manage your stamina: stamina is life for *Carnotaurus*;
- *Carnotaurus* swims like a rock;
- Common nickname: Carno;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
