# Yangchuanosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *Y. shangyouensis*;

## 🔍 Related genera
- *Sinraptor hepingensis*;

🐻‍❄️ **Size category**: Huge<br/>
🕑 **Passive growth time**: 3 hours 30 minutes<br/>
💟 **Total group limit**: 3 mature individuals<br/>
🔺 Minor group: **1**<br/>
🔹 Family group: **2**<br/>
🔶 Congregation: **3**

## 🍽️ Diet
- **Groundfeeder**: insects, roots, saltwater, meat, rotten flesh;
- **Dualist**: fruit, nuts, meat, rotten flesh;
- **Strong Stomach**: bones, meat, rotten and poisonous flesh.


## 🇨🇳 In real life
*Yangchuanosaurus* is a huge meat-eating theropod known from Late Jurassic China. It was the largest predator of Shaximiao Formation, coexisting with a large cast of sauropods and stegosaurs.
*Yangchuanosaurus* belongs to the obscure *Metriacanthosauridae* family that inhabited Jurassic Asia, with only *Metriacanthosaurus* itself known from Europe.
The skull of the animal was proportionally huge and deep, almost square in shape, which would give *Yangchuanosaurus* a rather charismatic appearance in life compared to many other theropods.

## In the Garden:
*Yangchuanosaurus* is a box-headed brute and a savage gourmand. Its massive skull bears jaws capable of effortlessly shearing through flesh, leaving deep bleeding wounds. A sturdy and bulky body combined with a stubborn spirit make this living dragon a dangerous opponent..
However, *Yangchuanosaurus* is not only a capable hunter, but a master forager as well. In times when live prey is scarce, it adapts, using its jaws to chew through the toughest of foods. It can be found ravaging termite mounds, cracking nuts and partaking in poisonous amphibians… Sometimes even doing so when prey is available, just to savor the taste.
Its distinguished palate shines the most as a tidal scavenger: *Yangchuanosaurus* delights in the taste of rotting leviathans stranded upon the coast.  
This Metriacanthosaurid is truly a master of survival, even if the price of survival is sometimes disgusting.


## ⛰️ Habitat preference
- **Coastal**: *Yangchuanosaurus* can adapt to drink highly saline water and feed on beached sharks. Some individuals live their whole lives on the coast as disgusting tidal scavengers;
- **Scrubland**: semi-arid sparsely vegetated scrublands are the perfect stage for *Yangchuanosaurus* to roam and hunt;
- **Woodland**: some populations prefer more lush and covered habitats, stalking and ambushing from the shadows.

## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- In coastal areas spend a lot of time beachcombing, searching for washed up carcasses, sniffing for the decomposing aroma;
- Have an incredibly adaptable palate for a carnivorous theropod, being able to survive off food sources of questionable quality, and even enjoy disgusting, strange new delicacies;
- Enjoy discovering the taste of each new prey species;
- Thanks to specialised kidneys, *Yangchuanosaurus* can gradually develop incredible tolerance to drinking saltwater, which allows some of them to live on the coast for longer periods of time;
- Can quickly bounce off from the very brink of death thanks to the same immune system that helps them digest carrion. This powerful immune response is shared among all Metriacanthosaurids;
- Quite tolerant to others of their own species and rarely fight for territory. There’s enough garbage around for everyone to eat!
- *Yangchuanosaurus* display to potential allies and partners with dance and gifts of exotic food;
- *Yangchuanosaurus* do not form long-term family bonds, but a temporary pairing during nesting season is very common;
- *Yangchuanosaurus* and *Sinraptor* have smaller “dwarf” relatives in the Garden.


## ♥️ Grouping
- A group can consist of 1-3 mature individuals;
- **Minor group**: 1 individual is called a loner;
- **Family group**: 2 mature individuals are called a pair;
- **Congregation**: 3 mature individuals are called a Dragon Dance;
- A group of *Yangchuanosauruses* is considered a *giant* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can freely group with *Rhamphorhynchus*, who often follow *Yangchuanosaurus* along the coastlines;
- A lone *Yangchuanosaurus* can group with 1-2 *Metriacanthosauruses* (see deviant profile).

## 🪺 Reproduction
- Nest materials: roots, mud, branches;
- Prefer to nest in well-vegetated spots with high water availability nearby;
- Young *Yangchuanosauruses* are known as munchers;
- *Yangchuanosauruses* are good parents;
- Supply their young with a rich and diverse diet;
- Parents often recognise their offspring even years after separation;
- Some young *Yancguanosaurus* have been observed returning to their parent’s territory;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.


## 🍖 Hunting
- **Minor group**: a lone (1) *Yangchuanosaurus* may hunt animals of any size;
- **Family group**: a pair (2) may only go for targets of *huge* size and above together. For hunting smaller prey, only one of a pair may engage;
- **Congregation**: a Dragon Dance (3) can only hunt *colossal* targets together, for lesser prey the group must send a smaller hunting party;
- For hunting rules when grouped with *Metriacanthosaurus*, see *The Emperor* deviant profile;
- A group consisting of only immature individuals count as a **Minor group** for hunting restrictions;
- **Non-cannibalistic**: flesh of their own kind is one dish they would rather avoid to be safe from disease.

## 🦴 Carcass interactions
- Can challenge any creature for a claimed carcass;
- Do not feed from fellow *Yangchuanosaurus* bodies;
- Immature individuals count as **scavengers**.


## 🚩 Claiming a territory
- *Yangchuanosaurus* can choose to claim any land POI as its *Cornucopia* by constructing a nest;
- While patrolling its *Cornucopia*, may challenge any land carnivores within hunting limits to leave the territory;
- If the nest of a *Yangchuanosaurus* is fully destroyed, the territory is considered lost, and cannot be reclaimed for at least another 15 minutes;
- A Dragon Dance (3) of *Yangchuanosauruses* is **hyper-territorial**: they don’t need a nest to lay claim to a *Cornucopia* and **must** challenge any other carnivores of  *huge* size and above out of their area.


## 👹 Deviant profile *The Emperor*
- Metriacanthosaurids are unique among many theropods in their relative tolerance for one-another. None of them are known to participate in cannibalism, and they often share territories. Conflicts are often resolved bloodlessly, by intimidation and bluff;
- Sometimes, when a lone old *Yangchuanosaurus* moves into a territory that is already occupied by smaller Metriacanthosaurids, the lesser predators can react with surprising amity to the presence of the brute;
- Slowly they come out of hiding, starting to cautiously share the same watering holes and carcasses, habituating the larger meat-eater to their presence;
- In rare cases, this mere neighbourship can evolve into true mutualism, where they rest, hunt and feed together;
- In such exceptional cases, the large *Yangchuanosaurus* is known as the *Emperor*, and its lesser relative as the subjects.

## 🔻 Deviant rules
- An *Emperor* can group with 1-2 *Metriacanthosauruses*;
- There can be only 1 *Emperor* in the group;
- *Emperor* with 1 subject can hunt prey of *huge* size and above together;
- *Emperor* with 2 subjects can hunt prey of *giant* size and above together;
- This grouping may include *Rhamphorhynchus*.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Your iron stomach can digest almost any food: starvation is a non-issue for *Yangchuanosaurus*;
- “*Groundfeeder*” metabolism allows *Yangchuanosaurus* to survive drinking saltwater;
- You can build your *Yangchuanosaurus* as a bleeder, focusing on damage over time and outlasting your enemy;
- Alternatively, you can become a tanky brawler with incredible fortitude to shove around your opponents;
- *Metriacanthosaurus* counts as an Allosauroid for the “*Emperor*” ability;
- Common nickname: yang;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

## 🦎 Roleplaying inspirations
- Repulsive charisma of a moray eel;
- Adaptability and impudent confidence of an American black bear;
- Relaxed, yet menacing disposition of a voracious tegu lizard or a blue-tongue skink;
- An amorous fondness for a varied and disgusting diet characteristic of bears and large lizards.
