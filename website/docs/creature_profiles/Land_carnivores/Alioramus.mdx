# Alioramus

## Known species
- *A. altai*: +3% attack damage;
- *A. eritquearcus*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *A. remotus*: +25% jump height;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: fresh meat;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, mud, roots;

## In real life
*Alioramus* was an unusually gracile and slender tyrannosaurid from the Late Cretaceous Mongolia. Unlike its robust and more famous relatives, it was a long-legged cursorial animal that employed a much different hunting strategy than the much larger *Tabrosaurus* it coexisted with. It was described from the famous Nemegt formation that was also home to *Barsboldia*, *Deinocheirus* and *Therizinosaurus*.

## In the Garden
*Alioramus* is a fleet-footed runner that hunts in the prairies and badlands of the island. Being outclassed by many other carnivores in sheer might, it relies on unparalleled speed and intelligence innate to all tyrannosaurs. Alone, they are excellent at chasing down small animals. A whole pack, however, can exhaust and wear down even serious prey…

## Roleplaying guidelines
- Their long legs make them perfectly adapted to roam open habitats like grasslands and deserts;
- Prefer to nest in arid and semi-arid habitats;
- Are strict carnivores that aren’t adapted for eating carrion and always hunt live prey;
- Lone individuals focus on taking critters and small fast-running prey;
- Packs often represent tight family groups that hunt larger prey with hight degree of coordination and tend to each-other;
- Temporary packs can also form from independent young individuals banding together;
- Hatchlings and juveniles are highly curious and like to play with unfamiliar objects.


## Friendly interactions
- Can have up to 4 mature individuals in a group, called a band;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group;
- Can substitute 2 *Alioramus* in a band with 1 deviant *outcast Nanuqsaurus*;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus* and enjoy letting them clean their feathery manes from pesky parasites;
- Can substitute 2 of their group members with 1 *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*;

## Conflicts
- Lone *Alioramus* can hunt animals of up to large size;
-  A band can hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- While solitary *Alioramus* are very skittish and wary around larger carnivores, large bands are known to aggress and bully other predators to ensure safety for their young.

## Other interactions
- Can attempt to steal meat chunks from kills made by other carnivores at their own risk.

## Deviant profile *The royal Marquis*
- Some *Alioramus* are known to closely associate with a lone mature *Tyrannosaurus* to benefit from the kills made by their tyrannical cousin;
- This is a very unstable alliance, and these *Alioramus* tend to keep their distance from the giant carnivore;
- The *Marquis* may directly assist its host in hunts, but they are *barely* tolerated by the tyrant;
- The *Marquis* can only eat from the kill after the *Tyrannosaurus* fills up, and if they attempt to eat out of line, their patron can snap at them with lethal intent;
- A *Tyrannosaurus* can only have up to 2 *Marquis* in a group.

## Survival tips
- Your swift legs let you escape almost any danger;
- Common nickname: alio;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

## Roleplaying inspirations
