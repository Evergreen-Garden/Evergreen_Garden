# Ceratosaurus

## Known species
- *C. dentisulcatus*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *C. nasicornis*: +10% venom and bleed healing;
- *C. magnicornis*: +5% armor;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, noxious amphibians, animals remains;<br/>
**Supplementary food**: fish;<br/>
**Nest Materials**: bones, rock, mud;

## In real life
*Ceratosaurus* was a stocky medium-sized ceratosaurian theropod from the famous Late Jurassic North American Morrison Formation. It is notable for having a very robust skull for its size, adorned with three horns that were likely used for visual display. It coexisted with larger predators like *Allosaurus* and *Torvosaurus* and herbivores like *Camptosaurus*, *Stegosaurus* and *Apatosaurus*. Some call *Ceratosaurus* “the underdog of the Morrison”.

## In the Garden
On the isle of Gondwa, *Ceratosaurus* is a tough, rough and mean opportunist. Covered in a chainmail of bony ossicles, it is an extremely hardy animal that can  leap into a fight with reckless abandon. This berserker of a predator is stubborn, relentless and ferocious, as well as a master of survival. They often bully other carnivores off their kills to avoid the hard work of hunting.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Prefer to dig nests in obscured habitats like rock fields, scrublands and dry woodlands;
- When hunting, often engages in violent close-quarters brawling with its prey;
- Is a capable and independent solitary hunter, but can form fission-fusion groups with closely living individuals. Such groups can fall apart and reform  seasonally;
- Very effective at processing skin, bones and organs and can stomach even highly decomposed flesh;
- Has a very potent sense of smell, allowing it to easily find corpses to scavenge or steal;
- Cackle when in a state of excitement, like upon finding a corpse, or when communicating with conspecifics. Other *Ceratosaurus* are quick to pick up this infectious laughter until the whole clan is snickering;
- Chuff, roar and hiss a lot in a confrontation to intimidate the opponent;
- When consuming a kill, adults and high-ranking individuals often eat first. *Ceratosaurus* groups are highly competitive. A corpse is often a place where duels for dominance happen. 

## Friendly interactions
- Can have up to 4 mature individuals in a group called a clan;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a clan;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- Can substitute 1 of their group members with a *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*.

## Conflicts
- A lone *Ceratosaurus* can hunt up to large-sized prey;
- A clan has no prey size limitations;
- As a scavenging specialist, *Ceratosaurus* can challenge any carnivore for a carcass;
- A clan can specifically challenge *Allosaurus* and *Torvosaurus* for territory, driving them from the area;
- Can engage in ritualistic duels when in possession of a large corpse. This is a way to show strength and rise through hierarchy;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated immature individuals as food and have no qualms about eating from any dead *Ceratosaurus* bodies;
- **Corpse bully**: can ignore 2-minute grace timer when challenging other animals for carcasses.


## Other interactions
- Often follow around ongoing hunts with the intention to steal the kill afterwards;
- Never tolerate scavengers or thieves at their kills;
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner isn’t actively eating.

## Survival tips
- *Ceratosaurus* possesses many abilities that let it locate and fight for player corpses, making it a perfect scavenger;
- Your turning is very slow, but you can use abilities like “*swerve*” and “*juke*” to outmaneuver foes;
- You can build your abilities in a variety of ways, prioritising damage, health or utility;
- *Ceratosaurus* is very tanky for its size, so don’t be afraid to fight head on!
- Common nicknames: cerato, cera;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
