## 🇲🇦 In real life
*Charcharodontosauridae*, or the “shark-lizards” are a family of carnivorous allosauroid dinosaurs that inhabited our Planet during the first half of the Cretaceous. 
Arguably one of the most successful families of gigantic predators to ever exist, Charcharodontosaurs started  from humble beginnings and eventually produced forms that would become the apex predators of their respective ecosystems all across the globe. They are known from Europe, North America, South America and Africa. On much of the planet during the Early Cretaceous you would encounter Charcharodontosaurs being on top of the food pyramid. 
Only after their disappearance, tens of millions of years later, would Life produce terrestrial carnivores as big as these when Tyrannosaurs evolved.
The North-African *Charcharodontosaurus*, namesake of this whole family and the type genus, lived roughly during the middle of the Cretaceous in the Kem Kem beds of what is now Morocco. It was the largest land predator known from this location, but was rivalled by the semi-aquatic *Spinosaurus* it shared its habitat with. *Charcharodontosaurus* likely preyed upon local sauropods and ornithopods. The environment it lived in was a vast riparian wetland similar to marshlands and mangroves.
Meanwhile, on the other side of the globe its *Gigantosaurini* relatives such as *Meraxes*, *Mapusaurus*, *Tyrannotitan*, *Taurovenator* and *Giganotosaurus* wreaked havoc upon South America.

## In the Garden
The smell of decay attracts a great many creatures. Flies and beetles show up first. Then the scavengers of wing and foot find their way to the rotting bounty. But there is one that stands above all of the corpse eaters. Like a shark, it picks up the faintest smell of spilled blood. It comes from the steaming mist of the marshlands to dig into the flesh, viscera and sinew. *Carcharodontosaurus* arrives to clean up the mess, and its grim duty is not a pretty sight.


## ⛰️ Habitat preference
- **Wetland**: *Charcharodontosaurus* favors marshlands, riverbanks and lake shores where animals gather or get stuck crossing the water;
- **Woodland**: often use forested areas to set up ambushes or retreat for shelter;
- **Grassland**: often venture out to patrol open prairie where there’s nowhere to hide and any carcasses are visible from a great distance.

## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- Shockingly, many of the Carcharodontosaurs of the Garden appear to be able to produce hybrid offspring. The hybridisation mostly occurs within the *Giganotosaurini* subfamily, and researchers have observed individuals with mixed characteristics from different South American Charcharodontosaurs. Due to the rarity of such extraordinary hybrids, it is currently unknown if these individuals themselves are fertile or sterile;
- *Charcharodontosaurus* can often be seen sniffing the air, trying to pick up a scent of blood. These dinosaurs possess a great sense of smell and can locate fallen animals with pinpoint precision, often being quick to arrive at any fresh kill to steal the spoils for themselves;
- Very competitive and have an aggressive temperament even for a Charcharodontosaur;
- *Charcharodontosaurus* often doesn’t wait for it’s prey to be dead to start eating: the shark-toothed brute simply starts devouring its victim alive while the struggle is still ongoing, until the hunted animal either escapes or dies trying;
- Have bizarre mating habits, where courtship may start as a violent exchange of mock bites and roars only to suddenly transform into a gentle dance;
- Tend to be more social than their cousins, and several individuals may share a territory and even opportunistically band together to mob big prey;
- Enjoy gnawing on bones.


## ♥️ Grouping
- A group of 2 *Carcharodontosaurus* is called a pair;
- A group of 3 *Carcharodontosaurus* is called a *tremble*;
- A group of *Carcharodontosaurus* is considered a *giant* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed-species grouping
- Can freely group with *Rhamphorhynchus*, who also have a taste for scavenging.

## 🪺 Reproduction
- **Nest materials**: bones, rock, mud
- Prefer to nest near lakes and rivers;
- Nesting adults often protect their nests with twisted roots and plant debris;
- Young *Carcharodontosaurus* are known as pups;
- *Carcharodontosaurus* ferociously protect their nests, but quickly lose their parental instincts as their offspring grows out of their hatchling coloration. “Teenage” *Carcharodontosaurus* often get chased off by their own parents who now see them as competition;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.


## 🍖 Hunting
- A lone *Carcharodontosaurus* may hunt animals of any size;
- A pair (2) may hunt targets of *huge* size and above together. For smaller prey only one individual may engage;
- A tremble (3) may only hunt *colossal* targets together. For smaller prey one or two individuals must stand back;
- **Infanticidal**: can hunt immature *Carcharodontosaurus*;
- **Cannibalistic**: can hunt mature *Carcharodontosaurus* if below 50% hunger;
- *Carcharodontosaurus* can satiate its hunger by merely flesh grazing on live targets and may retreat from the conflict after getting its fill from the dreadful harvest.

## 🦴 Carcass interactions
- Can challenge any creature within hunting limits for a claimed carcass;
- **Corpse Pirate**: can ignore the 2-minute grace period for contesting carcasses;
- **Scavengers Reign**: a *tremble* (3) of *Carcharodontosaurus* may choose to **claim** a colossal carcass, called a *whalefall*. Under normal circumstances *whalefalls* serve as communal feeding sites where fighting is forbidden. A *tremble* of Charcharodontosaurs is an exception, as they may set up their base on top of a *whalefall* and selectively restrict feeding access, gatekeeping and challenging any carnivores of their choosing. If the tremble loses a challenge, they must leave the *whalefall* forever. When a tremble shows up, peace is extinguished. Dogs rule this world now, and only dogs shall remain here;
- Can freely feed from *Carcharodontosaurus* carcasses.


## 🚩 Claiming a territory
- *Carcharodontosaurus* can choose to claim a land POI as its *Deshret* (“*Red Land*”) by constructing a nest;
- While patrolling the *Deshret*, *Carcharodontosaurus* may challenge any land carnivores within hunting limits to leave its territory;
- Additionally, it can challenge *semi-aquatic* carnivores to retreat from land, but does not harass them once they go into the water;
- If the nest is fully destroyed, the territory is considered lost, and cannot be reclaimed for at least another 15 minutes;
- Most often exhibit territorial aggression towards other Charcharodontosaurids and Spinosaurids: *Spinosaurus* may be the rightful ruler of the Black Lands of *Kemet* where great water meets the soil, but the dry Red Lands of *Deshret* belong to the corpse eaters.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Can you smell that sweet scent of blood upon the wind? *Carcharadontosaurus* has the ability to locate any fresh carcasses near itself;
- *Carcharodontosaurus* can gain food from flesh grazing upon other animals. You can bite off a whole chunk out of a passing sauropod and run away;
- *Carcharodontosaurus* is equipped to hunt big prey. Smaller creatures, however, can become a problem for it!
- You can specialise in corpse contesting, flesh grazing or pure bleed;
- Common nicknames: carch, carcha, carchar;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
