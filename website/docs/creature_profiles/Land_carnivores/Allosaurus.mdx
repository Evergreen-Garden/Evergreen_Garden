# Allosaurus

## Known species
- *A. fragilis*: +10% health recovery;
- *A. europaeus*: +10% venom and bleed healing;
- *A. jimmadseni*: +3% attack damage;
- *A. regalis*: modded variant (*ExA*);

## Similar genera
- *Megalosaurus bucklandii*

**Size category**: large;<br/>
**Diet** (*A. sp.*): meat, animal remains, fish and noxious amphibians;<br/>
**Diet** (*A. regalis*): meat and bone, as well as insects, roots and fruit;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, branches, roots.

## In real life
*Allosaurus* was a large theropod dinosaur from North America and Europe of the Late Jurassic. In the North American Morrison formation it is the most commonly found carnivorous dinosaur. It possessed both a large skull with serrated teeth and powerful arms with heavy hook-like claws. The lacrimal bones of *Allosaurus* were shaped into distinct crests above its brows. Bite marks and shed teeth from *Allosaurus* are associated with almost every other inhabitant of the Morrison formation, including other *Allosaurus*.
Some call *Allosaurus* “the lion of the Jurassic”.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Allosaurus* is a prideful predator with a regal disposition. While not the largest carnivore around, *Allosaurus* makes up for it with a nasty character, bullying predator and prey alike. Two lacrimal crests serve as its crown, and tooth and claw are its orb and sceptre. Commonly found alone or in pairs, they sometimes congregate into terrifying mobs to bring down big game.
Most species of *Allosaurus* are robust ambush predators that stalk the edges of the forest, but there exists a highly unusual species known as *A. regalis*, which differs significantly in ecology, being a lot more gracile, lean and quick, pursuing prey in the open fields and having adaptable omnivorous habits.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Most *Allosaurus* species prefer to nest in forests and vegetated areas;
- *A. regalis* prefers to establish nests in grassland habitats;
- All species nesting habitat overlaps in scrublands;
- Have a very bold character and a willingness to fight tooth and claw for resources;
- *Allosaurus* is the most gregarious member of the large carnivore guild, forming flexible social groups that don’t hunt together, but may share a living space, meals and raise young collectively;
- Such groups, known as *prides*, often have a steep hierarchy and a pecking order, being alliances of convenience rather than families;
- Prides often use a claimed corpse as a place to rest and socialise together, and if the hunting party made the kill far away from the rest of the pride, they gorge themselves and take chunks of meat back home to feed the rest;
- Lone *Allosaurus* or pairs that don’t belong to a pride are called *nomads*;
- Present themselves to a mate with a courting dance and outstretched arms;
- The smaller *A. regalis*, while being bullied by other *Allosaurs*, has an incredibly flexible dietary ecology, and in times of drought is known to consume roots and fruit to extract moisture and dig at termite nests for nutrition. This helps the lesser species of *Allosaurus* to survive and endure even the most dire scarcity when its larger cousins perish or migrate;
- All other species of *Allosaurs* view *A. regalis* as competition and try to eradicate them whenever possible.


## Grouping
- All other *Allosaurus* species do not tolerate *A. regalis* and cannot group with them;
- A group of 2 mature individuals is called a pair;
- A group of 3 mature individuals is called a triumvirate;
- A group of 4 mature individuals is called a pride;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fits in a group;
- A group of *Allosaurus* counts as a *huge* target;

## Mixed grouping
- 1 or 2 *Allosaurs* of any species may add (or even adopt) a single *Saurophaganax* into their group where it counts as another *Allosaurus* for hunting limitations;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*, enjoying the service of a tiny majordomo.

## Conflicts
- A solitary *Allosaurus* or a pair may hunt animals of any size;
- A triumvirate may hunt only *giant* and *colossal* animals together, for smaller prey only a pair may engage;
- A pride can hunt only *colossal* targets together;
- Any group of *Allosaurs* may challenge or defend themselves together;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated immature individuals as food and have no qualms about scavenging any dead *Allosaurus* flesh;
- *A. sp.* and *A. regalis* may hunt one-another freely.

## Other interactions
- Established prides tend to defend their territory from both nomads and other prides, chasing them away.


## Deviant profile *The Scarred One* and the *Foul Pride*;
- Some *A. regalis* specimens become host to a horrible parasitic amoeba, called *Abhorrens allosauroides*;
- This microorganism kills the host in 90% of the cases, but few who survive become twisted and misshapen for life…;
- Such rare individuals, known as *The Scarred Ones* are most often shunned and exiled by their pride. But travelling alone in the wasteland, they sometimes find themselves in a more sinister Jurassic alliance…
- **Mandatory subspecies**: *A. regalis abhorrens*: -10% damage, speed and defence;
- May group with up to 3 *Ceratosaurus*;
- *The Foul Pride* may only hunt other *Allosaurs*, *Saurophaganax* and giant carnivores, as their goal is to eradicate competition;
- May challenge and defend together;
- Can challenge any carnivore for a corpse, and oftentimes choose to steal their meal instead of hunting;
- Absolutely **hate** other *Allosaurs* and *Saurophaganax*;
- Despite the *Foul Pride* having an ominous aura, they often develop strong care for each-other and act as one kind supportive ugly family.

## Survival tips
- Beware heights: *Allosaurus* really is a fragile lizard;
- Utilise bleed to do a lot of the hard work for you while hunting;
- Common nickname: allo;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

- We in the Garden do not condone stereotyping of any IRL group, nor do we encourage jokes about inbreeding;
