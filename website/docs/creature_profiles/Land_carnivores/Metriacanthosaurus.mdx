# Metriacanthosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *M. parkeri*;

## 🔍 Related genera

- *Sinraptor latronemus*;

🐯 **Size category**: large<br/>

## 🍽️ Diet
- **Scavenger**: meat, rotten and poisonous flesh, mushrooms.


## 🇬🇧In real life
*Metriacanthosaurus* is a carnivorous theropod known from the Oxford Clay Formation of the Late Jurassic of what is now England. This fossil formation is primarily known for its diverse marine organisms, and *Metriacanthosaurus* is one of the few land animals known from this island environment.
This dinosaur is the namesake of the *Metriacanthosauridae* family, a mysterious lineage of Jurassic theropods that are mainly known from Asia: *Metriacanthosaurus* is the only genus of the family known from outside of Asia. Other genera in this family include the box-skulled *Yangchuanosaurus* and the more narrow-snouted *Sinraptor*, both of which were described from China.
A curious trait of *Metriacantjosaurus* were the tall neural spines of its vertebrae that formed a ridge running along the back of the animal.


## In the Garden
Poison. A potent weapon of nature. Yet, so hard to obtain. Only a few vertebrate families have the ability to synthesise toxic proteins - and it is always metabolically taxing. But many animals, including poison dart frogs, pufferfish and even some birds have found a way around this limitation…
*Metriacanthosaurus* lurks in shadows searching for a horrible meal. The delicacy in question - a deadly fungus. Most animals would avoid such a hazardous source of nutrition, but not *Metriacanthosaurus*. It is, in fact, immune to the deadly toxin. Not only that: the dinosaur can weaponise it.
*Metriacanthosaurus* cannot secrete poison on its own. Instead, it searches for specific natural sources of noxious substances: poisonous fungi, the flesh of *Platyhystryx* amphibians and rotten carrion. By sequestering these dangerous compounds, *Metriacanthosaurus* enables itself with a deadly weapon that would help it to both fend off danger and give it an edge when hunting. By grooming itself, *Metriacanthosaurus* coats its skin, claws and teeth in toxic secretions.
However, deprived of its dreadful meals, *Metriacanthosaurus* becomes almost entirely disarmed. Without access to poisonous food, it is just another ordinary theropod.


## ⛰️ Habitat preference
- **Coastal**: often patrol the shorelines in search of rotten carcasses washed up on the beach: not many creatures are able to take advantage of such a repulsive food source…
- **Woodland**: *Metriacanthosaurus* often stalk dense shadowy woodlands and forage upon the diverse banquet of forest mushrooms;
- **Scrubland**: sparse scrublands often tend to have clusters of highly toxic mushrooms, the favourite food of *Metriacanthosaurus*.

## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- Generally shy and secretive during the day, but become vocal during the dark hours, shrieking at the Moon;
- Feel most comfortable hunting and exploring at night;
- Like most *Metriacanthosaurids*, it has an incredible immune system that lets it miraculously heal from the most grievous wounds;
- Seek out decomposing carrion and beached sharks;
- Find great pleasure in eating the poisonous *Platyhystix*, and prefer to hunt them over any other critter;
- Fungi are the favourite food of *Metriacanthosaurus*: the theropod can effectively extract the toxic compounds from consumed mushrooms and use them to create its own poison;
- This toxic oil has more applications than violence: in daily life its main function is deterring parasites;
- Ambush and disorient their prey at night with deafening screeches and delirium-inducing toxins;
- *Metriacanthosaurus* tend to be great at surviving alone, but individuals inhabiting the same territory can sometimes gather into mobs and cohabitate;
- Often choose caves as their day shelter;
- When several individuals inhabit the same living space, become rather chatty;
- Engage in courtship and social behaviour by gifting mushrooms and displaying their throat sacks;
- *Yangchuanosaurus circumspectus* and *Sinraptor latronemus* both have larger cousins inhabiting the Garden…


## ♥️ Grouping
- A group can consist of 1-4 mature individuals;
- **Minor group**: 1-2 individuals are called a pair;
- **Family group**: 3 mature individuals are called a blight;
- **Congregation**: 4 mature individuals are called a scourge;
- A group of *Metriacanthosauruses* is considered a *huge* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can freely group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- A lone *Metriacanthosaurus* can join a group of medium-sized land carnivores (see deviant profile for the list of host species);
- 1-2 *Metriacanthosauruses* can group with a lone deviant *Yangchuanosaurus*.

## 🪺 Reproduction
- **Nest materials**: reeds, roots, branches;
- Almost always dig their nests around large mushroom clusters;
- The habitation of *Metriacanthosaurus* further fertilises the soil, so eventually mushrooms can spread around the nest in a rich fungal carpet;
- Building up toxic reserves is very important for young individuals to deter potential predators, and parents tirelessly seek out mushrooms, carrion and poisonous critters to feed their offspring;
- Young *Metriacanthosauruses* are known as goblets;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.


## 🍖 Hunting
- **Minor group**: a pair (1-2) may hunt animals of up to *giant* size;
- **Family group**: a blight (3) may only go for targets of *giant* size and above together. For hunting smaller prey, one group member should step back;
- **Congregation**: a scourge (4) can only hunt *colossal* targets together, for lesser prey the group must send a smaller hunting party;
- For hunting rules when grouped with other carnivores, see *Dubious Friend* deviant profile;
- A group consisting of only immature individuals counts as a **Minor group** for hunting restrictions;
- Never hunt other *Metriacanthosauruses*.

## 🦴 Carcass interactions
- Can challenge any carnivore within hunting limits for a claimed carcass;
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner isn’t actively eating;
- Do not feed from *Metriacanthosaurus* carcasses, as they would rather avoid the taste of their own kind’s toxins.


## 🚩 Claiming a territory
- *Metriacanthosaurus* can choose to claim any land POI as its *Mushroom Ring* by constructing a nest;
- While patrolling its *Mushroom Ring*, may challenge any land carnivores within hunting limits to leave the territory;
- Additionally, by claiming a territory, *Metriacanthosaurus* claims all mushroom patches within it and can challenge *anything* that tries to collect or consume its mushrooms. If the perpetrator continues to steal the mushrooms despite the warning, *Metriacanthosaurus* can retaliate and kill the intruder;
- If the nest of a *Metriacanthosaurus* is fully destroyed, the territory is considered lost, and cannot be reclaimed for at least another 15 minutes;
- Often signal their territory by screaming into the night.


## 👹 Deviant profile *Dubious Friend*
- In some cases lonely *Metriacanthosauruses* have been observed following around packs of medium land carnivores and slowly habituating them to its presence;
- It mimics their calls and serves as a scout and sentinel at first, associating closer and closer with them until they allow the deviant *Metriacanthosaurus* to stick around for full time;
- Such infiltrators are known as *Dubious Friends*, and serve as a rare example of competitive carnivore species working together;
- Each group reacts to the presence of a *Metriacanthosaurus* in their ranks differently: some prefer to keep a healthy distance and distrust, allowing it to partake in the meal only after everyone else is finished, and others fully recognising the usefulness of their new ally, even resting together;
- Toxic oils of *Metriacanthosaurus* are speculated to be beneficial to other carnivores, allowing their skin to more easily repel parasites and bacteria, thus healing wounds quicker;
- Some theorise that the unique ability of the deviant *Metriacanthosaurus* to gain trust may be linked to some of the odorous chemicals it produces.


## 🔻 Deviant rules
- A *Dubious Friend* can group with several species of *medium* land carnivores and can fully help the other species in every way, almost completely integrating into the group structure;
- There can only be one *Dubious Friend* in a group;
- The *Dubious Friend* can have 1-4 carnivore companions in a group, which should all belong to the same species;
- Additionally, a *Dubious Friendship* can include *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- A *Dubious Friend* with 1 companion can hunt targets of up to *giant* size;
- A *Dubious Friend* with 2 companions can hunt targets of *huge* size and above;
- A *Dubious Friend* with 3-4 companions can hunt targets of *giant* size and above;
- A *Dubious Friend* cannot nest in any offspring while grouped with its companions;
- A *Dubious Friend* group counts as a *large* target;
- **The full list of Dubious Friend host species includes**:
  - *Achillobator*;
  - *Alioramus*;
  - *Ceratosaurus*;
  - *Concavenator*;
  - *Dilophosaurus*;
  - *Megalania*;
  - *Nanuqsaurus*;
  -  *Utahraptor*.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Stacks of “*Noxious*” empower the abilities of your *Metriacanthosaurus* and give it access to toxin and poison. You can accumulate this effect by seeking out and consuming carrion, noxious meat from *Platyhystrix* and mushrooms;
- Mushrooms are the most efficient source of “*Noxious*” effect;
- Toxin reduces maximum health pool of the afflicted target’s by up to 20%;
- Poison reduces the afflicted target’s health regeneration by 25% and adds a thin purple filter to the screen;
- *Metriacanthosaurus* has an advantage during nighttime;
- The attacks of *Metriacanthosaurus* deal comparatively little damage, but it possesses a lot of utility and support abilities;
- *Metriacanthosaurus* has access to 2 types of voice abilities: healing calls to sustain and restore, and debuff calls to weaken and enfeeble;
- You can always carry around an extra mushroom in your mouth for an emergency “*Noxious*” effect (for example, to use the “*Noxious Frenzy*” ability);
- Common nickname: metri;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

## 🍄 Roleplaying inspirations
- Toxic birds like the spur-winged goose;
- The slow loris that produces poison and rubs it in its fur as a defence against predators;
- Striped and brown hyenas;
- The mythology of a repugnant cockatrice;
- Deviant behavior is based on the partnership seen in some carnivorans, including those between coyotes and badger in North America and those between individual striped hyenas and packs of grey wolves in some parts of the Middle East.
