# Concavenator

## Known species
- *C. corcovatus*: +5% bleed damage;
- *C. spinatergus*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *C. carcharus*: +10% swim speed;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, fish, mollusks;<br/>
**Nest materials**: roots, mud, branches;

## In real life
*Concavenator* was a theropod dinosaur from the Early Cretaceous of Spain in a time when Europe was broken up into marine islands. It belonged to the family *Charcharodontosauridae* or the “shark-toothed dinosaurs”, and was a distant relative of giants like *Tyrannotitan*. The most distinctive feature of this dinosaur was a strange crest rising from its hips and formed by tall neural spines. Its exact purpose is unknown, but there is a possibility that it was a display structure.

## In the Garden
*Concavenator* is a swift hunchbacked predator that patrols the beaches and shorelines of Gondwa in search of prey. These smaller relatives of the island’s shark-toothed giants are very adaptable and aren’t afraid to get a little wet. They can swim, fish and forage for clams, scrounging anything they could find at the edge of the sea. They are still competent and vicious land hunters of larger prey, using their razor-sharp talons to slowly exsanguinate their victims.

## Roleplaying guidelines
- Prefer to nest in coastal areas and on islands;
- Often spend their time beach-combing, searching the shorelines and tidal flats for crabs and clams;
- Act more agitated and vocal during rain and storm, often running around, screaming and playing;
- Are curious and active animals, often watch other dinosaurs from a safe distance;
- Pack members establish friendliness by showing off their hip-sails.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have up to 4 mature individuals in a group, called a surf;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- Can substitute 2 of their group members with 1 *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*.


## Conflicts
- A lone *Concavenator* can hunt animals of up to large size;
- A surf can hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- Can contest up to large carnivores for carcasses;
- A surf can claim a specific coastal strip as their *Home Beach*. While the surf is at their *Home Beach*, they can contest any carnivores for carcasses or territory, compelling them to leave;

## Other interactions
- Can attempt to steal meat chunks from kills made by other carnivores at their own risk;
- Communal feeders: can tolerate unrelated *Concavenators* coming to share their kill;

## Survival tips
- *Concavenator* is a fast swimmer and can quickly cross water to escape danger;
- Being able to eat fish and mollusks means that you aren’t as pressured to hunt as other land carnivores;
- Common nicknames: conca, conc;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

## Roleplaying Inspirations
- Opportunistic carnivorans that use wetland and coastal habitats like the crab-eating mongoose, the crab-eating fox and the crab-eating raccoon.
