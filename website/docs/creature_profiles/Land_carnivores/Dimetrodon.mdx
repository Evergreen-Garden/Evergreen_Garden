# Dimetrodon

## Known species
- *D. limbatus*: +15% lacerate damage;
- *D. borealis*: +3% attack damage;
- *D. grandis*: +10% stamina recovery;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, fish, animal remains, fruit;<br/>
**Land nest materials**: reeds, branches, roots;<br/>
**Water nest materials**: kelp, rock, branches;

## In real life
*Dimetrodon* was an early relative of mammals that lived during the Early Permian across the continent of Pangea, with most species being known from what is now North America. Its most prominent feature is the large neural spine sail that likely served as a display structure, advertising fitness. The larger species of *Dimetrodon* were apex predators of their time. It possessed canine-like teeth that were one of the earliest examples of mammal-like dentition.
It is thought to have lived in a wetland habitat similar to modern Everglades and coexisted with *Diplocaulus*.

## In the Garden
*Dimetrodon* is an ancient relic from the crimson canyons of Red Island. It was surviving there in isolation among other stem-mammals until geological forces pushed the smaller island to the shores of Gondwa. Now, introduced into a world dominated by dinosaurs, this primal predator has become a bane of the reptile-kind. 
With an aggressive territorial demeanour and a mean artery-puncturing bite, it continues to fill its predatory niche in this new world as it has done in the ancient days of the harsh, bygone ages.
According to all known laws of evolution, there is no way a *Dimetrodon* should be able to survive in a world full of evolved predators. Its sail is way too large and bright for it to succesfully take down anything remotely fast or agile. The *Dimetrodon* of course, persists anyway.
Because *Dimetrodons* don't care what dinosaurs think is impossible.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Have their evolutionary origins on Red Island, and have a population there;
- Prefer to nest in or around small lakes or oasises, making it their territory;
- Often spend the day actively patrolling arid or semi-arid environments, but retreat to water at night for protection, feeling more lethargic;
- Some *Dimetrodon* claim geothermal pools at night to help them keep stable body temperature;
- Use their flashy sails to both court mates and intimidate rivals, the two animals often circling each-other to compare sails;
- Have a strict hierarchy in their groups, with the meanest, biggest individual often being the leader and the owner of the territory;
- Often bask in the light of the sunrise, welcoming the new day with loud territorial broadcasts;
- Have a seemingly supernatural internal clock that always tells them when the sun is about to go up, making them hiss in anticipation.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have a maximum of 3 mature individuals in a group called a fleet;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a fleet, called sloops;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- __Cannot__ group with deviant *Metriacanthosaurus*, as *Dimetrodon* are so extremely territorial. Also, being from Red Island, they have no idea what a *Metriacanthosaurus* is, and are very confused. If it has no sail, it is food.

## Conflicts
- A lone *Dimetrodon* can hunt or challenge up to large-sized animals;
- A fleet can hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- Can claim a small lake and are fiercely protective of their nesting territory;
- Can clash with other *Dimetrodons* to claim their territory;
- Full cannibals: can hunt other *Dimetrodons*. In the desert, nothing goes to waste.

## Other interactions
- Can establish peaceful coexistence with the smaller inhabitants of its claimed watering hole if the water quality isn’t too low;
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner isn’t actively eating.

## Survival tips
- You can switch your nest type between terrestrial and aquatic by switching your back limb ability!
- *Dimetrodon* is strongly diurnally oriented, so be cautious at night!
- With equipped “*dive*” you can escape into the water;
- Common nickname: dime;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
