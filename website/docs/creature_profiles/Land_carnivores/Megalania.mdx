# Megalania

## Known species
- *Varanus priscus*: +25 jump height;
- *Varanus “varius”*: +10% swim speed;
- *Varanus teiidae*: +5% venom damage;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, fish, noxious amphibians, animal remains;<br/>
**Nest materials**: rock, reeds, roots;

## In real life
Megalania was an enormous monitor lizard that inhabited Australia during the Pleistocene. It was closely related to modern monitor lizards, being a sister species to the Komodo dragon. It filled a niche of an apex predator and is the largest lizard known to science that wasn’t a mosasaur or a snake.
Megalania may have shortly coexisted with the first humans in Australia.

## In the Garden
On the isle of *Gondwa*, megalania is queen among lizards. Like all varanids, it’s fairly active and intelligent for a lizard. Its has a lot of instruments in its survival arsenal, including large teeth with iron-enriched serrations, a forked tongue that provides an excellent sense of smell, and of course, venomous saliva that weakens its prey. Megalania is an adaptable generalist and a gluttonous eater, consuming fish, critters, carrion and being especially prolific in hunting juvenile dinosaurs in the undergrowth. It may not be a dinosaur, but it is still a terrible lizard.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Megalania, being so close to the ground, is a natural ambush predator, surprising its prey from bushes or water;
- Prefer to nest in earth cavities and crevices and heavily camouflage their nests with debris;
- Sometimes are known to swim to smaller islands to find safe nesting spots;
- Is very adaptable, being comfortable in all biomes, but has an affinity for drier habitats during the day and wetter habitats during the night;
- Being an excellent swimmer, often combs both the seashore and riverbanks in search of fish and aquatic prey;
- Sunbathes on rocks in the morning or after a meal to help its metabolism;
- Normally solitary animals, sometimes they form loose mobs for cooperative hunting;
- When two individuals meet, often size each-other up by standing erect;
- Not very affectionate parents, but moreso than smaller monitors. They let young lizardlings stick around and can still protect them from danger, but there is little to no tenderness between the generations;
- When in a group with young, older individuals always eat first. In the lizard world might makes right.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have a maximum of 3 mature individuals in a group called a lounge;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a lounge;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- Can substitute 1 of their group members with a *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*. What a delightfully toxic relationship.

## Conflicts
- A lone megalania can hunt animals of up to large size;
- A lounge can hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated lizardlings as food and have no qualms about eating lizard flesh.

## Other interactions
- Juvenile megalania sometimes mimic *Paleosaniwa* to escape competition with older conspecifics.
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner isn’t actively eating.


## Survival tips
- Megalania has a lot of escape options when faced with danger: it’s an excellent swimmer and can fit into tight holes and dens;
- Venom reduces stamina regeneration in the afflicted target;
- Always be on the lookout for food, megalania has an insatiable hunger;
- Common nicknames: meg, lizard;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
