## 🇦🇷 In real life
*Giganotosaurus* was a giant meat-eating dinosaur from the lower Late Cretaceous of what is now Argentina. It belonged to the family *Charcharodontosauridae*, a lineage of globally successful giant theropods that were especially numerous in South America. *Giganotosaurus* was perhaps the most famous of them all. Its lower jaw bears a signature square “chin” that makes it unique among its relatives.
It is theorised that *Giganotosaurus* hunted small sauropods and was the top-order carnivore of the Candeleros formation.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Giganotosaurus* is a walking giant trash compactor of a predator. Extremely opportunistic, it has a very unselective palate and an iron stomach that can digest decomposing carcasses and overripe fruit. It is, however, not only a prolific scavenger, but a brutal predator that uses its weight and saw-like teeth to overpower prey in displays of raw barbarous butchery, brushing off pain or grievous injury. Its favourite prey items - enormous sauropods, which *Giganotosaurus* has specifically evolved to hunt.
In lean times, the giant predator goes into a sluggish lethargy, sustaining itself on long dried carrion and barely edible debris, but when colossal sauropods arrive, *Giganotosaurus* comes alive and becomes the one to bring down the titans, feeding entire ecosystems with their flesh.
Bearing a jagged, unrefined appearance and carrying a dreadful aura, as well as the constant stench of death, *Giganotosaurus* is a mean, grim king of the underworld.


## ⛰️ Habitat preference
- **Wetland**: *Giganotosaurus* frequents flooded and swampy environments rich with cover and prey;
- **Scrubland**: *Giganotosaurus* also feels comfortable in semi-arid sparse forests;
- **Desert**: *Giganotosaurus* can spend long periods without food and water and is able to tolerate very dry conditions, sometimes making home in desert canyons and around ephemeral oases.


## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- **Sexual dimorphism**: males often sport a ridge of gnarly spikes along their backs and hanging flabby dewlaps on their throats, while females more often lack those features. This is not a hard rule, but merely a statistic. The older males have especially large saggy dewlaps;
- Consequently, males often display their size, spikes and dewlaps to attract a mate. In truth, the females don’t care much about such features and choose mostly based on healthy looks. Some *Giganotosaurus* don’t even participate in heterosexual mating behaviours;
- Have an extremely generalistic diet and an iron stomach, being able to consume almost anything. In times when live prey in scarce, *Giganotosaurus* can survive off rotten corpses, old bones and even roots and tubers. Digest the indigestible. Where other giant carnivores leave or perish of famine, *Giganotosaurus* alone thrives on garbage;
- Habitually consume naturally fermented overripe fruit that develop an alcohol content before drifting off to drunken sleep;
- Sometimes visit coasts to scavenge beached sharks, and are generally attracted to all kinds of rotten stuff;
- Can slow their metabolism and go for long periods without food, but once meat is available, will consume monstrous amounts;
- Can appear somewhat sluggish, but make up for it with relentlessness and persistence;
- Have a generally calm attitude outside of territorial aggression;
- Have a really high pain tolerance: it is not uncommon to see a *Giganotosaurus* casually walk off horrifying injuries;
- Rival **Giganotosaurus** sometimes duel using head-bashing;
- *Giganotosaurus* don’t form long-term familial bonds, but it is common for 2 individuals to pair up temporarily and stay together for some time. Some older individuals can have several alternating partners from neighbouring territories.


## ♥️ Grouping
- A group can consist of 1-2 mature individuals;
- **Minor group**: 1 individual is called a loner;
- **Family group**: 2 mature individuals are called a pair.
- A pair of *Giganotosauris* is considered a *colossal* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can freely group with *Rhamphorhynchus* and enjoy their services;
- Can have up to 2 *Royal Adjutant Deinonychus* in a group.

## 🪺 Reproduction
- Nest materials: bone, rock, mud;
- Prefer to make their nests near a source of water and cover it with branches and vegetation;
- Young *Giganotosaurus* are known as giggles;
- Usually raise young alone;
- The parent is usually very protective of the nest and hatchlings, helping them out of their eggs and providing food. However, once they start to shed their juvenile colors, parental care ends; 
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.


## 🍖 Hunting
- **Minor group**: a lone (1) *Giganotosaurus* may hunt animals of any size;
- **Family group**: A pair (2) may only go for targets of *giant* size and above together. For hunting smaller prey, only one of a pair may engage;
- A group consisting of only immature individuals count as a **Minor group** for hunting restrictions;
- **Infanticidal**: can hunt immature *Giganotosaurus*;
- **Cannibalistic**: may hunt other mature *Giganotosaurus* when below 50% hunger.

## 🦴 Carcass interactions
- Can challenge any creature for a claimed carcass;
- Can freely feed from *Giganotosaurus* carcasses;
- Immature individuals count as **scavengers**.


## 🚩 Claiming a territory
- **Hyper-territorial**: automatically claim any area they reside in and **have** to contest any other giant land carnivores they come across, otherwise they must leave;
- Any area of residence of *Giganotosaurus* automatically becomes its *Dominion*;
- Have to contest any other giant land carnivores or leave the territory;
- Optionally, may view any *huge* land carnivores as competition as well and challenge them;
- After being exiled from territory, must wait a minimum of 15 minutes before returning;
-  When faced with fellow *Giganotosaurus*, usually try to bluff to avoid injury, but will fight if intimidation fails;
- May consume the rival *Giganotosaurus* that fell during a territory contest;
- Immature individuals don’t participate in territorial behaviour, and can be tolerated by adults.


### ☂️ Survival tips:
- Being able to eat all kinds of garbage and go without food for ages makes *Giganotosaurus* the ultimate survivalist among the giant carnivores;
- *Giganotosaurus* relies on drawn-out attrition when hunting, combining bleed and damage mitigation to slowly outlast its opponents;
- *Giganotosaurus* is especially effective at fighting sauropods, but only experienced players should attempt to bring down full adults when alone;
- *Giganotosaurus* strikes the middle ground in terms of mobility and speed among its peers;
- Common nicknames: giga;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️(challenging)
