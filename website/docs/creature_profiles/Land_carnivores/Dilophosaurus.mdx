# Dilophosaurus

## Known species
- *D. wetherilli*;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, animal remains;<br/>
**Supplementary food**: fish;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, branches, roots;

## In real life
*Dilophosaurus* was an early theropod dinosaur from the Early Jurassic of North America. It was one of the oldest known large carnivorous dinosaurs, and was the largest predator in its ecosystem. It was adapted for taking big prey items, using its strong and robust jaws and powerful grappling arms. Several *Dilophosaurus* specimens have been discovered with severe skeletal trauma, indicating that these animals had quite a violent and intense lifestyle.
Its most iconic feature are the double head crests that likely served as display ornaments.

## In the Garden
On the isle of Gondwa, *Dilophosaurus* is an ancient master of survival. Lurking and slinking in the wastelands and dry woodlands, from shadow to shadow, this bright-crested predator possesses a set of long sharp teeth ideally suited to tear open deep wounds with surgical precision, leaving the target heavily bleeding. Even if the victim attempts to flee at this point, a fresh crimson trail will betray its path, making tracking it only a matter of time. And where there is one *Dilophosaurus*, there are often more…


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Are heavily fond of deserts and forests;
- Prefer to build their nests in protected hidden areas among large rocks or trees;
- Court each-other by bobbing their heads and showing off their crests;
- While perfectly capable of surviving on its own, closely living individuals sometimes band together into fission-fusion groups that can tackle larger prey and share the spoils. Such groups can form and disband depending on the season and prey availability in the area;
- Despite being cathemeral (active day and night), *Dilophosaurus* is more adapted for daytime foraging, relying on acute vision to locate food;
- Are known to be voracious eaters, eating as much as they can in one go to better survive scarcity, especially in desert areas;
- After a meal, Dilophosaurus can be often seen resting, basking or sleeping on large warmed up rocks to aid with digestion.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have a maximum of 4 individuals in a group called a coalition;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called slinkers;
- May substitute 2 of their coalition members with 1 deviant *Auroran King Cryolophosaurus*;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*, who often swarm around them attracted by the smell of blood;
- Can substitute 2 of their group members with 1 *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*;

## Conflicts
- Lone individuals can hunt animals of up to large size;
- A coalition can hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- Coalitions can be highly territorial and will readily challenge any carnivore that intrudes upon their hunting grounds, including their own kind, as the resources of the wastelands are scarce;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated immature individuals as food. Also have no qualms about eating flesh from any dead *Dilophosaurus*. Every bit of food is precious in the badlands.


## Other interactions
- Can attempt to steal meat chunks from kills made by other carnivores at their own risk;
- *Dilophosaurus* are moderately tolerant of smaller scavengers at their kills as long as they keep their distance;
- Will often sun themselves on rocks, welcoming any *Rhamphorynchus* for parasite cleaning services;
- Dilophosaurus sometimes tease basking crocodylomorphs by biting their tails seemingly for fun.

## Survival tips
- *Dilophosaurus* is a diurnally oriented animal and has a lot of abilities that work only during the daytime;
- You can empty your stomach for an emergency speed boost, but be careful not to drop your hunger to dangerous levels;
- During conflicts, keep on the move, *Dilophosaurus* are very fast and can dodge a lot of the most lethal attacks from their opponents or flee the confrontation altogether;
- You can utilise bleed to do most of the work for you during a difficult hunt;
- Common nickname: dilo;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
