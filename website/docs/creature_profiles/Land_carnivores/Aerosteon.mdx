# Aerosteon

## Known species
- *A. riocoloradensis*;
- *A. antiquus*;
- *A. deinotitan*;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat and bone;<br/>
**Nest materials**: bones, rock, mud;

## In real life
*Aerosteon* was a big megaraptorid theropod from the Late Cretaceous of Argentina. Its family, *Megaraptoridae*, were enigmatic southern predators known from fragmentary remains, and at least some of them were characterised by giant flexible forelimbs with enormous hooked claws. *Aerosteon* in particular is notable for preserving signs of extensive pneumatisation in its skeleton: it had a system of air sacks running throughout its body like a modern bird.

## In the Garden
On the isle of Gondwa, *Aerosteon* is a lanky grassland hunter that is most active when the sun is high. Like a noon demon, it appears in the hot shivering air of the midday, when its prey is most vulnerable to exhaustion and overheating. It envelops its victims in a hug of death, slicing open and puncturing vital arteries with its scythe-like talons. The extensive air sack system of *Aerosteon* and a skinny frame prevent this gaunt devil from overheating, and many of the hollow chambers penetrate deep into the very bones of the animal.
Beware the tall grass when the sun is high.

## Roleplaying guidelines
- *Aerosteon’s* long slender legs let it excel at hunting prey in open habitats;
- Prefers to nest in caves, shadowy places or in deep shrubbery;
- Primarily active during the day to avoid competition with *Maip*;
- Prefer to hunt in the open fields during the zenith of the day, retreating into the forests and canyons in the dark of the night;
- Court each-other by doing synchronised arm-dances in an open field;
- Often rest on tall rocks overlooking their hunting fields;
- Are known to sometimes vomit blood and scrape their claws together as a form of intimidation display;
- Can swallow and digest full bones;
- Are quite tender parents, bringing their young unusual objects to play with.


## Friendly interactions
- Can have a maximum of 3 mature individuals in a group called a coven;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a coven, called scratchlings;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- Can substitute 1 of their group members with a *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*.

## Conflicts
- Can hunt or challenge up to large animals on its own;
- A coven can hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated scratchlings as food.

## Other interactions
- Tries to minimise competition with its larger nocturnal cousin *Maip* by niche partitioning, and when the two species meet they usually try to avoid each-other;
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner isn’t actively eating.

## Survival tips
- You can fully specialise into either raw damage or bleed damage. Bloodloss drains health of your targets over time the more they move;
- *Aerosteon* can jump really high for its size, allowing it to scale difficult terrain and rocks;
- Common nickname: aero;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

## Roleplaying inspirations
- Lady Midday, also known as the Noonwraith or *Poludnica* from Slavic folklore, a malicious specter which appears during the high noon in the fields wielding a scythe or a sickle;
- Sighthounds like the greyhound and the borzoi;
- Leopards and jaguars.
