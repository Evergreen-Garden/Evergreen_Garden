# Deinonychus

## Known species
- *D. antirrhopus*: +10% health recovery;
- *D. apareonus*: +50% acceleration speed;
- *D. jayensis*: +25% jump height;

**Size category:** small;<br/>
**Diet:** meat, noxious amphibians, fish, animal remains, arthropods;<br/>
**Nest materials:** rocks, reeds, roots;

## In real life
*Deinonychus* was a birdlike dromaeosaurid from the Early Cretaceous of North America. The initial discovery of this active and mobile predator was very important for the public perception of dinosaurs at the time of its description, and led to the iconic depiction of Jurassic Park “velociraptors” as cunning and agile killers.
Its name “terrible claw” describes the enlarged hooked talon that the animal possessed on its second toe. There is no doubt that it was used as a hunting instrument.
To this day there is no definitive answer to the question if *Deinonychus* hunted alone or in groups.

## In the Garden
On the island of Gondwa, *Deinonychus* is one of the smallest hunters, smaller than even some of the critters. Nevertheless, it uses its superior speed, agility and cunning to successfully survive and hunt. One raptor poses a threat only to babies… But their numbers can snowball in a blink of an eye. A horde of raptors can strike fear even into the scariest giants of the island.

## Roleplaying guidelines
- Although they can gather together to hunt in large packs, solitary raptors are still independent and capable animals on their own;
- Can quickly switch from solitary to social if enough raptors are found in one place;
- Constantly chatter among each other while travelling and resting together;
- Often choose to nest on rocks and cliffs inaccessible to larger predators;
- Colonial nesters: pack members often make nests in close proximity to form an “alert net” and raise chicks together;
- Enjoy digging up termite mounds and anthills with their sickle claws to feast on delicious grubs!


## Friendly interactions
- A group of mature 2-5 *Deinonychus* is called a screech;
- A group of 6-12 mature *Deinonychus* is called a horde:
- Can freely accept *Latenivenatrix* individuals into a horde;
- Additionally, can have 1 deviant *Achillobator* as a mob boss in place of 2 small raptors (1 boss + 5 small raptors);
- During nesting, a baby deviant *Achillobator* can get adopted into the horde as a brood parasite to become a future mob boss!
- A horde can raise an unlimited number of immature small raptors.

## Conflicts
- Solitary *Deinonychus* are skittish and typically don’t hunt animals that exceed medium size;
- A screech (2-5) of raptors, however, gets much bolder with numbers, and can attempt to bring down any prey with patience and strategy;
- A horde (6-12) can hunt only animals of huge size and above together;
- Can sustain themselves by gouging out small chunks of flesh from living targets of any size and running away (at their own risk);
- Can attempt to contest anything for a carcass if they feel like their numbers are sufficient;
- **Swarm Tactics**: can sustain 2 casualties (body downs) before the hunt is considered over;
- *Swarm Tactics* doesn’t apply in a *Mob Boss* group;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated immature individuals as food.

## Other interactions
- Can attempt to share a meal with random carnivores or steal meat chunks at their own risk;
- Sometimes live as commensals inside *Lisowicia* burrows.


## Deviant profile *Royal Adjutant*
- A deviant *Deinonychus* doesn’t seek the company of other raptors, but instead groups up with **giant land carnivores**(both solo and pairs), forming a mutualistic relationship with them. It serves as a scout, leading the apex predator to prey, assists in fights and picks its teeth in exchange for protection from their terrifying patron and food from the royal plate.
- A giant land carnivore can have up to 2 *adjutants* with them - one for each shoulder!
- An *adjutant’s* death doesn’t stop combat unless it was specifically targeted as prey.

## Survival tips
- *Deinonychus* is the best jumping creature in the game, use it to escape danger!
- “Lucky feather” can save you from a lethal blow;
- Most aggressive critters will try to kill you;
- Common nicknames: deinony, raptor;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)

## Roleplaying inspiration
- Dholes, bush dogs and African painted dogs. Small, gregarious and fierce!
