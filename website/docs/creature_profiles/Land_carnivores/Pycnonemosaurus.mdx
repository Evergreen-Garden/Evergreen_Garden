# Pycnonemosaurus

## Known species
- *P. nevesi*: +10% stamima recovery;
- *P. taurinus*: +3% attack damage;
- *P. imperator*: +15 attack knockback;

**Size category*: large;<br/>
**Diet**: fresh meat;<br/>
**Nest materials**: rock, reeds, roots;

## In real life
*Pycnonemosaurus* was a theropod dinosaur known from the Late Cretaceous of Brazil. It is the largest known member of the *Abelisauridae* family, a distinctive group of southern hemisphere theropods that possessed incredibly small useless arms and short blunt, often ornamented skulls. Even though *Pycnonemosaurus* is only known from fragmentary remains, the preserved tail vertebrae and leg bones show that it had long powerful legs and a stiff tail that could anchor very large leg muscles.
In life it had lived in a semi-arid landscape.

## In the Garden
On the isle of Gondwa, *Pycnonemosaurus* is a speedy demon, a large carnivore specialised for running. Its rough and bumpy skin is infused with bony ossicles, making predators break teeth upon its scales, and its short blunt skull carries knobs and horns that are perfect for bashing. It may lack impressive claws or large powerful jaws, but *Pycnonemosaurus* makes up for it with sheer persistence and endurance.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Long legs and outstanding stamina make *Pycnonemosaurus* an excellent open country sprinter, proficient at ambushing its prey from the bushes and striking it in a lightning-fast attack;
- Prefer to nest in arid and semi-arid scrubland;
- A single *Pycnonemosaurus* often constructs a large complexly decorated nest that serves to attract a mate and acts as the courtship stage;
- Court each-other with the typical abelisaurid “wiggle-dance”;
- Males, being the brighter sex, tend to be more showy, and use a combination of dashing looks, a flawless dance and a dandy nest to impress a potential partner (though, not always);
- Females are generally more brutish and straightforward, being the more aggressive gender (though, again, not always);
- Although all dinosaurs are known to sometimes form same-sex pairs, *Pycnonemosaurus* for some reason displays a higher percentage of same-sex pair-bonding than seen in other large carnivores;
- Has a weak and shallow bite force, making it unable to process bones and carrion;
- Immature individuals of the same clutch can be seen roughhousing with one-another, head-butting their parents and being little troublemakers in general; 
- Some individuals are known to seek miscellaneous objects and bite onto them to better absorb shock from head bashing. Different individuals have a preference for different objects as “mouthguards”, which can range from common bones and branches to whole sulcata tortoises. This behaviour constitutes as active tool use.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have a maximum of 2 mature individuals in a group, called a pair;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called hotdogs;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- Can freely group with *Carnotaurus*.


## Conflicts
- A lone *Pycnonemosaurus* can hunt or challenge animals of up to huge size;
- A pair can hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- Sometimes individuals in a pair, especially if they’re siblings, engage in playful duels with each-other, using charges and head bashing. In a duel they rarely, if ever, use teeth, preferring blunt attacks;
- Upon wandering on a potential mate, a female will sometimes challenge them for a duel to test their fitness as part of the complex courtship ritual of *Pycnonemosaurus*. She may pair up with them if she is satisfied with both their strength and the beauty of their stage performance;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated immature specimens as food.

## Survival tips
- Your trot speed is one of the fastest among your peers, letting *Pycnonemosaurus* cover vast distances in little time when it patrols the island, as well as out-walk most dangers;
- *Pycnonemosaurus* has many abilities that incentivise hight risk in exchange for high reward, so it may be difficult to get used to optimally using your attack kit without concussing yourself on accident;
- Knockback can be utilised to push a foolish opponent off a cliff;
- Your speed allows you to disengage from most fights, and there’s no shame in running away to live another day!
- *Pycnonemosaurus* is bad at quickly turning, so don’t let enemies get behind you!
- Common nickname: pycno;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
