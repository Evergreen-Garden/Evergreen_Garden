# Utahraptor

## Known species
- *U. ostrommaysi*;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, animal remains;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, branches, roots;

## In real life
*Utahraptor* was a giant dromaeosaur (raptor), that lived in what is now the US during the Early Cretaceous. It is the largest dromaeosaur known from relatively complete remains and was the largest carnivore in its environment. Unlike many smaller raptors, it had a heavy and robust build with a large boxy skull. Like all members of its family, *Utahraptor* had an enlarged sickle claw on its second toe. This animal may have been gregarious based on the discovery of several specimens of different age preserved in close association.

## In the Garden
On the isle of Gondwa *Utahraptor* is a cunning birdlike predator equipped with deadly weapons. It uses its large and heavy skull to deliver devastating bites. It has immensely strong muscular legs that can strike with deadly force, ripping prey with sickle talons. Soft feathers help it with stealthy approach, and its wings and tail frond are perfect stabilisers for agile running. Yet, its deadliest weapon is its brain. Sharp senses, quick reflexes and keen awareness makes *Utahraptor* an efficient hunter that can adapt its strategy depending on the environment and the type of prey.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- *Utahraptors* are speedy, endurant hunters that excel in running their prey down across open biomes but are also proficient at parkouring rocky cliffs;
- Often nest on rocks and cliffs overlooking a point of interest;
- Their nests are often littered with crushed bones from regurgitated pellets;
- Lone raptors are independent hunters, but sometimes they form loosely hierarchical hunting groups;
- Though they are intelligent animals, *Utahraptor’s* sociality is somewhat awkward, with lots of bickering and hissing, even among the juveniles;
- Communicate using infrasonic drumming;
- Are known to produce loud metallic barking when excited or agitated, like during group hunts or upon finding a carcass;
- Are known to sometimes carry around and play with dead critters;
- Statistically, females tend to be more aggressive and behaviourally dominant in a group.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have up to 4 mature individuals in a group, called a cast;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called eyases;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;
- Can substitute 2 of their group members with a *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*.

## Conflicts
- Lone raptors can hunt animals of up to large size;
- A cast can hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- Bully and hunt smaller raptors, can contest any dromaeosaur or troodontid for territory, considering them competition;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated eyases as food.

## Other interactions
- Can attempt to steal meat chunks from kills made by other carnivores.

## Survival tips
- You can use your “*Shocking screech*” to disrupt most positive effects on your target;
- You can jump very high, but you still take quite a lot of fall damage;
- Common nickname: utah;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately difficult)


## Roleplaying inspirations
- Owls like the barn owl and the eagle owl;
- Hawks and falcons like the goshawk and the peregrine falcon;
- Felines like the lynx, bobcat, caracal and puma;
