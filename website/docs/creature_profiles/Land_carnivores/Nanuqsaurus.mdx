# Nanuqsaurus

## Known species
- *N. hoglungi*;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, animal remains;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, branches, roots;

## In real life
*Nanuqsaurus* is a tyrannosaurid that lived in Alaska during the Late Cretaceous. In that time, Alaska was located ever further north than today, making *Nanuqsaurus* a polar predator, but the overall climate was warmer than today. It is known from the Prince Creek formation, where it coexisted with herbivores like *Pachyrhinosaurus*.

## In the Garden
On the isle of *Gondwa* this smaller cousin of *Tyrannosaurus* is a recent migrant, dispersing from higher, colder latitudes. Despite Gondwa’s tropical climate, this feathered predator has perfectly adapted to prowl windswept plains and shaded forests of the island. As a tyrannosaur, *Nanuqsaurus* is an intelligent and socially-inclined animal, often hunting in family groups. Though, a lone polar tyrant is not exactly a pushover either, employing stamina, persistence and ferocity to wear down its prey. All hail king in the north!

## Roleplaying guidelines
- Is a pursuit predator highly adapted to chase down prey across open landscapes; 
- Prefers to nest at the forest edges, hiding its den among gnarled roots;
- Food remains often litter the vicinity of the den; 
- Detest arid habitats and never nest there;
- Pups are very playful with each-other and often engage in play-fighting;
- *Nanuqsaurus* can get very distressed upon experiencing a pack member’s death and make lots of panicked noises;
- When damaged, lick their wounds to clean them.

## Friendly interactions
- A group of 2-3 mature individuals is called a growl;
- A group of 4-5 mature individuals is called a maul;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called pups;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*, the pups in particular liking to play with the pterosaur;
- Can substitute 1 of their group members with a *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*.


## Conflicts
- Lone *Nanuqsaurus* are surprisingly bold and can hunt or contest up to large animals;
- A growl (2-3) can hunt or contest animals of any size;
- A maul (4-5) can only hunt animals of giant size and above together;
- Partial cannibals: can eat fallen pack members as a last resort;

## Other interactions
- *Nanuqsaurus* has a very special relationship with *Rhamphorhynchus*, recognising them as beneficial presence and freely allows them into their dens;
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner isn’t actively eating.

## Deviant profile *Outcast*
- Sometimes a *Nanuqsaurus* gets thrown out of the pack. Forced to make a living on its own, such an exile develops strange new habits to survive;
- An *Outcast* is a lone wolf and cannot group with other *Nanuqsaurus* while following this profile;
- Can group with up to 3 *Alioramus*, leading them into battle;
- Can actively hunt and cannibalise its own kind;
- *Outcasts* can live in the desert and learn to tolerate arid conditions better than other *Nanuqsaurus* specimens;
- Are often battle damaged from conflicts with their previous pack (you can choose the *Battle Damaged* variant).

## Survival tips
- Stamina management is the key to survival;
- You are fast, but not a very agile animal;
- Common nickname: nanuq;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
