# Daspletosaurus

## Known species
- *D. torosus*: +25% knockback resist;
- *D. profundus*: +10% fracture damage;
- *D. horneri*: +3% attack damage;

**Size category**: large;<br/>
**Diet**: fresh meat;<br/>
**Nest materials**: rock, mud.

## In real life
*Daspletosaurus* was a tyrannosaur theropod from the Late Cretaceous of North America. Being a derived member of the *Tyrannosauridae* family, it was a smaller relative of the famous *T. rex*, and shared a similar body plan, although, less extremely robust than the latter. It was widespread across the Larimidian half of the divided North American landmass, coexisting, and possibly predating upon many famous genera of hadrosaurs and ceratopsids.

## In the Garden
On the isle of Gondwa, *Daspletosaurus* is an advanced killer that uses its tyrannosaurian jaws as a versatile tool for dispatching any type of prey. Biting down with awesome force, it can strategically maim, fracture bone and pierce the toughest of armors. But far from a mindless brute, *Daspletosaurus* is an intelligent and observant hunter. To become successful predators, they learn from their parents where to strike the weak spots of their prey, how to ambush, and how to work together. If *T. rex* is the king of dinosaurs, *Daspletosaurus* is the duke.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- *Daspletosaurus* is very versatile in terms of habitat preferences, adapting to almost any conditions;
- Prefer to nest on the edge of lush grassfields, scraping a nest among bordering rock fields and under lonely trees;
- *Daspletosaurus* is a picky eater. It does not tolerate rotten flesh, only going for fresh red meat;
- Court one-another by gifting dead critters;
- *Daspletosaurus* are very involved parents, teaching their young how to hunt from a young age;
- Have a very strong pair-bond, working together with high coordination and assuming complementing roles during a hunt. Also cuddle affectionately;
- Sometimes loudly broadcast at the beginning of the day to mark their territory;
- Some populations of *Daspletosaurus* specialise on taking down giant prey items, while others specialise in small quarry. These two distinct “cultures” or ecotypes exist parallel to one another and don’t compete for resources. The offspring tend to inherit the hunting preferences of their parents, learning and observing their tactics.

## Friendly interactions
- A group of 2 mature individuals is called a pair;
- A group of 3 mature individuals is called a dynasty;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called princelings;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*: those teeth aren’t gonna clean themselves.

## Conflicts
- Lone *Daspletosaurus* and pairs, being incredibly versatile predators, can hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- A dynasty (3) can only hunt animals of giant size and above together;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated princelings as food and have no qualms about feeding from *Daspletosaurus* corpses.

## Other interactions
- Are known to bite and bicker over corpses even with their mate or siblings;
- Can be fiercely territorial over their preferred hunting grounds, especially to their own kind.


## Survival tips
- Use the correct hide ability depending on if you’re gonna hunt big or small things. There is nothing so undignifying as dying to some rats when you have your giant-slaying hide active;
- Learn how to time your special bites;
- As an obligate carnivore, always be on the lookout for critters to snack upon. You can’t rely on carrion!
- Common nickname: dasp;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
