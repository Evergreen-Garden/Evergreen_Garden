# Yutyrannus

## Known species
- *Y. huali*;

**Size category**: large;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, animal remains, fruits, roots;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, branches, roots;

## In real life
*Yutyrannus* was a theropod dinosaur from the Early Cretaceous of China. It was an early representative of the tyrannosauroid lineage, and possibly belonged to the family *Proceratosauridae*. *Yutyrannus* is the largest dinosaur found with preserved evidence of fossilised feathers, possessing insulating down over its body. This carnivore is part of the Yixian formation biota, and has lived in a temperate rainforest environment with high seasonality that frequently experienced cold weather.
It was contemporaneous with *Psittacosaurus*.

## In the Garden
Hailing from a colder climate, this singing feathered tyrant has managed to make a home on the isle of Gondwa. Lurking in the hills and forests, it is a swift pursuit hunter, ripping at tendons of its prey and making it panic with rumbling echoing roars. Its feathery coat makes *Yutyrannus* stand out among the large carnivore cast of the island, being uniquely suited for helping better tolerate shifts in weather and temperature.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Sexual dimorphism: males more often tend to have larger crests and thicker manes of feathers, while females are more modest in their features. However, this is not a hard rule, but merely a statistic;
- Prefer to nest deep within the forests, often finding a secluded cave, den or a rocky overhang;
- Like to roam and hunt in plains and woodlands;
- Are very talkative animals, and have a complex vocal repertoire;
- Often form monogamous pairs, mating with their partner for life. Bonded individuals exhibit great care and tenderness for each-other, snuggling together, constantly communicating with soft calls and chirps and feeding critters to each-other;
- Perform courtship display by song and dance;
- Grunt and pant when excited;
- Parents often bring their adolescent offspring on hunts to teach them how to take down prey. The younglings usually try to provide support for their parents, but let the adults do the bulk of the work;
- Some individuals greatly enjoy sweet roots and melons as a staple part of their diet.

## Friendly interactions
- A group of 2 mature *Yutyrannus* is called a pair;
- A group of 3 mature *Yutyrannus* is called a triad;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called chicks or floofs;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*.

## Conflicts
- Lone *Yutyrannus* can hunt or challenge animals of up to huge size;
- Pairs (2) can hunt animals of any size;
- Triads (3) can hunt animals of huge size and above together.

## Other interactions
- *Yutyrannus* is quite inquisitive and can exhibit more curious friendliness towards random animals than most large carnivores. 

## Survival tips
- *Yutyrannus* is extremely speedy for its size, being able to catch up to many medium-sized prey items in a straight-line pursuit or escape being mobbed by packs;
- *Yutarannus* has a variety of support calls in its arsenal, use them!
- Common nickname: yuty;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
