# Tyrannotitan

## 🔎 Known species
- *T. chubutensis*: +10% venom and bleed healing;
- *T. grandis*: +10% health recovery;

## 🧬 Hybrids
- x *Giganotosaurus carolinii*: +5% bleed damage.

🦖 **Size category**: Giant<br/>
🕑 **Passive growth time**: 4 hours<br/>
💟 **Total group limit**: 2 mature individuals<br/>
🔺 Minor group: **1**<br/>
🔹 Family group: **2**

## 🍽️ Diet
- **Hypercarnivore**: only the bloodiest meat.


## 🇦🇷 In real life
*Tyrannotitan* was a giant carnivorous theropod from the Early Cretaceous of what is now Argentina. It belonged to the notorious *Carcharodontosauridae* family or the “shark-toothed” predators that filled the role of giant carnivores across the globe during the Early Cretaceous. While the ancestors of *Tyrannosaurus rex* were still small, this lineage of titanic tyrants terrorised the Earth. Like most of its relatives, *Tytannotitan* possessed serrated cutting teeth. It skeleton was highly pneumatised (filled with hollow spaces) and its arms were very small even when compared to other carcharodontosaurs. *Tyrannotitan* was an apex predator of its environment where it fed on long-necked dinosaurs.

## In the Garden
*Tyrannotitan*. The king of blood. The crimson scourge of the wastelands. This horrifying carnivore is the most athletic of top-order predators of The Garden. With strong muscular legs and an expansive system of air sacks for advanced breathing, it is perfectly adapted to relentlessly pursuit its prey with a tireless stride and run it down with uncanny speed. Instead of bone-crushing biteforce of Tyrannosaurs, *Tyrannotitan* uses its sharp serrated teeth to leave shallow, but devastating bleeding wounds, slashing open veins and arteries with each bite. Its grisly jaws leave behind the most nightmarish of injuries.
However, there is more to this animal than mindless carnage: *Tyrannotitan* is a behaviourally complex and observant theropod that has a capacity to learn and adapt. Their behaviours are multi-faceted and nuanced. *Tyrannotitan* is an heir to a long dynasty of royal killers.


## ⛰️ Habitat preference
- **Scrubland**: semi-arid scrublands with low tree cover provide the optimal environment for *Tyrannotitan*;
- **Desert**: *Tyrannotitan* is perfectly adapted for harsh, dry conditions and long treks across the sands.

## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- *Tyrannotitans* are patient hunters, and can circle their prey for hours like sharks as the victim bleeds out dry;
- *Tyrannotitan* has a highly efficient stride and can cover vast distances in search of prey;
- *Tyrannotitans* use both booming vocalisations and visual body language to signal their presence to others of their kind: a healthy *Tyrannotitan’s* scales and hornlets shimmer under the desert sun like precious jewels;
- Different individuals develop diverse hunting strategies, honing specialised tactics for decades;
- Some individuals quite literally bathe in the blood of their enemies, using it as skin protection and macabre moisturiser in arid conditions. A crimson mantle for the king in red;
- Others use their serrated shark teeth to rip flesh from still living victims, causing catastrophic bloodloss;
- Experienced pairs of *Tyrannotitans* sometimes specialise in bringing down colossal sauropods…
- Often sleep under the shade of trees or canyons while digesting a meal;
- *Tyrannotitans* tend to be viciously territorial to one-another and don’t engage in long-term relationships, but sometimes form temporary alliances when prey is plentiful enough;
- Perform courtship by showing off a healthy physique.

## ♥️ Grouping
- A group can consist of 1-2 mature individuals;
- **Minor group**: 1 individual is called a loner;
- **Family group**: 2 mature individuals are called a pair.
- A pair of *Tyrannotitans* is considered a *colossal* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*. Blood-sucking imps for the court of the blood tyrant!
- Can have up to 2 deviant *Royal Adjutant* deinonychus in a group.

## 🪺 Reproduction
- Nest materials: bones, rock, mud 
- Prefer to nest on warm, sandy soil;
- Young *Tyrannotitans* are known as whelps;
- Most often raise young alone, chasing away even their partner from the nest;
- Very protective of their nesting sites, trying to provide the freshest meat for their hatchlings. However, the parent often chooses a favourite among the clutch, and young whelps fight for this position. Only the strongest will survive, while the weak get swallowed by the sands;
- Parents often exile their own children with extreme hostility once the young predators reach an independent age;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.


## 🍖 Hunting
- **Minor group**: a lone (1) *Tyrannotitan* may hunt animals of any size;
- **Family group**: A pair (2) may only go for targets of *giant* size and above together. For hunting smaller prey, only one of a pair may engage;
- A group consisting of only immature individuals count as a **Minor group** for hunting restrictions;
- **Infanticidal**: can hunt immature *Tyrannotitans*;
- Do not hunt other mature *Tyrannotitans* (although devouring fallen territorial rivals is very common).

## 🦴 Carcass interactions
- Can challenge any creature for a claimed carcass;
- Can freely feed from *Tyrannotitan* carcasses;
- Immature individuals count as **scavengers**.


## 🚩 Claiming a territory
- **Hyper-territorial**: automatically claim any area they reside in and **have** to contest any other giant land carnivores they come across, otherwise they must leave;
- Any area of residence of *Tyrannotitan* automatically becomes its *Court*;
- Have to contest any other giant land carnivores or leave the territory;
- Optionally, may view any *huge* land carnivores as competition as well and challenge them;
- After being exiled from territory, must wait a minimum of 15 minutes before returning;
-  Teeth will flash and blood shall spring in the Court of the Crimson King;
- May consume the rival *Tyrannotitan* that fell during a territory contest;
- Immature individuals don’t participate in territorial behaviour, and can be tolerated by adults.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Tyrannotitan* is the fastest of giant land carnivores and has endurance to spare;
- Your mobility allows you to effectively avoid damage as horrendous bleed chips away at your victim’s health;
- *Tyrannotitan* excels at fighting in open scapes where it has room to run circles around its enemies;
- You have the option to specialise either in dealing more damage, or on sustained healing through flesh grazing;
- Beware groups of small creatures! In a fight against a horde, cover your back!
- Ironically, *Tyrannotitan* is weak to bloodloss;
- Common nicknames: titan, TT;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️(challenging)
