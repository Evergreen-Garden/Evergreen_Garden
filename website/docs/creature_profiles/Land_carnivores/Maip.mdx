# Maip

## Known species
- *M. macrothorax*;

**Size category**: large;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, animal remains;<br/>
**Supplementary food**: fish, mollusks;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds , mud, roots.

## In real life
*Maip* was a large theropod dinosaur from the Late Cretaceous of Argentina. It is the largest known member of the *Megaraptoridae* family, and is known from preserved enormous ribs that formed an unusually wide torso. Other members of this mysterious family are known from even more scattered remains, but at least some of them possessed giant arms with long claws.
The name of this genus was borrowed from the Tehuelche mythology, referencing an evil supernatural entity associated with shadow, death and cold wind.

## In the Garden
*Maip* is the night screamer of Gondwa, an elusive moonlight spectre that harvests souls with its giant scythe-shaped talons. It is the most enigmatic and secretive among the large carnivores of the island, and possesses an unusual body plan with grotesquely large forelimbs and extremities that are too slender for its size. Its main method of dispatching prey is equally unsettling, with *Maip* slowly and methodically bleeding out its victim dry.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Prefer to blend their nests with grass, and weave elaborate wicker constructions out of tall grass;
- Prefer to stick to forests and closed shady habitats when the sun is up;
- Prefer to go into open fields and hills when the sun goes down and during fog;
- Prefer to be silent during the day, but often broadcast during night and fog, as if welcoming the darkness;
- Communicate with family members by making giggling and cackling noises, especially during hunts;
- Court one-another by outstretching their arms;
- During hot sunny days can be seen rolling in the grass in forest clearings and sunning;
- Extremely voracious eaters, consuming unimaginable quantities of meat in one go and leaving little behind for scavengers;
- Sometimes come down to comb shorelines in search of mollusks and crustaceans;
- Juveniles sometimes drink their own mother’s blood to heal.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have a maximum of 2 individuals in a group, called a pair;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called squeaks;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*, who are easily drawn to the smell of dried blood that lingers where *Maip* hunts.

## Conflicts
- Can hunt or challenge anything that bleeds.

## Other interactions
- Niche partition with its smaller cousin *Aerosteon* by having different activity cycles and prefer to leave each-other in peace if they cross paths;
- May share their kills with random unrelated *Maips*, as they exhibit a strange solidarity and altruism towards members of their own species.


## Survival tips
- As a *Maip*, gravity is your biggest enemy. Even tiny falls can break your fragile frame;
- *Maip* has a fast stamina drain, and many of your special abilities drain stamina on top of that, so manage your stamina carefully, running in short bursts;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

## Roleplaying inspirations
- The nocturnal deadly majesty of owls;
- The roguish trickery of hyenas;
- The voracious and unsettling table manners of a monitor lizard.
