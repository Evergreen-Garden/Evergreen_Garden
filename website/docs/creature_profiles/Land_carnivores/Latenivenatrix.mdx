# Latenivenatrix

## Known species
- *L. mcmasterae*: +10% damage at night
- *L. inequalis*: +25% jump height
- *L. formosus*: +10% stamina recovery

**Size category**: small;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, noxious amphibians, fish, animal remains;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, mud, roots;

## In real life
*Latenivenatrix* was described as an unusually large member of the family Troodontidae from Late Cretaceous of North America. It was a family of very bird-like long-legged carnivores and omnivores closely related to dromaeosaurids, or “the raptors”.
North American troodontids had a very convoluted history of discovery. Most of troodontid material from the continent was historically assigned to the genus *Troodon*, which was later revealed to be a wastebasket taxon and a *nomen dubium*. Later all “*Troodon*” remains were reclassified as either *Latenivenatrix* or *Stenonychosaurus*, and even more recently, *Latenivenatrix* was shown to likely be a junior synonym for *Stenonychosaurus*, lacking any distinguishable traits of its own.
So, strictly speaking, the animal in the game is a representation of *Stenonychosaurus*, at least until further developments in the field. Only future will shed more light on the enigmatic taxonomy of North American troodontids.

## In the Garden
*Latenivenatrix* is among the most intelligent animals on the island of Gondwa. Having a proportional brain size as large as in modern birds helps this perceptive and sleek predator to come up with new survival strategies and pass them down to its young. Their large eyes with binocular vision are perfect for seeing in the dim moonlight, and their long legs with sickle claws are good for both running and killing. And while their cunning makes them proficient, if small hunters on their own, *Latenivenatrix* are known to congregate in large groups that can make even the giants afraid of the dark…


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Lone *Latenivenatrix* are very curious animals and often hang around other creatures to watch them;
- Though *Latenivenatrix* are cathemeral (awake during both day and night), their activity levels skyrocket at night, making them venture out into more open habitats and run amok; In contrast, during the day they tend to be more wary and alert;
- Prefer to build nests on scenic cliffs or rock formations;
- Colonial nesters: a tribe often shares a nesting spot with everyone contributing to raising the young, the end result looking somewhat like a rookery;
- Can join groups of *Deinonychus* by mimicking their calls;
- In mixed groups behaviourally dominate their lesser peers; 
- Immature individuals are very playful, often jumping over adults and play-fighting with each-other;
- Have an eye for decorating their nests with peculiar objects such as shiny rocks, shells and skulls. This curiosity for pretty things leads them to often carry unusual objects around when not hunting;
- Have complex social interactions and hierarchies within a tribe;
- Bark at the Moon;

## Friendly interactions
- A group of 2-5 mature *Latenivenatrix* is called a murder;
- A group of 6-10 mature individuals is called a tribe:
- Can freely join a *Deinonychus* horde;
- Additionally, can have 1 deviant *Achillobator* as a mob boss in place of 2 small raptors (1 boss + 5 small raptors);
- During nesting, a baby deviant *Achillobator* can get adopted into the tribe as a brood parasite to become a future mob boss!
- A tribe can raise an unlimited number of immature small raptors; can adopt young *Deinonychus*.


## Conflicts
- Solitary *Latenivenatrix* can hunt animals of up to medium size;
- A murder (2-5) of raptors can hunt anything;
- A tribe (6-10) can only hunt animals of huge size and above;
- Can attempt to contest anything for a carcass if they feel like their numbers are sufficient;
- A tribe can contest anything for territory during the dark hours;
- **Swarm Tactics**: can sustain 2 casualties (body downs) before the hunt is considered over;
- *Swarm Tactics* doesn’t apply in a *Mob Boss* group;

## Other interactions
- Can attempt to share a meal with random carnivores or steal meat chunks at their own risk;

## Survival tips
- Don’t forget to switch your abilities between night and day! Some of your abilities are useless in the wrong time of day;
- *Latenivenatrix* gets more deadly at night;
- Common nickname: laten;
- ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
