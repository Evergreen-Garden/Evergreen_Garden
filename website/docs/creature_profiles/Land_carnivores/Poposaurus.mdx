# Poposaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *P. gracilis*;
- * P. lagstoni*;

🐺 **Size category**: medium;<br/>
🍗 **Diet**: meat, carrion, noxious amphibians, bones, mollusks;<br/>
🌐 **Range**: Gondwa.

## 🦴 In real life
*Poposaurus* was a bipedal carnivorous reptile known from the Late Triassic of North America. Despite looking strikingly like a dinosaur, *Poposaurus* was in fact, a pseudosuchian, a cousin to crocodiles! It belonged to the group *Poposauroidea*, a bizarre collection of archosaurs some of which walked on two legs, some on four, some possessed sails, and some were even aquatic.
It is theorised that bipedal Poposauroids achieved an erect two-legged stance fully independently from dinosaurs and even other pseudosuchians.

## In the Garden
*Poposaurus* is a bizarre one-of-a-kind creature. It is a small, but mean opportunist from a forgotten lineage. Covered head to toe in tough scutes and with powerful jaws it runs around in search of any suitable meal, be it live prey, rotten carcasses or dry bones. *Poposaurus* doesn’t let anything go to waste.
Although this peculiar pseudosuchian faces competition with many larger dinosaurs, it exhibits a fearless attitude and is ready to greet any danger with an unbothered stare and a mouthful of teeth. *Poposaurus* is a tough survivalist that more than compensates for its small stature with bravado and boldness.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Prefer to nest and rest in dry habitats.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Bold and fearless, even nosy;
- Chatty! Often produce a variety of little grunts and snorts while walking around and inspecting things;
- Love to bask on rocks;
- Often form gregarious groups to roam and huddle together for rest;
- Some individuals can secrete a paralytic poison to fend off attackers;
- Simple, straightforward creatures that prefer headstrong daring-do over cunning and smarts;
- Often menacingly stare down creatures that try to intimidate them;
- Voracious eaters prone to feeding frenzy;
- Group members often squabble over food;
- Scavenge, steal and bully other carnivores for corpses if it means that they don’t have to hunt themselves;
- Use aggression and bluff to appear more dangerous than they are.


## ♥️ Grouping
- A group of 2-3 *Poposaurus* is called a crash;
- A group of 4-5 *Poposaurus* is called a confusion;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called popsicles;
- A group of *Poposaurus* counts as *large* target.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can freely group with *Rhamphorhynchus*.

## 🍖 Hunting
- A lone *Poposaurus* can hunt animals of up to *large* size;
- A crash can hunt animals of up to *huge* size;
- A confusion can only hunt animals of *huge* and *giant* size together. For smaller prey only a crash may engage;
- Can perform a one-off parasitic flesh-grazing attack on animals of any size and run off with a meat chunk;
- **Partial cannibal**: can scavenge bodies of fellow *Poposaurus*. Nothing goes to waste.

## ⚡️ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of up to *large* size for territory;
- A solo can challenge animals of up to *large* size a carcass;
- A group can challenge animals of any size for a carcass;
- **Corpse bully**: can ignore 2-minute grace timer when challenging other animals for carcasses;
- A group of *Poposaurus* may challenge and defend together.

## 🍗 Other interactions
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed bodies as long as the owner isn’t actively eating.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Poposaurus* is not very fast, but is quite sturdy for its size!
- These critters become a lot more dangerous near a corpse, especially in large numbers;
- You can eat almost any kind of meat or animal remains;
- Your stamina replenishes quite slowly: *Poposaurus* has a slower metabolism;
- *Poposaurus* is a great swimmer - perhaps a callback to its crocodilian relations;
- Common nickname: popo;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
