# Deltadromeus

## Known species
- *D. agilis*;
- *D. salerii*;
- *D. inexpectatus*;

**Size category**: medium;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, carrion, fruits, berries, roots, nuts, fish, mollusks, *Platyhystrix*;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, mud, roots;

## In real life
*Deltadromeus* was an enigmatic theropod dinosaur from the mid-Cretaceous of Morocco. It had long and slender hindlimbs adapted for fast running. No skull was ever found from this animal, making any guesses about its lifestyle, diet and classification very speculative. The most common hypothesis places *Deltadromeus* in the *Noasauridae* family, a group of bizarre ceratosaurs with unusual dentition (or lack thereof), who’s dietary habits are quite puzzling to figure out themselves.
It was part of the Kem Kem Beds biota, living alongside the famous *Spinosaurus*.

## In the Garden
On the isle of Gondwa, *Deltadromeus* is a strange gangly theropod with bizarre life habits. To some, this creature is an ill omen, a baby snatcher, devourer of putrid carrion, carrying the smell of death and crying serenades of woe in the most unpleasant of tones. To others - an endearing lakeshore jester that brings color to the scenery with its strange dances and agile performance.
The truth is that *Deltadromeus* is all of the above and even more. It is a fleet-footed omnivore that can eat almost anything, and a curious troublemaker that can get on everyone’s nerves by poking its snout into other’s business.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Nests in river valleys, estuarine and coastal areas;
- Is a true omnivore, having an extremely broad and varied diet; 
- When craving protein, specialises in catching small-sized prey like critters and immature dinosaurs;
- Especially loves to eat crabs, frogs and toxic *Platyhystrix*, finding its poisonous mucus delicious;
- Often comb shorelines and riverbanks in search of invertebrates and washed up shark carcasses;
- Can tolerate highly rotten meat;
- Likes to swim and wade in shallow water;
- Court each-other by doing bizarre dances in the shallows;
- Groups can be very noisy, constantly communicating with their unsettling calls;
- As a defence mechanism, it can secrete a smell so unbelievably foul that it disrupts the digestion of all nearby animals.

## Friendly interactions
- Can have up to 3 mature individuals in a group, called a procession;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called noodlets;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*, a fellow pest upon this world;
- Can substitute 1 of their group members with a *dubious Metriacanthosaurus friend*.

## Conflicts
- Lone individuals can hunt or challenge animals of up to large size, but prefer smaller prey;
- A procession can hunt animals of any size;
- Can challenge carnivores of any size for an opened carcass, as they enjoy feasting on internal organs;
- Full cannibals: can hunt their own kind.

## Other interactions
- Can attempt to steal meat chunks from kills made by other carnivores at their own risk;
- If in possession of a larger carcass, tolerate all kinds of scavengers feasting with them, as it makes collectively processing a body easier.


## Deviant profile *The Pharaoh’s Acolyte*
- Some *Deltadromeus* develop a strange relationship with the giant predator *Spinosaurus*;
- In exchange for being fed with fish leftovers, they follow the megatheropod around, seemingly boosting its confidence with their chorus of calls and harassing its enemies;
- An Acolyte is not restricted, but recommended to equip “*Pharaoh’s Favourite*” metabolism, allowing it to only eat fish, shellfish and *Platyhystrix*, making it dependent on their august patron;
- A lone *Spinosaurus* can only have 2 Acolytes in a group;
- If their anointed host dies, the Acolytes can mournfully eat it.

## Survival tips
- *Deltadromeus* is among the fastest animals on the island;
- Compared to most dinosaurs, *Deltadromeus* is a very adept swimmer;
- You can steal hatchling dinosaurs from their parents! Eat children!
- *Deltadromeus* is not very liked by some of the players, so don’t assume kindness from strangers…
- Common nickname: delta;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
