# Torvosaurus

## Known species
- *T. tanneri* (JFD version);
- *T. gurneyi* (PT version);

**Size category**: huge;<br/>
**Diet**: meat, animal remains;<br/>
**Supplementary food**: fish, noxious amphibians, fruit, roots;<br/>
**Nest materials**: branches, reeds;

## In real life
*Torvosaurus* was a formidable predator of the Late Jurassic with a wide distribution, most famously known from North America and Portugal. In Colorado, it was neighbours with the famous dinosaurs of the Morrison Formation, and in Europe it coexisted with similar animals. *Torvosaurus* was the largest known member of *Megalosauridae*, a family of early-diverging tetanuran theropods, and shared its world with predators such as *Allosaurus* and *Ceratosaurus*, as well as herbivorous stegosaurs and the giant sauropods.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Torvosaurus* is a sleek stalker with a powerful bite. With a sinuous low-slung body and square skull-ripping jaws, it glides from shadow to shadow and strikes from ambush, snapping necks in a matter of seconds. The huge megalosaur sports a rather calm and reclusive temperament for such a fearsome carnivore. It is a patient hunter, and consumes both herbivores and carnivores that lack awareness to hear death’s approach…
While older animals usually hunt alone, younger *Torvosaurus* without an established territory often stick together and wander as a troop.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Sexual dimorphism: on average, female *Torvosaurus tanneri* are more bulky than males and more aggressive, holding larger territories. This is not a hard rule, but merely a statistic;
- On average, females also tolerate one-another way less than males;
- American *T. tanneri* prefers to nest in lush vegetated habitats next to freshwater streams;
- European *T. gurneyi* prefers to nest in coastal forests;
- The American species is more social, while the European one is more solitary;
- All *Torvosaurus* like to hide their nests among dense brush;
- Enjoy resting in cool water and wading through streams, and sometimes even indulge in fishing;
- Often targets lesser predators that wander into its hunting range;
- Habitually consumes bitter roots to cull gut parasites that *Torvosaurus* accumulates from eating other carnivores;
- *Torvosaurus* appear to have no negative reaction to melanistic individuals of their species and socialise with them perfectly fine: this helps “panther” genes to be more widespread;
- Communicate with deep rumbling purrs that go into infrasound spectrum and are heard over long distances;
- Like to stay hidden and shadowed, often moving along canyon walls and through tall bush.

## Grouping
- A group of 2 mature individuals is called a pair;
- A group of 3 mature individuals is called an *overkill*;
- Both species can join in a mixed group;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fits in a group;
- A group of *Torvosaurus* counts as a *giant* target.

## Mixed grouping
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*;

## Conflicts
- A lone (1) *Torvosaurus* may hunt animals of any size;
- A pair (2) can hunt animals of *huge* size and above together;
- An *Overkill* (3) may only hunt animals of *giant* size and above together, for smaller prey only a pair may engage;
- Any group of *Torvosaurus* may challenge or defend themselves together;
- Partial cannibals: can see unrelated immature individuals as food;


## Other interactions
- Despise *Allosaurus*.

## Survival tips
- Aim for the head: *Torvosaurus* always deals increased damage to the skull of the target!
- Your bites get stronger with each hit and can quickly overwhelm an opponent caught off-guard;
- Your speed-reducing “*Competitive strike*” can help you ambush even faster animals;
- *Torvosaurus* can swim very well and can even hold its breath underwater for a long time if it runs out of stamina;
- Only use “*Shatterpoint*” bite when hunting in a group! A lone *Torvosaurus* isn’t capable of delivering the combo: at least a pair is needed;
- A successfully primed “*Shatterpoint*” deals increased damage to colossal animals;
- You can *shove* your prey if it tries to stand its ground on the edge of a cliff;
- Common nickname: torvo;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
