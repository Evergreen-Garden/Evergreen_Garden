# Saurophaganax

## Known species
- *Allosaurus anax*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *Allosaurus “fragilis”*: +20% bleed damage;
- *Allosaurus maximus*: +5% attack damage;

**Size category**: huge;<br/>
**Diet**: meat and bone;<br/>
**Nest materials**: reeds, branches, roots.

## In real life
*Saurophaganax*, formally known as *Allosaurus anax*, is a huge fragmentary species of theropod, possibly the largest member of the *Allosaurus* genus. The original name, *Saurophaganax* is not scientifically supported for the huge theropod anymore because it turned out that the original “Saurophaganax” vertebrae belonged to a sauropod. However, there still was a giant species of allosaurus roaming around in the Morrison formation of Late Jurassic North America.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Allosaurus anax* is a cruel scarlet monarch. The king allosaur holds a niche of a slower, bulkier predator than its lesser cousins. With enough mass to brawl elephant-sized prey onto the ground with its meathook talons, *Saurophaganax* proves correct its name as Lord of the Lizard Eaters. Smaller carnivores are terrified of walking into the desolate canyons that it calls home and whole herds get nervous when a King Allosaur comes down to the watering hole. Yet it has one fatal weakness.
Pride. The cutthroat intraspecific competition proves to be the downfall for most of their kind. In a world of lizard-eaters you are either the lizard, or the eater.


## Roleplaying guidelines
- Prefers to dig out its nest in arid environments as well as dry scrubland;
- Often hold and patrol large territories;
- Violently hate each-other’s presence and are incredibly territorial to other *A. anax* in their range;
- Groups of them begrudgingly work together with distrust and friction, and tolerate one-another only to take down truly gargantuan prey that will satisfy all their immense appetites. Once their quarry falls, however, *A. anax* immediately start squabbling for food and fight. The most dominant individual always fills up first and only then lets others take a bite: there is only room for one king!
- This intraspecific hostility is contrasted with their willingness to keep smaller allosaurs around. A lone *A. anax* often attracts one or two *vassal* allosaurs to lead. The *sovereign* generally acts fair towards their vassals, but may snap and bite to keep them in line;
- T-pose to assert dominance;
- May stuff themselves with tremendous amounts of meat in one sitting;
- Often target and consume other carnivores and on average have an immense parasite load because of that;
- Feel most calm and content when alone: even briefly meeting up for mating is stressful for them;
- Enjoy resting under lonely trees and on scenic rocks;
- Some specimens develop ossicles on their necks to protect them from other *A. anax* teeth in intraspecific combat;
- Is a very athletic and active animal for its size;
- Does not expend energy to roar without there being a reason: usually intimidate by sheer presence;
- In groups *Saurophaganax* become natural-born sauropod-slayers.


## Grouping
- A group of 2 mature individuals is called a pair;
- A group of 3 mature individuals is called a *titanfall*;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group;
- A group of *Saurophaganax* counts as a *giant* target.

## Mixed grouping
- A single *Saurophaganax* may group with 1 or 2 lesser *Allosaurus* (called *vassals*) and follows allosaur hunting limitations when in group with them; such grouping is called a *dominion*;
- Can group with *Rhamphorhynchus*, who are often attracted like crows to the carnage.

## Conflicts
- A lone *Saurophaganax* may hunt or challenge animals of any size;
- A pair may only hunt animals of *huge* size and above together, and for smaller prey only 1 from a pair may engage;
- A titanfall may hunt only *colossal* targets together;
- A *dominion* counts as an *Allosaurus* group of the same size;
- Any group of *Saurophaganax* may challenge or defend themselves together;
- Full cannibals: may hunt their own kin and may even betray and **kill their own group members** when there is no other prey;
- Duel one-another on carcasses for dominance.

## Other interactions
- Will never let scavengers eat from its kill;
- Often uses its impressive size to bully lesser predators off their kills.


## Deviant profile *Sharklord*
- Some *A. anax* find themselves in the company of a *Concavenator* surf;
- The small charcharodontosaurids follow the massive allosaur around initially as scavengers, attracted by the smell of blood;
- The greater carnivore may then develop a further mutualistic relationship with them, starting to go on hunts together…
- The *Saurophaganax* in this relationship is called a *Sharklord* or a *Shark King*;
- A *Sharklord* may group with up to 3 *Concavenators*;
- The group must follow specific criteria to hunt as one unit;
- The group will only hunt together when the *Sharklord* is hungry: the king will not provide for the landsharks;
- The group will only hunt together huge, giant and colossal targets: anything less is not worth the effort;
- If the members wish to hunt smaller prey, it must be hunted by either the Sharklord or the landsharks separately;
- May hunt other *Saurophaganax*, but not other *Concavenators*.
- A surf of *Concavenators* may adopt abandoned *Saurophaganax* babies: the result is a *Sharklord* that believes itself to be a big *Concavenator*.

## Survival tips
- You can build your *Saurophaganax* to be either a self-sufficient hunter, or as a support anchor for your vassals;
- To hunt sauropods in a titanfall utilise bleed and precise strikes;
- Common nickname: sauro;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
