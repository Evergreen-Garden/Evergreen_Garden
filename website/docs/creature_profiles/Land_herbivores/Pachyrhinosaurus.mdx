# Pachyrhinosaurus

- *P. lakustai*;

🦏 **Size category**: huge;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, nuts, roots;
🦴 **Supplementary food**: bones;

## 🇨🇦 In real life
*Pachyrhinosaurus* was a thick-nosed ceratopsid dinosaur from the Late Cretaceous of North America, known from Alberta and Alaska. Unlike its ceratopsid kin, instead of horns *Pachyrhinosaurus* carried a unique bony nasal boss on its face. The northernmost Alaskan species of this genus (*P. perotorum*) lived above the Arctic Circle, being an example of dinosaurs surviving at the poles.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Pachyrhinosaurus* is a dinosaur with a sledgehammer for a face. One of the larger ceratopsians on the island, it is a tough and formidable animal that prefers to face all but the biggest of dangers head on. As stubborn as any other ceratopsid, and even more-so, *Pachyrhinosaurus’* way of dealing with problems is to smash through them.
When not fighting though, it is a calm and gentle creature that marches through the land curiously sniffing out new and interesting things.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Prefers semi-obscured habitats, such as lush scrubland, woodland edge and grass-covered rock fields;
- On Beringia *Pachyrhinosaurus* wander the vast open steppe and rocky highlands;
- Very tolerant to cold temperatures and can survive in the snow;
- Like to nest in forest clearings and in montane forests;
- Colonial nesters: a herd almost always occupies a nesting meadow together;

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Can be found alone, in small family groups or in vast megaherds, especially in colder climates;
- When moving around and foraging, often grunt, sniff and dig;
- When quality vegetation is not available, can use their beaks to crack open tough energy-rich food like nuts and roots and habitually consume bones for calcium;
- Have quite a grumpy character and get agitated quickly;
- This genus has a high degree of morphological variability, with many individuals growing out their skull ornaments in a unique way;
- Enjoy relaxingly bathing in hot springs;
- Partake in play behaviour, especially when young. Juveniles will even attempt to drag other species into their games!
- Adults sometimes go into a rut and become even more aggressive;
- When in a herd, employ a shield wall formation to repel predators;
- Sometimes mix into the herds of smaller ceratopsians, becoming a dominant presence;
- Affectionately huddle together when resting;
- Often curiously approach other herbivores to sniff and inspect them.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 3 mature individuals in a group, called a siege;
- A group of *Pachyrhinosaurus* counts as a *giant* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called calves;
- Sometimes several groups of *Pachyrhinosaurus* can migrate together, but disperse upon facing danger, with only the group that’s getting attacked staying to face the fight;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores.

## ⚡ Aggression
- May challenge large, huge and giant animals;
- Members of a herd can duel each other, mostly preferring to use low-damage fracture-based attacks and shoves for sparring;
- Lone individuals sometimes roam around and bully carnivores out of their territory for the thrill of it.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Your thick skull takes reduced damage;
- Cover your back! It is your most vulnerable point;
- Conserve stamina and use it to punish mistakes made by the enemy;
- Avoid giant carnivores;
- Common nicknames: rhino, pachyrhino;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
