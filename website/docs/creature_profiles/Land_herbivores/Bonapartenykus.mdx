# Bonapartenykus

## 🔎 Known species
- *B. ultimus;*

🐇 **Size category**: small;
🐜 **Diet**: insects, flower nectar, berries;
🥭 **Supplementary food**: fruits, carrion, bones, fish;

## 🇦🇷 In real life
*Bonapartenykus* was a small dinosaur from Late Cretaceous Argentina. It belonged to the family Alvarezsauridae, a bizarre bunch of theropods that had highly reduced arms with only one big claw present. It is the largest known representative of this family. It is hypothesised that Alvarezsaurids could have evolved to be specialised insectivores.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Bonapartenykus* fills a highly unique ecological role. It uses a combination of morphological traits like a long sticky tongue, stubby digging claws and sensitive hearing to open up nests of eusocial insects like ants ant termites and forage on them. This silky-feathered agile animal is also known to use its long tongue to feed on energy-rich flower nectar, spreading pollen in the process.
 They are more active during the night hours, using their highly acute senses to explore deserts, plains and forests of Gondwa under the starlight.

## ⛰️ Habitat
- Are highly tolerant to extremely arid conditions and can be excellent desert survivors, although, they can live elsewhere just as well;
- Prefers to nest in places with an abundance of eusocial insects. Termite mounds or anthills in the area almost always mean that there’s *Bonapartenykus* living nearby;
- More sugar-inclined specimens can nest in places with abundant flowers, serving an important role in pollinating nocturnal flowering plants;


## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Head plumage is often subject to high individual variation in *Bonapartenykus*, with males oftentimes having more elaborate face feathers than the more modest females. This is not a hard rule, but merely a statistic;
- Find both corpse flowers and flies that are attracted to them delicious;
- Often fish out and eat maggots from rotting carrion;
- Highly dislike water, instead fulfilling their hygienic needs by dust-bathing and anting (rolling in ants and their acid for disinfection);
- Tend to be more bold, active and vocal at night, dancing and calling to each-other in the dark;
- Their poisonous saliva is the result of accumulating formic acid in the mouth from eating ants;
- Their nests often attract a species of butterfly who’s caterpillars feed of the dinosaur’s fallen downy feathers and pupate among them;
- Use their own tail feathers like a blanket when curling into a sleeping position;
- Often follow larger herbivores around to eat arthropods spooked by the bigger animals;
- Often find themselves in a mutualistic relationship with other herbivores, picking skin parasites in exchange for protection;
- For some reason hate snakes and kill them on sight.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 12 individuals in a group, called a marathon;
- A group of *Bonapartenykus* counts as a *medium* target;
- Immature individuals are called floofs;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- Especially love the company of lone sauropods and therizinosaurs;
- **Collateral damage**: don’t count as a body down in mixed groups unless specifically being a target of a hunt.

## 🦂 Hunting
- Are known to occasionally hunt *Pulmonoscorpius* critters, finding them delicious, apparently.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of up to medium size;
- Usually run from danger, but switch to violence when defending their young;
- Tend to be more inclined to fight in self-defence during the night hours.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- As a *Bonapartenykus* you have incredible running stamina and excellent agility;
- Your unique diet lets you avoid competition with most animals;
- Common nickname: bona;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️ (easy)

## 🦤 Roleplaying inspirations

- Hummingbirds, roadrunners and the kiwi bird;
- Jerboas, tree shrews.
