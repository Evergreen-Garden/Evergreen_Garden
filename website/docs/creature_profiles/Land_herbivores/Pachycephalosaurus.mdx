# Pachycephalosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *P. wyomingensis*: +15% attack knockback;

## 🔍 Related genera
- *Dracorex hogwartsia*: +25% jump height;
- *Stygimoloch spinifer*: +3% attack damage;

**🦌 Size category**: medium;
**🌿 Diet**: shrubs, fruit;
**🦴 Supplementary food**: carrion, bones, *Platyhystrix*;


## 🇺🇸 In real life
*Pachycephalosaurus* was a dome-skulled dinosaur from the Late Cretaceous of North America. It was the largest among its thick-headed kin, the pachycephalosaurs. Skull lesions found on fossil remains heavily suggest that pachycephalosaurs used their weaponised craniums to headbutt each-other in intraspecific combat.
There exists controversy if animals like *Stygimoloch* and *Dracorex* represent separate genera or are they just immature specimens of *Pachycephalosaurus*, but in game they are represented as separate species.

## In the Garden
*Pachycephalosaurus* is a rambunctious and fighty herbivore. It is an active, curious and bold animal that enjoys scaling the most difficult terrain. Sometimes, it can be too curious for its own good, getting into trouble with other dinosaurs or hopping on a rock that it can’t safely get off from. But what’s a few broken bones for a thick-headed lizard?


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Enjoy difficult terrain;
- Prefer to nest in rocky areas and on cliffs next to deadly drops;
- Colonial nesters: herd members often nest as a group;

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Are very bold and playful animals, often being loud and jumping on obstacles for fun;
- Often investigate the commotion in the area, poking their noses into other’s busyness; 
- Fearless and not easily startled;
- Due to having simple teeth, are restricted to selectively browsing soft vegetation, but can supplement their diet with carrion and bones;
- When it comes to being hunted, *Pachycephalosaurus* is rather defensive and ready to fight back;
- If grouped with larger herbivores, can sometimes headbutt them for fun and run away;
- Juveniles will often train their strength by head-butting their older relatives;
- Do not like to share resources such as bushes with other herbivores;
- Often display reckless behavior.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have a maximum of 6 mature individuals in a group called a clobber;
- A group of *Pachycephalosaurus* counts as a *large* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called bonklets.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores.

## 🐸 Hunting
- *Pachycephalosaurus* find the mind-altering effects of *Platyhystrix* poison enjoyable, so they sometimes deliberately dig them out of their burrows and consume their meat to experience unusual sensations.
- Often bump trees, group members and even invisible enemies after being blissfully intoxicated.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of up to *large* size;
- Members of the clobber often engage in duels using *charged headbutt*: whoever gets broken bones first, loses! Don’t worry, they heal back… most of the time. These duels can be a good way to determine the herd leader if everyone is fine with that;
- *Pachycephalosaurus* has a bad temper and is quick to bonk any animals trespassing its personal space.


## 👹 Deviant profile *the Junkie*
- Some *Pachycephalosaurus* develop an unhealthy addiction to the psychoactive poison of *Platyhystrix*;
- Constantly being either intoxicated or suffering from withdrawls, they walk around in a state of aggressive delirium, seeing ghosts and hearing phantom noises;
- The *Junkie* may attack animals of up to *large* size without warning like a predator on the hunt;
- If the *Junkie* makes a hit, it must stay on the body until it decays to at least 80%;
- The *Junkie* is recommended use the “*scavenger*” metabolism to represent its addiction, but may switch to “*herbivore*” when in dire need of food;
- The *Junkie* cannot group with any other herbivore species because it is shunned for its narcomaniac tendencies;
- *Junkies* can form a group of up to 3 animals called a *Joint*;
- The *Junkie* often acts stupid and attacks inanimate objects.

## ☂️ Survival tips
- As a fast and endurant runner, you can outpace most dangers of Gondwa if you manage your stamina;
- *Pachycephalosaurus* is a good jumper and can use terrain to escape predation;
- You have a variety of different attacks in your arsenal that deal daze, fracture and knockback: use them to disorient your opponent to either escape or give you the upper hand in a fight;
- You can use *dodge* to jump out of the way of incoming attacks!
- Common nickname: pachy;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
