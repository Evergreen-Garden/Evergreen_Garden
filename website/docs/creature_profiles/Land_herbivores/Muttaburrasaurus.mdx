## 🇦🇺 In real life
*Muttaburrasaurus* is a famous dinosaur from Australia! It was found near Queensland in Early Cretaceous rocks. *Muttaburrasaurus* was a herbivore and an ornithopod, a distant relative of iguanodonts and hadrosaurs. Interestingly, recent studies have shown support for the hypothesis that *Muttaburrasaurus* was related to the members of the mysterious *Elasmaria*: a group of poorly understood ornithopods endemic to the southern continents of the Cretaceous.
The most famous trait of *Muttaburrasaurus* is its bulbous nose that may have supported expansive, possibly inflatable nasal chambers.

## In the Garden
*Muttaburrasaurus* loves to get its big nose into trouble. Curious and adventurous, they migrate across the land, consuming all vegetation in their path. Noisy and inquisitive, they often bring commotion wherever they travel, with splashing of mud and dust clouds in their wake.
When danger strikes, however, the cacophonous mob can quickly coordinate themselves to flee or fight - what appears as thoughtless honking from the outside is an entire world of complex social signals for a *Muttaburrasaurus*.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- **Woodland**: *Muttaburrasaurus* feels most at home among the splendour of forests;
- **Grassland**: herds of *Muttaburrasaurus* often come to open prairie to graze on ground vegetation;
- **Wetland**: *Muttaburrasaurus* enjoy spending time in muddy and boggy habitats, splashing in water and mudbathing to get rid of pesky insects.

## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- *Muttaburrasaurus* are rather social animals and love company. It is common for them to travel in noisy herds, systematically stripping the landscape of vegetation;
- Constant vocal communication is very important for groups of *Muttaburrasaurus*: proper group coordination is invaluable for the survival of the herd;
- Some individuals, mostly males, love to posture and mock-fight within the herd;
- Courting consists of ritualised display involving nasal bellowing;
- *Muttaburrasaurus* are a curious kind, and often gather around unfamiliar animals to sniff them;
- *Muttaburrasaurus* is very migratory and the herd rarely stays in one place for long.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 4 individuals in a group, called a mob or a moot;
- A group of *Muttaburrasaurus* counts as a *huge* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed-species grouping
- Can group with any *small*-sized herbivores;
- **Ornithopod Orchestra**: can group with *Lambeosaurus*, *Lurdusaurus*, *Iguanodon*, *Barsboldia* and *Parasaurolophus*;
- A lone *Muttaburrasaurus* may join any herbivores in a group (see deviant profile).

## 🪺 Reproduction
- **Nest materials**: rock, mud;
- Prefer to make nests among soft ferns;
- Young *Muttaburrasaurus* are called muttonchops;
- *Muttaburrasaurus* are diligent and responsible parents;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.

## 🌳 Claiming food
- If under 75% hunger, may challenge any feeding herbivores away from a berry bush. Once the defeated party flees the vicinity of the bush, the contest is over, and the victor must eat up.


## 👹 Deviant profile *The Ringer*
- *Muttaburrasaurus* have sharp senses. Their massive noses are able to easily pick up the scent of danger, and their deep bellows can alert other herbivores in the area;
- Because of this, sometimes a lone **Muttaburrasaurus** collects an entourage of different herbivore species following it around to utilise it as a sentry and a sentinel for the herd;
- Such individuals are known as *Ringers*, shepherds of the bushlands;
- *Ringers* can oftentimes coordinate the herd with their vocalisations, inspiring death-defying courage or signalling a swift retreat; Even larger herbivores respect the *Ringer*;
- A lone *Ringer* may join a group of any herbivore species;
- There can only be one *Ringer* in a group;
- A *Ringer* can coexist with a *Troubadour Lambeosaurus* in the same group, each providing different utilities.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Muttaburrasaurus* is deceptively bulky for its size;
- *Muttaburrasaurus* turns quite clumsily, but makes up for it with good speed and stamina;
- You can use a variety of vocal abilities to buff yourself or your allies;
- Common nickname: mutta;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
