# Therizinosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *T. cheloniformis*;

🐘 **Size category**: giant;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, insects.

## 🇲🇳 In real life
Originally identified as a turtle in 1954, we can assure you that *Therizinosaurus* is no turtle and is actually an extremely large herbivorous theropod from the Late Cretaceous of Asia. Found in the Nemegt Formation, this animal was found alongside other herbivores such as *Barsboldia* and *Deinocheirus*, as well as carnivores like *Alioramus* and *Tarbosaurus*. It is most famously known for its impressively built forearms tipped with three gigantic unguals (the largest of any land animal!) combined with a long neck, traits that certainly made this animal a unique sight in the somewhat arid environment it inhabited. While Science cannot currently decide on the purpose of Therizinosaurus's claws, we in the Garden believe it likely served as a tool with multiple purposes.

## In the Garden
Roaming the Garden, Therizinosaurus is one of the more extravagant members of the local fauna. It is a giant theropod with terrible claws… That eats leaves!
Its loud screeches combined with a usually fearless attitude mean that this is one herbivore you will be unlikely to find scurrying away from danger. Standing as tall as the trees, it has an appearance of a lumbering mound of shaggy feathers and possesses an easily irritable character. The reaper lizard’s claws may not be specialised for combat, but can still be incredibly deadly weapons that it will not hesitate to use.
Therizinosaurus will strike first, and ask questions later, if at all. Fear the reaper!


### ⛰️ Habitat
- Prefers to roam deep woodland during the day, wading through dense vegetation with its long claws;
- Often wanders out into open plains and deserts during the night to dig up termite mounds and anthills for extra protein;
- Prefers to nest in forests.

### 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Is a picky and selective browser of leaves, but enjoys supplemental protein in form of eusocial insects;
- Prefer to be quiet during the day and screech at the night sky;
- Often mark their territory by scraping trees and termite mounds;
- Often set up a *lek* in a forest clearing to build an elaborate nest out of roots and attract mates;
- During the mating season, both sexes of *Therizinosaurus* often molt brightly-colored feathers, displaying them for potential partners and competitors alike, all the while stomping their feet and flaring their claws in an elaborite dance reminiscent of their smaller cousins, *Citipati*;
- *Therizinosaurus* displays a hight percentage of same-sex couples: it cares not for what is between your legs, only that your feathers are bright and your claws in good shape.

## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 2 mature individuals in a group, called a pair;
- A pair of *Therizinosaurus* counts as a *colossal* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fir in a group, called flufflings;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any land herbivores;
- Same-sex pairs of *Therizinosaurus* will adopt all sorts of baby herbivores.

## ⚡ Aggression
- During the day may challenge animals of huge, giant and colossal size;
- During the night and poor weather a **lone** individual may attack animals of huge, giant and colossal size without warning;
- Lone *Therizinosaurus* are known to be very territorial and ill-tempered to most creatures;
- May defend their nesting grounds with ferocity;
- Perhaps a remnant of its theropod ancestry, Therizinosaurus is one of the few herbivores that will stake out a territory, only allowing other members of its species and smaller animals access when mating season arrives;
- Are extremely protective of their young (both hatched and adopted).


## 👹 Deviant profile *The Lovers*
- Some *Therizinosaurus* individuals develop an unbreakable monogamous life-long pair-bond;
- Such individuals, simply known as *The Lovers* are devoted to one-another, being able to recognise their partner even after spending years apart;
- When roaming together, *The Lovers* often subtly communicate and preen each-other, showing affection. They are content, so *The Lovers* do not display the usual short temper of their species, being calm around other animals (but will still defend their personal space);
- When one of the *Lovers* dies in conflict, the other one goes into a blind grieving frenzy and __**may ignore the body down rule**__ to avenge its fallen partner. This makes dealing with such a pair very dangerous for any predator: even in death they won’t leave each-other’s side;
- If one of the *Lovers* survives the death of the other, they may spend months or years wandering alone, being in distress and attacking innocent dinosaurs to spend their fury. Sometimes they even keep the bones of their perished love in their nest as a memento. Eventually, they may settle down for another and start anew: life always finds a way.
- *The Lovers* may only group with one-another and their offspring (born or adopted), nothing else is allowed in the family.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- You have very high damage, but not a lot of health!
- Do not “facetank” other giant animals, use your mobility;
- Even as flufflings *Therizinosaurus* are surprisingly dangerous;
- You are a very poor swimmer;
- Common nickname: theri;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (challenging)
