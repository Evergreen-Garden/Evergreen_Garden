# Barsboldia

## 🔎 Known species
- *B. sicinskii*: +10% health recovery;
- *B. gibbosi*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *B. dorsum*: +10% venom and bleed healing;

🐘 **Size category**: giant;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation;

## 🇲🇳 In real life
*Barsboldia* was a “duck-billed” dinosaur that lived during the Late Cretaceous in what is now Mongolia. It belonged to the hadrosaur family and was relatives with other Cretaceous giants such as *Edmontosaurus* and *Shantungosaurus*. Like other members of its family, *Barsboldia* was an efficient herbivore that could chew up vegetation with the help of complex teeth batteries.
*Barsboldia* shared its habitat with other massive herbivores such as *Deinocheirus* and *Therizinosaurus*, as well as terrible tyrannosaurs like *Tabrosaurus* and *Alioramus*.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Barsboldia* is a towering wanderer of the desert. A giant among hadrosaur kin, it roams the desolate landscapes, grinding any available vegetation using its beak and sophisticated jaws. Despite not having any apparent weapons, this giant nomad has enough sheer bulk to crush most of the threats it could encounter on its long journeys. Do not underestimate the hadrosaur!
It is masterfully adapted to dry conditions and serves as an important keystone species of desert ecosystems.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- *Barsboldia* is most commonly found in the arid desert;
- Prefer to nest in spots shaded by desert trees and rock shelters;
- Many unrelated *Barsboldia* may come together to nest in a colony;
- Can tolerate cold climates as well, being very prominent in the steppes of *Beringia*.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Rear up on hind legs when trying to get a look of their surroundings and to intimidate others;
- *Barsboldia* uses its large nasal cavities to cool hot air and condense airborne moisture;
- Use tall neural spines above the hip as a hump to store fat reserves;
- Don’t stick in one place for long, undertaking long migrations and stripping all vegetation along the way;
- Can eat and drink tremendous amounts of food and water in one go, depleting an area from resources;
- Can use the constellations in the night sky to navigate in their journeys;
- Communicate by honking, bellowing and inflating the skin over their nasal chambers;
- Despite being unbothered most of the time, sometimes have fits of bad temper and become a problem for everyone else;
- Barsboldia nests can become little green oasises amidst the arid landscape, fertilised by the abundance of dung;
- Like other hadrosaurs, *Barsboldia* is a gentle and attentive parent;
- Let smaller animals take rest in their shadow;
- Have an incredibly keen sense of smell, and other herbivores sometimes follow *Barsboldia* who can sniff out the freshest greenery.

## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 2 mature individuals in a group, called a pair;
- A pair of *Barsboldia* counts as a *colossal* target;
- Can have unlimited immature individuals in a group, called calves;
- Easily form *caravans* with other ungrouped *Barsboldia* for traversing the desert together. Naturally, when predators strike, the caravan will disperse, and individuals will protect only their own group;
- One individual in a caravan usually serves as a *pathfinder* to locate food by smell and lead the whole caravan to it.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- Like the company of other hadrosaurs and oviraptorids.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of huge, giant and colossal size;
- If resources are scarce, may become territorial towards other herbivores;
- An irritated *Barsboldia* may get very sensitive about its personal space.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- You have a variety of abilities that focus on knockback and area of effect to control the battlefield and zone out opponents;
- Your survival calls let you more easily sustain yourself with food, and are invaluable in a big group;
- Combat calls make *Barsboldia* a menace in battle;
- Always manage your stamina. If your stamina runs out, you are defenceless;
- Common nicknames: bars;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (challenging)
