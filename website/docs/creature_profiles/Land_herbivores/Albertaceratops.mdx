# Albertaceratops

## 🔎 Known species
- *A. nesmoi*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *A. taurus*: +5% armor;
- *A. crinitus*: +3% attack damage;

🦓 **Size category**: large;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs and fruit, ground vegetation;

## 🇨🇦 In real life
*Albertaceratops* is a herbivorous horned dinosaur known from the Late Cretaceous of what is now Canada. It belonged to *Ceratopsidae*, a family of other horned dinosaurs that produced a stunning diversity of forms in the Late Cretaceous North America. *Albertaceratops* in particular possessed large brow horns, a bony nose ridge and two distinct hook-shaped frill-spikes.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Albertaceratops* is a powerful woodland beast, a grove guardian. Slow and bulky, it relies on its heavy physique to deter predators, and is never is a rush or a hurry. A stubborn fighter, it rarely backs away from danger, facing any enemies head on. Despite their willingness to fiercely protect their home forests, *Albertaceratops* are gentle beasts that enjoy the comfort of a herd and are good parents.
However, you should never test their patience. Demons run when a good beast goes to war.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Lone *Albertaceratops* are incredibly paranoid and careful creatures that hide in dense forests, canyons and other obscured habitats;
- When forming a herd, however, they become much bolder and go to forage in open plains;
- Prefer to nest in most hidden and protected parts of the forest, in deep sheltered canyons and in sunlit open groves;
- An *Albertaceratops* can pick a forest or a grove as its home territory and zealously protect it against predators and competitors.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Display gentle relaxed calmness on sunny days, but become nervous and easily spooked during the night and bad weather, when visibility is reduced. A spooked *Albertaceratops* is a dangerous *Albertaceratops*;
-  In a herd, the oldest females, called *Babushkas*, typically lead the way, with males and younger females serving as protectors of the young;
- If caught in the open, *Albertaceratops* can crash into the forest line to face the danger in familiar terrain;
- Regard acorns as a delicacy;
- Are incredibly stubborn fighters: it’s not the size of the ceratopsian in the fight that matters, but the size of the fight in a ceratopsian.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 4 mature individuals in a group, called a vanguard;
- A group of *Albertaceratops* counts as a *huge* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called calves;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge medium, large and huge animals to a territorial fight;
- Can be very aggressive if frightened and quickly turn to violence if the other animal doesn’t respond to warning calls and leave their personal space. If you poke the bull, expect to get the horns;
- When raising young, can charge any predators that get too close. If you look at *Albertaceratops’* children for too long, expect to get the horns;
- Members of a vanguard often lock horns in duels, but almost never try to harm one-another seriously, sparring mostly for play or show.


## 👹 Deviant profile *the Berserker* a.k.a *the dagga boy*
- Some old *Albertaceratops* individuals, both male and female, become kicked out of the herd or fall behind, due to age, physical condition or just plain old bad temper. These old-timers become increasingly more territorial and grumpy even by their species standards. Such individuals, called *dagga boys* or *dagga girls* are very unpredictable, often attack unprovoked and seek to satisfy their boiling blood by goring anything they come across;
- The *berserkers* often inhabit desolate places and wastelands and patrol a territory;
- They can attack both carnivores and herbivores without warning, acting like a predator on the hunt;
- A *berserker* can attack medium, large and huge animals. It typically ignores small animals and doesn’t mess with giants;
- A *berserker* is always a loner and can’t act friendly or group with other herbivores;
- The exception to this are deviant *Psittacosaurus doomriders* that can ally themselves with the mad ceratopsian and aid it in its conquest in exchange for scavenging the victims;
- A *berserker* can have up to 4 *doomriders* in a group with itself;
- A nest of a *berserker* is often littered with morbid trophies of war;
- These grumps often harass and kill critters to make them shut up.


## ☂️ Survival tips:
- Your head takes reduced damage as in all ceratopsids;
- You can lean into a more defensive style of play using block and parry, or go all in with the unhinged aggression by foregoing parry altogether;
- *Albertaceratops* is a slow herbivore, so always be on the lookout for danger to be able to hide or escape in time;
- Moving between areas discretely and under the cover of shrubbery can help avoid deadly predators a lot: *Albertaceratops* is quite vulnerable in the open due to its slow speed;
- Both the back and the front of Albertaceratops are deadly!
- *Albertaceratops* is very compact and maneuverable for its size, which helps in confined spaces;
- Common nicknames: alb, albert;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (challenging)
