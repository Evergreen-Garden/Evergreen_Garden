# Kentrosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *K. aethiopicus*: +5% bleed damage;
- *K. multiscornus*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *K. parvusplectrum*: +20% spikes damage;

🦌 **Size category**: medium;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation;

## 🇹🇿 In real life
Kentrosaurus is a stegosaurid dinosaur known from the Late Jurassic Tendaguru Formation of Tanzania with it being a costal region with dense foliage. It was one of the smaller members of its family, and had differed from the more famous Stegosaurus by having spikes not only on the tip of the tail, but along the whole length of the tail, above the hips and on its shoulders. most specimens of this animal are currently in German collections.

## In The Garden
In the Garden, *Kentrosaurus* is an angry, grumpy ball of spikes. In an ecosystem full of dangerous predators, this tiny-brained herbivore survives by being a walking morning-star mace with an unpredictable temperament to match its prickly exterior. Do not mess with it, do not touch it, those spikes are not for show.
They roam the forests, deserts and scrublands of the island in groups and families, nipping at the vegetation and defiantly spitting on most things that looked funny their way.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Most commonly found in scrubland and forest;
- Prefer to nest in deep shrubbery, often digging out a nest in between gnarly roots;
- A thin beaked snout betrays a selective low browser in *Kentrosaurus*, snipping away at a buffet of forest and arid plants, not really being suited for grass-dominated biomes;

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Often roll around the scenery in groups, but don’t depend on a social unit and feel just as content alone;
- Have a highly bizarre habit of collecting bones and dragging them into their nest;
- Not easily spooked, but if you manage to startle a *Kentrosaurus*, expect being spat on;
- Possess the ability to gradually regenerate lost osteoderm-derived spikes on their tail, which is not seen in other stegosaurids. The mechanism at work is similar to the antler regrowing process in deer;
- *Kentrosaurus* are incredibly short-tempered and go into defensive mode if they see any dangerous animal in their space;
- Will often pick fights with various animals. The reason being “you looked at me the wrong way, mate”;
- Despite being very aggressive to most species, *Kentrosaurus* somewhat respect the company of *Miragaia*, as they can coexist without competing for food, exhibiting niche partitioning with being low browsers and high browsers respectively.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 6 mature individuals in a group, called a prickle;
- A group of *Kentrosaurus* counts as a *large* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called steglings;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of up to large size;
- Can initiate contests using spit;
- They are very quick to stab interlopers that don’t heed warning calls. Stabbing makes most problems go away;
- If anything enters the personal space of 1 body length, *Kentrosaurus* may strike without warning, as its pea-sized brain doesn’t comprehend long-term plans;


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Kentrosaurus* has a very wide turning circle, so always work to keep your tail facing towards the enemy!
- Sometimes you don’t need to stick around to kill your opponent, as the massive bleed damage is enough to stop a predator from chasing you, giving you time to escape to safety;
- Bleed damage increases the more your opponent moves around, so make them run and chase!
- The directional tail attacks can take some time to get used to;
- Common nickname: kentro;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
