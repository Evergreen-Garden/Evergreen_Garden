# Peloroplites

## 🔎 Known species
- *P. cedrimontanus*

## 🔍 Related genera
- *Edmontonia longiceps*;
- *Borealopelta markmitchelli*.

🦏 **Size category**: huge;
🕑 **Passive growth time**: 3 hours;
🔷 **Group size**: 1-3

## 🍽️ Diets
- **Herbivore**: leaves, fruit;
- **Forager**: flowers, nuts, roots, fruit.


## 🇺🇸 In real life
*Peloroplites* was a nodosaurid ankylosaur that lived in the Late Cretaceous of western North America. It was a close relative of the more famous *Polacanthus*.
Like other nodosaurids, it was a low-slung, four-legged herbivore built around protection rather than speed, with bony armor embedded in the skin and prominent shoulder spines that likely made it difficult for large predators to get a clean, safe bite.
*Peloroplites* and other nodosaurids differed from their relatives Ankylosaurids by not have a tail club. Their defenses were concentrated along the back, flanks, and shoulders: a broad, rigid body, dense osteoderms and spikes positioned to punish anything that tried to grapple from the side. *Peloroplites* likely fed by browsing low vegetation - shrubs, ferns, and other ground plants - moving steadily between patches of food and relying on its armor and bulk when threatened.

## In the Garden
*Peloroplites* is a grumpy, but simple fella. All it wants to do is to peacefully eat plants and live its life. But there’s just so much noise and irritation… Creatures existing around. *Breating*. Disgusting. Get off my lawn, punks!
In response to irritation, *Peloroplites* does not posture for long, nor does it wait to be tested repeatedly. Retaliation is immediate and physical: a shift of weight, a turn of the shoulders, and a heavy swing of the armored tail. A perfect living shield, this ankylosaur is a wide mass of armor and spikes born to break theropod teeth.
Any attempt to circle or press close is met with abrupt correction; shoves, checks, and sweeping turns that force attackers to reposition or disengage. The longer an interaction continues, the more costly it becomes for whatever chose to remain nearby. 
*Peloroplites* absorbs punishment without spectacle and answers pressure with pressure, until the exchange ends through withdrawal rather than defeat.
Attacks glance, scrape, and bleed; momentum falters; stamina drains. 
You do not lose to *Peloroplites* because it outchased you, outplayed you, or surprised you.
You lose because you stayed too long, pushed too hard, and ignored the moment when disengaging was still an option.
*Peloroplites* has truly turned unbreakable defence into unstoppable offence.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- **Scrubland**: *Peloroplites* is perfectly adapted to forage on ground plants in dusty semi-arid scrublands;
- **Woodland**: forests present a feast for the armored beast;
- **Coastal**: sometimes *Peloroplites* will visit coastlines to wash off dust and get salt.

## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- *Peloroplites* very often spar, roughhouse and push each-other around when in a group, honing their fighting skills;
- Prefer environment with natural defensive features, such as rock fields and canyons;
- Feel deep comfort in settling into a stable territory with plenty of food;
- *Peloroplites* is reactive rather than aggressive; it answers intrusion but does not seek out conflict;
- Favor brawling over pursuit; it quickly loses interest in a chase;
- Defend personal space decisively and aggressively once approached within close distance;
- Disengaging opponents are rarely pursued far, reclaiming space is the goal, not securing kills;
- Hold ground during confrontations, forcing others to reposition rather than rushing them;
- Retreat only when sustained pressure makes holding position impossible, and do so deliberately;
- Injuries increase stubbornness rather than panic; wounded individuals may become more confrontational;
- Repeated disturbances in the same area should provoke faster and stronger reactions over time.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 3 individuals in a group, called a stand;
- A group of *Peloroplites* counts as a *giant* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed-species grouping
- Can group with any *small*-sized herbivores;
- **Armoured alliance**: can group with *Anodontosaurus* and *Desmatosuchus*;
- **Pilgrims of Cretaceous**: can group with *Iguanodon*;
- Can include 1 deviant *Troubadour Lambeosaurus* in a group.

## 🪺 Reproduction
- **Nest materials**: rock, reeds, roots;
- Prefer to hide their nests in dense brush;
- Young *Peloroplites* are called bucklers;
- *Peloroplites* give minimal care to their young and don’t share a deep connection with their offspring;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.


## 🌳 Claiming food
- If under 75% hunger, may challenge any feeding herbivores away from a berry bush. Once the defeated party flees the vicinity of the bush, the contest is over, and the victor must eat up.

## 🚩 Claiming territory
- *Peloroplites* may claim any POI as its *lawn* by constructing a nest;
- While foraging on its *lawn*, *Peloroplites* may challenge *any* creatures to leave or fight;
- It also takes claim of every edible bush that grows on its *lawn* and can defend them from any herbivores;
- *Peloroplites* may strike any creatures that don’t respect its personal space, no warning needed;
- If the nest is destroyed, the territory is considered lost, and *Peloroplites* must exile itself from the POI for at least 15 minutes.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Peloroplites* has an insanely good turn-in-place;
- Hold ground rather than chase; distance regained is safer than distance gained;
- Use terrain to limit approaches and prevent being surrounded;
- Common nickname: Pelo
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)

## 🐢 Roleplaying inspirations

- The sudden temper of a rhinoceros;
- The planted, immovable stance of a musk ox.
