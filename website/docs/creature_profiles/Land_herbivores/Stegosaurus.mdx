# Stegosaurus

- *S. stenops*: +3% attack damage;
- *S. ungulatus*: +5% bleed damage;
- *S. sulcatus*: +5% health;

🦏 **Size category**: huge;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation;

## 🇺🇸 In real life
*Stegosaurus* is an iconic dinosaur that lived in the Late Jurassic North America and possibly some other areas around the world. Most famous representative of its family, this animal has an eye-catching appearance with alternating spinal plates and a set of four tail spikes sometimes menacingly called a “thagomizer”. *Stegosaurus* coexisted with other Jurassic celebrities such as *Apatosaurus*, *Camptosaurus*, *Allosaurus*, *Ceratosaurus* and *Torvosaurus*. There exists fossil evidence that highly suggests that several known *Allosaurus* individuals suffered trauma related to *Stegosaurus* tail spikes.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Stegosaurus* is a dangerous and unpredictable herbivore. An animal with an enormous hulking body and a tiny brain, it doesn’t think twice where its thagomizer swings, only that it does. A *Stegosaurus* spends its day eating and patrolling its home territory to drive away any unwanted interlopers. All it wants is a simple life undisturbed by any annoying neighbours.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- *Stegosaurus* is a very territorial animal and often chooses a home range that it patrols and fiercely defends, preferring scrubland and prairie;
- Prefer to nest in dry scrub.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Usually don’t like members of their own species and are reluctant to group;
- Often shake their plates at each other upon meeting to show tolerance;
- Like to sunbathe in the morning;
- Some individuals build elaborate bizarrely symmetrical structures around their nests, even employing bloodstone crystals as decoration. This serves to attract a mate, with the most complex nests being most popular;
- *Stego Mountain* often attracts *Stegosaurus* for enigmatic reasons. They act bizarrely agitated around the mountain, screaming into the night sky;
- Sometimes congregate together in one sunning spot, but act grumpy because they all hate each-other;
- Like the company of smaller animals, but don’t like other huge and giant herbivores around;
- *Stegosaurus hates* when other creatures make excessive noise and will react with violence.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 3 mature individuals in a group, called a battlement;
- A group of *Stegosaurus* counts as *giant* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called steglings or *kebabs*;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- Like to employ *Camptosaurus* as sentinels.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of large, huge and giant size for a territorial battle;
- Can exhibit incredible hostility when defending their home range: get close, get thagomized;
- Can swing their tail without warning at anything that gets into the personal space of a *Stegosaurus*. The brain is too small to distinguish friend from foe.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Stegosaurus* is one of the easiest dinosaurs to control in a fight;
- *Stegosaurus* is incredibly effective in defensive combat;
- Use quick tail swings against more nimble opponents;
- Use charged tail attack against slower foes;
- Common nickname: stego;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
