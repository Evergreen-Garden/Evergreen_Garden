# Struthiomimus

## 🔎 Known species
- *S. altus*: +50% acceleration speed;
- *S. plumas*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *S. calvitius*: +25% jump height;

🐇 **Size category**: small;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, ground vegetation, insects;

## 🇨🇦 In real life
*Struthiomimus* was a genus of ornithomimid from the late Cretaceous, in what is now the Oldman formation in Dinosaur Provincial Park in Alberta, Canada. This animal was fairly standard for the clade, with a small beaked skull perched on a long, thin neck with similarly lithe back limbs. This lightly built animal was contemporaneous with dinosaurs like *Albertaceratops*, *Parasaurolophus*, and *Daspletosaurus.*

## In the Garden
Due to their flexible diet and mostly unmatched speed and agility *Struthiomimus* has become a fairly ubiquitous animal across Garden’s grasslands and savannas. Struthiomimus are both skittish and curious creatures, and at times they also seem somewhat air-headed, a flock is just as likely to run from a Parasaurolophus’ sneeze as they are to watch a creature be dragged into the depths by a crocodylomorph without flinching. This mix of fear and curiosity has also led to fascinating interspecific interactions, both with similarly sized herbivores and larger genera. They make for excellent scouts in mixed herds!


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Prefer to roam and forage in flat open habitats like grasslands, scrublands and deserts;
- Ground nesters: prefer to construct nests on the ground in open habitats, using bushes for cover the nest from view;
- Colonial nesters: a flock often nests together for safety. More eyes to look for danger!

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Very inquisitive and curious, often follow other creatures around and observe them;
- Very vocal and noisy animals, their squawks, chirps, chatter, and hisses are often heard before they are seen.
- Often startled by sudden loud noise or quick movements, especially from creatures outside of their flock. If something startles one flock member, the entire flock is apt to scatter, screaming their heads off;
- Mostly eat plants, but often use their powerful legs to dig away at the nests of eusocial insects to consume them as a source of supplemental protein;
- Enjoy taking baths in shallow puddles to cool off in the heat of the day;
- While built to run, *Struthiomimus* are no strangers to conflict, and will actively defend themselves if their numbers are sufficient. Parents can get especially fierce when defending their chicks. Powerful legs deliver powerful kicks;
- Have an irritable temperament and sometimes threaten, annoy and harass random juvenile carnivores;
-  If a member of the flock notices any unusual activity in the area they will usually inform the rest of the group, who will gather and observe the commotion from a safe distance. It is not uncommon to see a few Struthiomimus watching a hunt or territorial dispute from atop a nearby hill;
- Groups sometimes walk in circles around dead animals for unknown reasons;
- Scream and run in panic during thunderstorms;
- Court each-other by presenting gifts like sticks and rocks. Males tend to take most of the care of the offspring;
- Love to join flocks with *Citipati*.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 12 individuals in a group, called a flock;
- A group of *Struthiomimus* counts as a *medium* target;
- Immature individuals are called chicks.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores, serving as scouts in exchange for protection;
- **Collateral damage**: don’t count as a body down in mixed groups unless specifically being a target of a hunt.

## 🦂 Hunting
- Can hunt *Pulmonoscorpius* critters for food. Scorpion legs are delicious.

## ⚡ Agression
- Can challenge animals of up to medium size for resources and territory;
- Often kick to death random critters because their noises startled them;
- Individuals in a flock often bicker and duel for seemingly no reason;


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Speed and agility are your greatest asset as a *Struthiomimus.* While you cannot directly fight a lot of Gondwa’s threats alone, you can certainly out speed or out maneuver them;
- Even as an adult there are still a plethora of hostile critters that see you as something worth bullying, so watch your back;
- You are a terrible swimmer;
- Common nickname: struthi;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️ (easy)

## 🦃 Roleplaying inspiration
