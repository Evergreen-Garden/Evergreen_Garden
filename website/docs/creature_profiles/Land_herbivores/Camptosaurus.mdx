# Camptosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *C. dispar*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *C. conditus*: +25% jump height;
- *C. latas*: +5% armor;

🐇 **Size category**: small;
🌿 **Diet**: ground vegetation, fruit, shrubs;

## 🇺🇸 In real life
*Camptosaurus* was an iguanodontian ornithopod that was part of the famous Morrison Formation biota in the Late Jurassic of North America. It was a bipedal low-browsing herbivore with a robust torso and strong legs adapted for fast locomotion. Like its more derived *Hadrosauriform* relatives, it was found with chewing teeth with signs of extensive wear, indicating a diet of tough ground vegetation.
*Camptosaurus* coexisted with famous herbivores like *Stegosaurus* and *Apatosaurus* and had to avoid predators like *Ceratosaurus*, *Allosaurus* and *Torvosaurus*.

## In the Garden
*Camptosaurus* is a fast-running evasive herbivore adapted to quickly hop out of harms way. It’s not much of a fighter, but can still exhibit feisty temperament! Quite social, they love the presence of other herbivores and have complex hierarchies in their own herds. These relationships are often decided by screaming, and if screaming is not enough, fighting! They are quite bold in the face of predators as well, going into a cacophony of screams and hisses to disorient the unfortunate carnivore and give away its location. And if everything fails, they can always rely on their strong sinuous legs: *Camptosaurus* can assume an extremely fast hopping gait for a short time to make it to the nearest treeline and disappear into the forest.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- *Camptosaurus* is well adapted for open environments, having the speed to quickly dash away from any danger. They can comfortably exist in forests and rocky areas as well, but prefer dry scrubland;
- Prefer to use scrublands, forest edges, canyons and gorges for nesting;
- Colonial nesters: a group can completely occupy a small area with their nests. The seeds and spores that they bring with the food for their young in turn can quite quickly grow in the stable microclimate of the nesting colony, turning into a lush natural cover of greenery that masks the nests from predators.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Often scream if spot predators nearby, alerting the herbivores around;
- Adults of the group can aggressively taunt predators to lure them away from the young;
- When foraging, one of the herd members can stand watch on a rock or a stump as a sentinel;
- Court each-other by gifting nuts, berry branches and nesting materials;
- Often cuddle close together when resting;
- *Camptosaurus* often serve as sentinels of the area, alerting everything around them if they spot danger. They scream at carnivores, preventing them from making an ambush. They are the alarm system of the Garden’s plains and forests!


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 12 individuals in a group, called a hop or a court;
- A group of *Camptosaurus* counts as a *medium* target;
- Immature individuals can be called keets;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- Especially prefer the company of *Stegosaurus* and *Apatosaurus*;
- **Collateral damage**: don’t count as a body down in mixed groups unless specifically being a target of a hunt.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of up to medium size;
- Members of a court will often duel each-other for a place in the hierarchy;
- Immature individuals often fight as well! Nibbling each-other is quite common;
- Adults will viciously mob aggressive critters to protect the young.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Your main defence is to hop away. Make sure to manage your stamina so that you can always have enough for an escape;
- Difficult terrain can interrupt your hopping;
- You can’t take falls very well!
- *Camptosaurus* is very awkward in water;
- Be sure to eat and drink regularly, as your hunger and thirst drain very quick!
- Common nickname: campto;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️ (easy)
