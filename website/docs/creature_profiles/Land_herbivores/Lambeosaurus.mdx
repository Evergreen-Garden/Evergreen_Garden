# Lambeosaurus

- *L. paucidens*: +3% health recovery;
- *L. lambei*: +10% swim speed;
- *L. magnicristatus*: +10% stamina recovery;

🦓 **Size category**: large;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation;

## 🇨🇦 In real life
*Lambeosaurus* was a crested herbivorous dinosaur known from the Late Cretaceous of Canada. It belonged to the family of *Hadrosauridae*, a group of highly successful plant-eaters best known as the “duck-billed dinosaurs”. All hadrosaurs possessed broad beaks for cropping vegetation and bizarre teeth batteries for chewing in the back of the mouth. Most hadrosaurs are also thought to have been facultative bipeds, which means that they could switch their locomotion from 4 legs to 2 legs depending on the situation. *Lambeosaurus* in particular is most famous among its family for having a hatchet-shaped crest on top of its skull.
In the Dinosaur Park formation, *Lambeosaurus* lived alongside its larger relative, *Parasaurolophus*.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Lambeosaurus* is a gentle and elegant plains-dweller. It is a highly musical and flamboyant creature, possessing a hollow head crest that serves for both visual display and as a sound resonator. On its own, *Lambeosaurus* is a very versatile animal, capable of many feats, like running very fast for its size, swimming for long periods, fighting back with powerful hoof kicks and surviving extreme injuries with minimal consequences. But it begins to truly shine when it’s a part of a herd.
The friendly aura of *Lambeosaurus* attracts gatherings of other herbivores, who are inclined to feel safer in its presence. The crested hadrosaur often serves as the heart of a herd, soothing allies with comforting melodies.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- *Lambeosaurus* spend most of their time grazing and browsing in lush grasslands and river valleys, feeling at home in vast open floodplains;
- Some populations use their impressive swimming skills to live in wetlands or reach offshore islands with untouched vegetation;
- Prefer to build nests near big landmark rocks that they use for courtship display;
- Colonial nesters: members of a herd often make nests close together.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Love water, and often swim and splash for fun;
- *Lambeosaurus* are very acoustic and social animals, and communication between herd members is important and comforting for them;
- *Lambeosaurus* subscribes to the notion of “safety in numbers”, often herding together or sticking close to other herbivores;
- Especially enjoy the company of *Parasaurolophus*, *Styracosaurus*, *Albertaceratops* and *Anodontosaurus*;
- The presence and gentle trumpets of *Lambeosaurus* have a calming effect on other herbivores, helping them to snap out of the dread and shock caused by predators;
- Have an incredible immune system that allows them to recover from the most grievous of wounds;
- *Lambeosaurus* stand bipedally when nervous or to assert dominance;
- Court one-another by singing atop a big rock and gifting rocks and twigs to their potential mate.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 4 mature individuals in a group, called a choir;
- A group of *Lambeosaurus* counts as a *huge* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called lambs;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- Are known to adopt any other hadrosaur babies.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of medium, large and huge size;
- Members of a chorus can duel one-another for social status by rearing up on their hind legs and throwing hooves at each-other;


## ☂️ Survival tips
- When solo, *Lambeosaurus* is a master of survival. When in a group, it can serve as a great support dinosaur;
- Instead of crouching, *Lambeosaurus* enters a bipedal stance which makes your front kicks deal more damage!
- *Lambeosaurus* has plenty of ways to outright avoid combat and focus on escaping predators rather than fighting them;
- *Lambeosaurus* is a very good swimmer, and can quickly cross rivers, especially with the right abilities equipped. This can greatly help with dodging predators;
- Your vocal abilities eat up a lot of stamina, so be careful when using them!
- Healing calls can be a life-saver when fighting enemies alone or supporting allies, but remember that bleed negates all healing;
- Combat calls shine the most when you have a powerful ally to buff up;
- Survival calls can make travelling across the map or escaping predators a lot easier when you’re solo;
- Common nicknames: lamb, lambeo;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

- Zebras and horses;
- The presence of goats is known to have a calming and therapeutic effect on nervous horses. This phenomenon can be used to handwave healing and other vocal abilities!
- The elegance and musical vocalisations of a trumpeter swan.
