## 🇵🇱 In real life
*Lisowicia* was an ancient relative of mammals that lived in what is now Poland during the Late Triassic. It was the largest known representative of dicynodonts - a group of tusked herbivorous synapsids. It was also the largest known non-mammalian synapsid. Synapsids would not reach such sizes again until after the extinction of the non-avian dinosaurs.
*Lisowicia* lacked any teeth, and developed pointy flanges on the sides of the beak in place of tusks. Lipie Śląskie, where *Lisowicia* lived, was a wet and swampy area during the Late Triassic, surrounded by arid environments.

### In the Garden
Thick. Round. Rotund. Heavy. Those are the words that come to mind when one lays eyes upon a *Lisowicia*. This water-loving ancient stem-mammal originates from the isolated Red Island and has crossed the seaway onto the shores of Gondwa. Now it enjoys lakes and wetlands of the greater island, laying claim to any hole, puddle or well.
Despite not being adapted to a world ruled by dinosaurs, this wide-boned dicynodont survives by virtue of being dangerously unpredictable, often alternating between states of serene calm and sudden moments of extreme violence. *Lisowicia* exodus into Gondwa may have environment-altering consequences, as the huge dicynodonts transform waterways with their microbiome and feeding habits, becoming the new rulers of the wetland.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Prefer to stick close to water bodies ranging from desert oasises and watering holes to wetlands and marshes;
- Although *Lisowicia* itself is a water-loving animal, other related dicynodonts may exhibit more terrestrial habits, inhabiting arid landscapes;
- Red Island is the ancestral nesting area of *Lisowicia*;
- Choose shaded areas near water for nest-building, as well as small islands;
- Construct an elaborate huge nesting mound to lay their eggs.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Very protective of their home water source;
- Enjoy lying in water throughout the hottest hours of the day, and often come onto land to forage at night or in rainy weather;
- Use “sumo stomps” as a threat display, especially in duels and territorial fights;
- Scream and roar a lot, not being shy about their presence in the slightest;
- Can retain moisture for long periods of time when migrating;
- Can come off as extremely lazy sometimes, lethargically floating in water or resting on the banks. Do not let this fool you!
- Sometimes let small animals inhabit their nesting mounds;
- Love *Hibbertopterus* and their cleaning services;
- Several *Lisowicia* groups can tolerate each-other in one body of water.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 3 mature individuals in a group, called a larder;
- A group of *Lisowicia* counts as a *giant* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called lardlings.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- Love the company of *Halszkaraptor*.

## ⚡ Aggression
- *Lisowicia* can challenge animals of large, huge and giant size with loud roars and by bearing their beak flanges;
- *Lisowicia* can claim a body of water and attack large, huge and giant animals that come near it without warning. These synapsids are very territorial;
- *Lisowicia* that has a claimed water source can interfere in any ongoing conflicts as a 3-rd party as long as they take place in and around its home. It should target the party that fits large, huge or giant size category;
- *Lisowicia* may body-slam any creature that gets too close.
- Like to brawl and duel one-another;
- **Swimmer**: is allowed float in water to escape from a fight.


## 👹 Deviant profile *the Omega Critter*
- *Wadiasaurus indicus* is known to be a small, but feisty herbivore common in the arid parts of the island. However, some field researchers claim that they have seen a giant *Wadiasaurus* variant the size of a rhinoceros…
- Official science dismisses these claims as pure imagination and cases of misidentification, but recently discovered footprints and giant burrows indicate that there may be more to this legend than pure fantasy…
- This cryptid is known as the *Omega Critter*;
- Recommended species: *W. indicus*;
- Recommended skin: “*Critter*”;
- The Omega *Wadiasaurus* prefers to inhabit desert biomes much like its smaller counterpart;
- Like normal *Wadiasaurus*, the Omega Critter possesses extreme territorial aggression and may rush to attack anything without any provocation;
- However, their temper becomes *slightly* cooler if they roam in groups. Grouped Omega Critters should challenge other animals like normal herbivores. Only solitary *Wadiasaurus* may attack without warning. But even grouped Omega Critters are incredibly cranky and scream at everything;
- A group of Omega *Wadiasaurus* is called a fury;
- When a solo Omega Critter makes a kill, it must stay with the body until it decays to 80%;
- As a land-loving animal, The Omega Critter *cannot* claim and defend a body of water, and thus cannot third party like a regular Lisowicia!


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Lisowicia* is an incredible swimmer, you can always rely on water to escape land dangers… Just be wary of crocodiles!
- You take *a lot* of fall damage;
- Use your stamina wisely!
- *Lisowicia* has incredibly slow thirst drain, which allows it to survive without water for very long;
- Common nickname: liso;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
