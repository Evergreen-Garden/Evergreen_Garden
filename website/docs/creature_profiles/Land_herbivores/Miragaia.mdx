## 🇵🇹 In real life
*Miragaia* was a stegosaurid dinosaur from the Late Jurassic of what is now Portugal. Like its relatives, such as *Stegosaurus*, it had two parallel rows of triangular plates on its back and a spiky tail. Unlike most other stegosaurs, however, it had an unusually long neck and tall front legs. It is thought that *Miragaia* used its elongated neck to browse high vegetation inaccessible to other herbivores of its size.
The name *Miragaia* may turn out to be a junior synonym of the genus *Dacentrurus*, another long-necked stegosaur known from the same time period from England.

## In the Garden
*Miragaia* is an oddly shaped stegosaur. With strange ungainly proportions, it looks like a bizarre chimaera of a stegosaur and a sauropod. Yet, in the Garden, this is the least of its weird qualities.
A mysterious and reclusive animal, *Miragaia* displays cryptic habits, being fond of deep overgrown thickets and moonlit nights. With its thin beak it selectively browses on poisonous parts of common plants… For a nefarious reason.
As if things couldn’t get any more unusual, *Miragaia* is the only known poisonous stegosaur. Any predator that would try to take a bite out of *Miragais’s* thick side would find its mouth covered in a thick toxic exudate that disrupts normal nerve activity and causes paralytic effects. This adaptation makes *Miragaia* one of the most unique herbivores in the Garden, a true herald of strangeness.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- *Miragaia* are very shy and elusive creatures that spend the day in thick woodlands browsing and snipping upon a large variety of forest plants;
- During the night, however, they come out of hiding and run around the grasslands, calling to each-other across the hills;
- Such strange habitat shifts are thought to be very important for *Miragaia* to collect all the rare plants and fungi it needs to produce an arsenal of poisons;
- Prefer to nest in forests and lush shrubbery, especially in places rich with mushrooms;
- Sometimes *Miragaia* nests become encircled by fairy rings of mushrooms.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- All *Miragaia* secrete an oil upon their skin that contains neurotoxin. The substance causes involuntary muscle spasms, disruption of motor functions and sickness, but otherwise isn’t lethal and its effects are completely reversible in time. The internal tissues of *Miragaia* aren’t toxic;
- Some Miragaia possess even more toxic properties, with their diet and microbiome allowing them to secrete other poisons. Two additional types are currently known, with one causing severe fatigue, and the other being a dangerous cytotoxin. It is currently unknown what influences the toxins of *Miragaia*, but it most likely depends on the diet;
- Other *Miragaia* specimens don’t secrete additional poisons, using mud wallowing as a defense instead. For such populations wallowing in mud is an important daily routine that helps them to cool off and protect themselves from skin parasites. The animals also seem to find the process of wallowing itself to be highly pleasant;
- Highly enjoy the company of their smaller kin, *Kentrosaurus*, avoiding competition by eating vegetation on different heights.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 4 mature individuals in a group, called a hazard;
- A group of *Miragaia* counts as a *huge* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called steglings;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge medium, large and huge animals;
- Often spit as a form of last warning to bothersome creatures before attacking;
- Members of a hazard can spit at each-other as a way to show discontent.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Venom* slows stamina recovery of your target, making it a good escape tool;
- *Toxin* reduces max health of your target up to 20%, and is a deadly offensive tool;
- *Paralysis* slows down your target, prolongs its cooldowns, and makes it freeze in place for a few seconds upon reaching 100%, which can enable *Miragaia* to escape;
- *Mud* increases your armor, greatly expanding *Miragaia’s* defensive capabilities. Exposure to water washes away mud;
- A lot of the times *Miragaia* can slow its enemies enough to escape without needing to engage in dangerous combat;
- Always be mindful of your stamina!
- Your tail is very short for a stegosaur, so beware opponents with high reach;
- Common nickname: mira;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
