# Eotriceratops

## 🔍 Related genera

🐘 **Size category**: giant;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation;

## 🇨🇦 In real life
*Eotriceratops* was a big horned dinosaur, a close relative of the famous *Triceratops*. It lived during the Late Cretaceous in what is now Canada. Much like its more famous relative, it was a massive animal with three impressive horns and a large rounded neck frill. Such giant ceratopsids existed only for a short time in a geological sense, but are one of the most magnificent and culturally impactful dinosaurs known to science.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Eotriceratops* is the king among ceratopsids. It is the largest horned dinosaur on the island, dwarfing all of its relatives. A massive skull crowned with long elegantly curved horns serves both as a shield and a weapon. The three horned king has a mortal rivalry with another monarch: *T. rex*. While normally a calm animal, some *Eotriceratops* will attack any giant theropod they come across, as such is the instinct calling in their blood.
But the giant ceratopsid not only does taketh away: serving as important ecosystem engineers, *Eotriceratops* are the mighty gardeners, trimming shrubs and breaking trees, they create landscapes and often collect an entourage of smaller creatures looking for protection in their shadow.

## ⛰️ Habitat
- Like to wander scrubland and plains, from lush floodplains to dry prairie;
- Prefer to nest in medium cover: among rocks, shrubbery or small trees.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Some individuals habitually scrape their horns on the ground to mark territory;
- Greatly enjoy mudbaths and like to soak their skin in shallow pools on hot days;
- Engage in courtship display by showcasing their neck frills and mock-fighting. This behaviour resembles a ritualised contactless duel. The animals end up choosing a mate based on being able to successfully synchronise their moves with one-another;
- Live alone, in pairs or satellite other herbivores;
- Overall curious animals and often poke their horned noses around to see what’s afoot;
- Often rest under the shade of lonely trees. Having to move such a heavy head around is tiring!
- Young *Eotriceratops* are rowdy and naughty, and love to chase around critters and smaller herd mates, exercising their itching growing horns.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 2 mature individuals in a group, called a pair;
- A pair of *Eotriceratops* counts as a *colossal* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called scrunglets;
- Ungrouped *Eotriceratops* pairs may sometimes stick together, forming loose herds for travelling. Naturally, when predators strike, *Eotriceratops* will only defend own group.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- Readily integrate into herds of smaller ceratopsids.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of huge, giant and colossal size;
- Lone *Eotriceratops* can attack *giant carnivores* without warning! Against Earth’s mightiest predators, offence is the best defence;
- Same goes for a lone parent raising young;
- Pairs of mature *Eotriceratops* oftentimes still act hostile and scream at giant carnivores, trying to drive them away;
- Often duel and mock-fight;
- Lone individuals, especially ones rearing young, will sometimes defend their home range from other *Eotriceratops* and challenge them off.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Eotriceratops* recovers from bleed, venom, toxin, fracture etc. twice as fast as other animals;
- Your skull is immune to headshot damage multiplier;
- When fighting larger opponents, try to flank them!
- When fighting smaller opponents, play defensively and use terrain;
- If you use “*Sharpen Horns*”, apply it all the time in case of an ambush;
- “*Block*” reduces damage taken on the head and body, but not the tail;
- Common nicknames: eo, eotrike, trike;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (challenging)

## 🐘 Roleplaying inspirations
- The presence and gravitas of an African bush elephant;
- The tenacity and irritability of wild yak.
