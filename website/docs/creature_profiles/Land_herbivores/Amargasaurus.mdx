# Amargasaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *A. cazaui*: +10% armor;
- *A. angulus*: +25% attack knockback, spines cause bleed;
- *A. serratus*: +5% attack damage, spines cause bleed;

🦏 **Size category**: huge;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs and fruit;

## 🇦🇷 In real life
*Amargasaurus* is one of the strangest sauropods ever known to science. It was a long-necked dinosaur that lived during the Early Cretaceous in what is now Argentina. Sauropods were typically gigantic herbivores that used their long necks to browse on treetops. Being on the smaller end of its clade’s size range, *Amargasaurus* was a very different kind of sauropod. It possessed a short neck with extremely tall paired neural spines that could’ve supported either horns or soft-tissue sails. Both speculative variants are present in the game!

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Amargasaurus* is a bizarre miniature diplodocoid. With odd ornaments adorning their necks, they wander the shaded woods and dusty canyons, singing echoing songs to each other and coiling their long tails in the wind.
It is the smallest species of true sauropod on the island. Yet in spite of being outright dwarfed by its colossal kin, *Amargasaurus* remains a huge and tough animal when compared to everything else in the Garden. A slow and compact walking bastion, it is defended by a lengthy whip-tail, and one crack is enough to send most carnivores flying.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Like to roam deserts and woodlands;
- Prefer to nest in forested areas.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Obligate browsers: eat exclusively leafy stems and occasional fruit;
- Since *Amargasaurus* are not interested in nuts and seeds, they are consumed as a byproduct and pass right through. This way the sauropod acts as an important seed disperser for many forest plants;
- Communicate with rumbles and melodic songs, as well as flicking and coiling of the tail;
- *Amargasaurus* do not form families, but life-long friendships instead. Such friendships and alliances often go all the way back to when small sauropodlets had to hide together in the undergrowth;
- Greatly enjoy taking baths in small ponds together to cool off;
- Sometimes even splash in ocean surf for the same reason!
- Attract the attention of mates by singing and showcasing their neck spines or sails.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 3 mature individuals in a group, called a parade;
- A group of *Amargasaurus* counts as a *giant* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called sauropodlets;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- Can adopt small sauropodlets of any species;
- Like to group with *Bonapartenykus*.

## ⚡ Aggression
- May challenge animals of large, huge and giant size;
- Sometimes duel one-another using their bullwhip-tails.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Use your long tail to slap away your enemies! The knockback is the best tool in your kit and can help you consistently whip your opponent away without getting hit in return;
- Sometimes it is possible to whip your enemy from a high place;
- If your foes get too cocky, stomp them!
- Spiky variants of *Amargasaurus* reflect bleed damage upon opponents;
- *Amargasaurus* is a decent swimmer;
- Being a slow herbivore, you need to employ a lot of strategy in choosing which terrain will serve you better in battle;
- Common nickname: amarga;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

## 🐘 Roleplaying inspirations
- African forest elephant;
- Asian elephant;
- The shy attitude of a Minke whale;
- Horned species defend themselves like oryx antelopes. Like oryx and gemsbok, they also like arid habitats!
