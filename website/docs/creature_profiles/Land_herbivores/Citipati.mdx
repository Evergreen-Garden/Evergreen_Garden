# Citipati

## 🔎 Known species
- *C. osmolskae*;
- *C. khondti*;

## 🔍 Related genera
- *Huanansaurus ganzhouensis*;
- *Corythoraptor jacobsi*;

🦌 **Size category**: medium;
🍉 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation, insects, mollusks, bones, meat, fish, carrion;

## 🇲🇳 In real life
*Citipati* was a bird-like theropod dinosaur from the Late Cretaceous of Mongolia. It belongs to the family *Oviraptoridae*, which includes a multitude of similar species of short-skulled crested (and crestless) beaked omnivores. Some of the *Citipati* specimens were found in a brooding position on top of their nests, shedding light on their parental behaviour. The environment in which *Citipati* had lived was made up of sand dunes with ephemeral water bodies, where it would use its strong beak to crush tough vegetation.

## In the Garden
On the isle of Gondwa, *Citipati* is a colourful feathered omnivore, a flamboyant wanderer of The Garden. They run and parade around with their crests held high, booming, chirping and chattering, observing other dinosaurs with bold curiosity. A very social species, they are most famous for being good parents. A flock of *Citipati* is a beautiful sight.
However, there is also a dark side to this colorful birdie… They can be as dangerous as they are majestic.

## ⛰️ Habitat
- Highly nomadic and can be found in a variety of biomes, but prefer deserts and dry woodlands above all else;
- Prefer to nest on the ground in arid biomes near a water source;
- On Beringia mostly stick to forests;
- Colonial nesters: the massive nesting colonies of *Citipati* are famous and one can stretch across a field like a chattering city. A large nesting colony of *Citipati* is known as a *Pandemonium*.


## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Are famous for being very attentive parents, always keeping an eye on their chicks;
- Can be seen carrying their offspring around;
- Often grab random small animals;
- In a group, there exist 2 behavioural subsets of *Citipati*: the *wanderers* and the *guardians*;
- The *wanderers* represent the main bulk of the flock, and are the main foragers and caretakers. They tend to nests, raise the young of the flock, and collect food. They can often be seen carrying food objects around to feed the hungry flock members. In case of danger they are the ones that grab chicks and carry them away;
- The *guardians* are the protectors and sentinels, always on the lookout for danger. They defend the flock and hiss at any potentially dangerous animal that comes close to the group. *Guardians* often moult into very bright colors when they assume their duty. Can go especially berserk when it comes to protecting the chicks;
- All *Citipati* are very curious and talkative animals, always booming, chirping and singing;
- Attract mates with a dazzling courtship display;
- *Citipati* are omnivores with very broad dietary options, and each individual has its own food preferences that often dictate which biomes they choose to travel to exploit those resources;
- Can be friendly with other animals and curiously inspect young dinosaurs of other species;
- Often scream, panic and scatter when startled by larger animals;
- Some misguided individuals are known to attempt courting random dinosaurs of different species;
- Can often be seen brooding over their offspring on a nest;
- Citipati can get quite defensive near carnivores and do not hesitate to show their anger with elaborate threat displays.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 6 mature individuals in a group, called a flock, a festival or a prattle;
- A group of *Citipati* counts as a *large* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called chicks.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores, being a very friendly species;
- Can adopt any other feathered herbivore babies of different species: the instinct to care is strong in them;

## 🍖 Hunting
- **Opportunistic carnivory**: can hunt animals of small size and immature dinosaurs for protein;
- Must follow carnivore hunting rules;
- Other herbivore species never help them to hunt;
- Can freely hunt critters.

## ⚡ Aggression
- May challenge animals of up to *large* size;
- This includes challenging carnivores over carcasses;
- May attack animals of up to *large* size unprovoked if they invade the nesting colony.

## 🍗 Other interactions
- **Scavenger**: may eat or steal from claimed corpses if the owner is not actively eating.


## 👹 Deviant profile *Cemetery Lord* or the *Death Dancer*
- Sometimes nutritionally stressed *Citipati* individuals abandon their usual merry demeanour when their organism starts craving protein;
- They start stalking the island, snatching and killing dinosaurs for food, having developed a taste for flesh;
- Such predatory hermits are called *death dancers* or the *mournful ones*;
- A *death dancer* is locked into using the “*scavenger*” diet;
- There can be a maximum of 2 death dancers in a group!
- A *death dancer* cannot group with any other herbivores while following this profile;
- *Death dancers* can hunt up to *large* sized animals;
- Often assume dark and mournful colors;
- Chronic *death dancers* sometimes even start to decorate their nests with bones and remains of their victims, transforming the nest into a grim cemetery;
- While many *Citipati* can cycle in and out of being a *death dancer* throughout their life, old and sick individuals often never go back to normal, becoming *Cemetery Lords*.

## ☂️ Survival tips
- You can run fast, but remember to keep an eye on your stamina, as you don’t have much!
- Keep foraging! Always keep a look out for food and water, as your thirst and hunger drain quick!
- *Citipati* is an awful swimmer. Floof don’t float;
- Jumping takes a lot of your stamina, especially while you’re still growing;
- Be careful where you sleep, because *Citipati* is a conspicuously loud snorer;
- When *Citipati* is low on stamina, it begins to pant;
- Common nickname: citi;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
