# Anodontosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *A. lambei*: +10% venom and bleed healing;
- *A. obtunsi*: +5% armor;
- *A. rasai*: +10% fracture damage;

🦏 **Size category**: huge;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation;

## 🇨🇦 In real life
*Anodontosaurus* was an armored ankylosaurian dinosaur known from the Late Cretaceous of what is now Canada. Like many derived ankylosaurids, it possessed a tail club, and in *Anodontosaurus* in particular it had a shape especially reminiscent of a hammer. Paleopathologies (traces of injuries on fossils) and the range of motion theorised for ankylosaurid tails suggest that the primary use of the club was to fight other members of its own species.

## In the Garden
*Anodontosaurus* is the living boulder of the Garden. This armorclad herbivore has very little enemies, and those who are foolish enough to try it are quick to learn how easily bones can crack when struck with a tail club shaped like a sledgehammer.
As a living immovable object, *Anodontosaurus* has a fitting personality: very little can surprise or startle this animal. A big bad *T. rex* can huff and puff all it wants, but this brick house on legs will never budge. In the Garden *Anodontosaurus* is unbothered. Happy. In its lane. Focused. Flourishing.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Typically lead shy existence in woodlands and rock fields, but the endless pursuit of food can lead *Anodontosaurus* into any biome;
- Nest in forests, scrublands and canyons in the shadow of berry bushes or close to other edible vegetation;
- Also like to have a shallow forest pond or an oasis present near their nest;

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Have an endless appetite. Spend most of their time munching and foraging;
- *Anodontosaurus* fears almost nothing. No ferocious carnivore or massive herbivore can move or intimidate it. The ankylosaur will always just be on its way, unmoved and unbothered;
- *Stubborn as a rock*;
- Likes to take baths in small water holes on sunny days;
- Sometimes lichen starts to grow on the backs of especially old individuals;
- Most of the time mature individuals are quite intolerant to their own kind, strictly competing for territory;
- Young ‘taters, however, often stick together, forming a group for safety;
- Instead of their own kin, mature *Anodontosaurus* prefer the company of other herbivores, often becoming a satellite of an existing herd;
- In an unlikely event of many *Anodontosaurus* gathering together, they congregate in open plains or near watering holes where everyone can bathe and wallow.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have a maximum of 3 mature individuals in a group, called a rumble;
- A group of *Anodontosaurus* counts as a *giant* target;
- In contrast to lone *Anodontosaurus*, rumbles are often loud, noisy and conspicuous parades of grunting and snorting;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit a group, called ‘taters;

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge large, huge and giant animals;
- Very territorial: upon encountering another *Anodontosaurus*, often immediately challenge it, trying to drive it away from the area. Oftentimes brutal fights break out, with tail clubs swinging and hammering down;
- Intraspecific combat is the most common cause of death in adult *Anodontosaurus* (the second one is hunger);
- Members of the rumble can duel one-another using tail attacks and bone snaps, trying to inflict as much fracture on the opponent as possible. The first one to crack loses!
- Can swat away *Rhamphorhynchus* without a warning. *Anodontosaurus* have a special disdain for the winged pest.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Are quite vulnerable as juveniles, being slow and defenceless. Your best option is to disappear in the dense forest and hide;
- An adult *Anodontosaurus* is a very tough target;
- You can focus on a more defensive stationary build focused on devastating shockwave attacks, or can use a more mobility-focused kit to chase your opponents around and break their ankles;
- Your true greatest enemy, however, is hunger. *Anodontosaurus* is always hungry, and starves out very quickly without constant access to food;
- Common nickname: ano;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
