# Styracosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *S. albertensis*: +2x frill damage;

## 🔍 Related genera
- *Einosaurus procurvicornis*: +10% stamina recovery;
- *Stellasaurus ancellae*: +3% attack damage;

🦌 **Size category**: medium;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation;

## 🇨🇦 In real life
*Styracosaurus* is an iconic ceratopsid dinosaur known from the Late Cretaceous of Dinosaur Park Formation, Canada. It is best known for its skull ornamentation in the form of a long nasal horn and a crown of parietal frill spikes, which were subject to great individual variation. It shared its environment with other famous North American dinosaur species like *Lambeosaurus* and *Daspletosaurus*.
*Einosaurus* and *Stellasaurus* are two other centrosaurine genera from the Upper Cretaceous of North America, each sporting their own strange horn shape!

## In the Garden
Many predators have tried to bring down the one-horned beast, thinking it an easy meal. Many of them found death on the tip of said horn.
In the Garden, *Styracosaurus* is a fierce child of the horned dinosaur family. They roam plains of grass and rocky wastes alone or in herds, sniffing for shrubs, roots and nuts to crack open with their beak. Fearless and bold, they don’t shy away from a fight, ready to fence with their nasal horns. *Styracosaurus* exists on the smaller end of the ceratopsid size spectrum, but is still a big menace!


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Prefer to roam open or semi-open habitats like grasslands, scrublands and desert, but sometimes visit forests;
- Prefer to dig out nests in the shade of lonely standing trees or under the canopy of small groves;
- Colonial nesters: a herd prefers to nest together.

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Gregarious and love being in a herd;
- Act perpetually gruff;
- Adults are very protective of the herd’s young, often forming a “shield wall” between them and the predators;
- Do not form long-term pairs, but all adults in the herd collectively foster the young generation, even taking in orphans;
- *Styracosaurus* are very easily startled due to not having the sharpest eyesight among dinosaurs, and can exhibit extreme aggression and territoriality to unknown animals suddenly approaching;
- Are prone to attack things that enter their personal space;
- Groups often come to shallow ponds and oasises during the heat of the day to cool off, rest and socialise. Juveniles love to play in the mud;
- Sometimes try to bluff and intimidate larger herbivores out of their territory, but hastily retreat if the bluff didn’t work;
- Sometimes lose epiparietal spikes in the heat of combat, but they grow back in time as long as the base isn’t torn off;
- When agitated, can flush blood into their frill, causing it to glow bright orange;
- Often chuff and grunt while moving around.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have a maximum of 6 mature individuals in a group, called a phalanx;
- A group of *Styracosaurus* counts as a *large* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called calves.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- Take comfort in the presence of *Struthiomimus*: a creature that’s all eyes pairs well with a short-sighted one;
- Can adopt small immature ceratopsids of other species.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of up to *large* size;
- Members of a phalanx can duel each-other, with showmanship and intimidation display being as important as the fight itself;
- Calves often hone their fighting skills in play-duels.


## 👹 Deviant profile *the Ronin*
- Sometimes, when a lone *Styracosaurus* is left as a sole survivor of a butchered herd, it develops negative behavioural changes…
- As a response to trauma, its normal social responses deteriorate, and it begins to see the enemy in everything that moves;
- This *Ronin* can’t group with any other creatures, nor it can exhibit friendliness: the blood-soaked path is a lonely one;
- Exception: a *Ronin* can group with up to 4 *Psittacosaurus Doomriders*;
- The *Ronin* can attack animals of up to **large** size unprovoked, without needing to challenge them first. In a sense, it acts more like a carnivore hunting than a herbivore defending its territory. It can slaughter both carnivore and herbivore;
- A *Ronin* must stay with the slain body until it decays to 80%;
- The *Ronin* is not a normal animal. It is suffering from stress and trauma, and its behaviour should reflect that;
- Some *Styracosaurus* manage to eventually recover from this crimson mind fog and return to normal sociality. Others remain wandering in the red mist until they inevitably find a bitter end.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- As with all ceratopsids, your head takes reduced damage!
- Stand your ground against similarly sized opponents! By turning back, you allow faster enemies to chew at your backside for free;
- You are the fastest of the ceratopsids, and your speed allows you to escape larger predators;
- *Styracosaurus* has a very simple ability kit, and is very easy to learn for beginners!
- Common nicknames: sty, styraco;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️ (not very hard)
