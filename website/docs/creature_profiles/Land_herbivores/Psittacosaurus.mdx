# Psittacosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *P. sibiricus;*
- *P. mongoliensis;*
- *P. lujitunensis;*
- *P. gobiensis;*
- *P. sinensis*

🐇 **Size category**: small;
🫐 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation, insects;
🥩 **Supplementary food**: mollusks, meat, fish, carrion, bones;


## 🇲🇳 In real life
*Psittacosaurus* was a genus of early ceratopsian that lived all across Asia during the Early Cretaceous. It is the most species-rich genus of all known non-avian dinosaurs, and one of the most researched dinosaurs in the history of science: hundreds of specimens of all ages are known, and soft tissue and skin imprints help scientists to restore its coloration and life-like appearance.
Unlike its more derived larger horned cousins, *Psittacosaurus* was a relatively small and bipedal animal.
The in-game default skin represents what is known about real life coloration of this animal.

## In the Garden
*Psittacosaurus* is one of the smallest herbivores in the Garden. Active, social and playful, they exist under the feet of giants, thriving and surviving thanks to being able to eat pretty much anything and exist in every habitat. Despite their diminutive size, *Psittacosaurus* possess a bold and stubborn character befitting of a ceratopsian.
They sport a brush of barbed tail quills they can stick into predator’s faces, and some individuals are even known to rub these quills in natural toxins from poisonous plants.

## ⛰️ Habitat
- Very adaptable and can inhabit all biomes from dry deserts to woodlands to grasslands due to their omnivorous generalist diet;
- Prefer their nests to be covered from direct sunlight, sometimes in holes and caves, and also on rocks;
- Colonial nesters: group members prefer to build their nests in close proximity to each-other.


## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Sexual dimorphism: in *P. sibiricus* on average males tend to possess larger facial horns, while the females can more often be found with shorter ones, although this is not a strict trend. Other species of *Psittacosaurus* do not exhibit sexual dimorphism;
- Hyper-social: group members actively communicate by chirping, screaming and rattling their quills;
- Bold, brave and not easily threatened. Their curiosity often overcomes their frear towards dangerous animals;
- Often join herds of larger ceratopsians;
- When resting or foraging, one individual in a group usually assumes the position of a sentinel, looking out for danger from a high position;
- The whole group often burrows into the ground when hearing the sentinel’s cry;
- Often cling onto the sides of random animals for a free ride;
- As omnivores often supplement their diet with carrion, bones and mollusks for calcium and protein.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have a maximum of 12 individuals in a group, called a colony or a legion;
- A group of *Psittacosaurus* counts as a *medium* target;
- Immature individuals are called taquitos.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores;
- **Collateral damage**: upon death don’t count as a body down in mixed groups unless specifically being a target of a hunt.

## 🍖 Hunting
- **Opportunistic carnivory**: Can hunt animals of small size for protein, as well as immature creatures;
- Must follow carnivore hunting rules;
- Other herbivores can’t help *Psittacosaurus* hunt;
- Can freely hunt critters;
- **Partial cannibals**: may hunt unrelated immature *Psittacosaurus* and scavenge dead bodies of their own kind.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of up to *medium* size for resources and territory;
- This includes contesting carnivores for carcasses to get some protein;
- Despite their size, can get fiercely territorial to anything that gets close to their nests;
- Often kill critters that get too close to their nesting colony;
- Members of a colony often duel and bump heads for status.

## 🍗 Other interactions
- **Scavenger**: can eat and steal from claimed corpses when the owner is not actively eating.


## 👹 Deviant profile *The Killer Taco of Caerbannog*;
- Some *Psittacosaurus* that live in nutritionally poor environments develop an unusual taste for flesh;
- These individuals become more antisocial and aggressive, even ravenous;
- They are the most foul, cruel, and bad-tempered basal ceratopsians you ever set eyes on!
- Recommended skin: albino or generally pale;
- Can have up to 4 individuals in a group;
- Can’t group with any herbivores with *two notable exceptions*….
- Can hunt and devour targets of up to *large* size;
- Prefer to set up nests in caves, littering the surroundings with bones;
- But those bloodthirsty little monsters don’t always work alone… Enter…

## 👺 Deviant profile 2 *Doomriders* or *The renegade legion*
- Some *Psittacosaurus* of the wastelands have developed a highly unusual method of acquiring food;
- They find themselves in close association with the enraged lonely deviant *Berserker Albertaceratops* **or** *Ronin Styracosaurus*;
- They follow their ceratopsian host around the badlands, riding on its back and screaming cries of war;
- When the mad ceratopsid gores another victim, the *Doomriders* feast, consuming precious protein that will help them survive in the wastes;
- They can help their temperamental host in all battles, mirroring its savagery;
- A lonely *Berserker* or *Ronin* can have up to 4 *Doomriders* in its group;
- The group must follow carnivore hunting rules and stay with the body until it decays to 80%;
- Fallen *Doomriders* follow *collateral gamage* rules.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Your tiny size lets you escape into tiny holes and burrows where big predators can’t follow you!
- Even adults get attacked by most aggressive critters, so be wary!
- Don’t fall from high places: your weighty little body does not take kindly to gravity;
- Common nickname: taco;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️ (easy)

## 🐿️ Roleplaying inspiration
- Marmots, prairie dogs, groundhogs and other social rodents.
