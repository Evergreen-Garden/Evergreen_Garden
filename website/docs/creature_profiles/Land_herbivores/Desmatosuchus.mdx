# Desmatosuchus

## 🔎 Known species
- *D. spurensis*;
- *D. smalli*;

🦌 **Size category**: medium;
🕑 **Passive growth time**: 2 hours;
🔷 **Group size**: 6

## 🍽️ Diets
- **Garbage Disposal**: leaves, ground vegetation, succulents, reeds, lakeweed, kelp, branches, meat, rotten and poisonous flesh, bones, fish, mollusks, mud, rocks.


## 🇺🇸 In real life
*Desmatosuchus* was a strange armored herbivore that inhabited what is now Texas during the Late Triassic. It belonged to the *Aetosauria* group, and thus was a pseudosuchian, a distant crocodile relative. Despite being on the croc family tree, aetosaurs had teeth adapted for eating plants. They had wide armored bodies. *Desmatosuchus* in particular had massive shoulder spikes and a shovel-like snout hypothesised as a tool for digging up plants. Its remains are commonly found alongside riparian fauna, meaning that it enjoyed living around waterways of its ecosystem. 

## In the Garden
*Desmatosuchus* is an intriguing, primordial creature. Before dinosaurs became dominant terrestrial vertebrates, crocs ruled the world. Some took the form of mighty predators. Others became bootleg ankylosaurs.
*Desmatosuchus* is a short, but confident fellow. It may lack advanced brainpower, but it has all the spirit. An absolute omnivore, armoured from head to tail and an adept swimmer, it is an unrivalled survivalist.
These creatures can be seen weaving through dense, fern-draped undergrowth or wading into shallow water to reach aquatic plants. Their movement is quiet and calculated, yet when threatened, they are capable of surprising agility, leveraging their low profile and muscular frame to make quick sudden lunges.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- **Wetland**: *Desmatosuchus* enjoys spending its time in muddy bogs and swamps digging dirt;
- **Waterside**: *Desmatosuchus* feels at home in and around streams, lakes and puddles;
- **Scrubland**: *Desmatosuchus* can find plenty of food in semi-arid bushlands, such as succulents and eusocial insects.

## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- **Sexual dimorphism**: female *Desmatosuchus* tend to have wider bodies and more curved shoulder spikes than males on average. This is not a hard rule, but merely a statistic;
- Crooked or stunted spikes are common among *Desmatosuchus* individuals of both sexes;
- *Desmatosuchus* moves with deliberate, assured grace;
- Capable of surprising bursts of speed, particularly when startled or when moving between feeding patches;
- Gluttonous little eaters that will try to taste everything they find;
- Enjoy breaking into termite mounds to gorge on the insects;
- Love spending time in mud puddles and often dig around for yummies: what may be garbage for others is a treasure for *Desmatosuchus*!
- Swallow rocks as gastroliths;
- Consume riverbank clay to neutralise poison from succulents;
- Will not pass on the occasional carrion or meat;
-  Can be quite aggressive and territorial towards unrelated *Desmatosuchus*! Males can be especially intolerant;
- Feisty and rather bold creatures (sometimes to their own detrimemt);
- *Desmatosuchus* is surprisingly buoyant and loves to swim;
- Love to play and splash around during rain;
- During hot weather often cool themselves off by passively floating in shallow pools;
- Often venture to explore and forage during nighttime;
- *Desmatosuchus* often forage in groups, but leadership tends to be informal: most often the group follows around the fattest individual, or the one with the biggest shoulder spikes.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 6 individuals in a group, called a testudo;
- A group of *Desmatosuchus* counts as a *large* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed-species grouping
- Can group with any *small*-sized herbivores;
- **Armoured alliance**: can group with *Anodontosaurus* and *Peloroplites*;
- **Wetland crew**: can group with *Lurdusaurus*, *Agathaumas* and *Deinocheirus*;
- Can include 1 deviant *Troubadour Lambeosaurus* in a group.

## 🪺 Reproduction
- **Nest materials**: roots, mud, branches.
- Prefer to dig out nests among scrub, ferns and reeds;
- Young *Desmatosuchus* are called tankettes or piglets;
- *Desmatosuchus* are very oblivious parents that often forget that their hatchlings even exist;
- Exhibit linear growth, having the same growth speed throughout all of their life stages.


## 🦴 Carcass interactions
- Can challenge any creature for a claimed carcass;
- **Corpse Pirate**: can ignore the 2-minute grace period for contesting carcasses;
- Can freely feed from *Desmatosuchus* carcasses. Their brains are too small to tell right from wrong;
- **Scavenger**: can eat or steal from claimed carcasses as long as the owner isn’t actively eating.

## 🌳 Claiming food
- If under 75% hunger, may challenge any feeding herbivores away from a berry bush. Once the defeated party flees the vicinity of the bush, the contest is over, and the victor must eat up.

## 🚩 Claiming territory
- *Desmatosuchus* can claim a POI as its *Wallow* by constructing a nest;
- While wandering through its *Wallow*, *Desmatosuchus* may challenge any animal to leave the POI;
- While holding a territory, *Desmatosuchus* claims all the berry bushes in it and can chase other herbivores away from them regardless of its hunger level;
- Additionally, *Desmatosuchus* claims all water in its territory and can defend it from intruders;
- Can throw dirt onto its opponents in place of a mock attack;
- If the nest is fully destroyed, the territory is considered lost, and *Desmatosuchus* cannot claim it for at least another 15 minutes;
- Display high aggression for ungrouped *Desmatosuchus* in their territory, and try to intimidate their rivals. Brutal shoving contests are common.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- *Desmatosuchus* is superb at overcoming survival difficulties;
- You can eat almost anything except for mushrooms. That includes any plant matter, succulents, insects, bones, any meat and even mud and rocks;
- *Desmatosuchus* does not sink in water and can use this to escape danger;
- In combat, *Desmatosuchus* focuses on switching between defensively standing its ground and rapid charges to punish opponents that turn their backs;
- *Desmatosuchus* has a poor turn radius, but can attack from both ends;
- Common nicknames: desmato, deathtomato;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
