# Iguanodon

## 🔎 Known species
- *I. bernissartensis*: +10% health recovery;
- *I. galvensis*: +10% bipedal armor;
- *I. anglicus*: +10% quadrupedal damage;

🦏 **Size category**: huge;
🌿 **Diet**: shrubs, fruit, ground vegetation;
🌐 **Range**: Gondwa, Beringia.

## 🇧🇪 In real life
Thumbs up! *Iguanodon* was a large ornithopod dinosaur from the Early Cretaceous of Europe. It is one of the first dinosaurs to ever become known to science and is known from incredibly complete material from the mines of Bernissart. The most famous feature of *Iguanodon* are its large thumb spikes that were likely used for intraspecific fighting or predator defence in life. *Iguanodon* could likely switch between bipedal and quadrupedal locomotion.

## In the Garden
Iguanodon is the warrior of the Garden. This martial herbivore is might and courage embodied. Smaller creatures often look for shelter in the presence of an *Iguanodon*, who will rush to defend its herd mates. With arms strong enough to break the spine of an *Allosaurus* in half and massive thumb spikes, *Iguanodon* is not the kind of herbivore to run away. *Iguanodon* is a brave and noble fighter and protector, and is as gentle with smaller herbivores as it is brutal with any meat-eaters stupid enough to threaten its herd.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Prefers open forest and forest-edge habitats, but generally gravitates to more open terrain during the day and clear weather, and retreats into woods and rocky areas during night and rain;
- Prefer to nest in scrublands and on the edge between forest and open field;
- Like to line their nests with ferns and greenery, and sometimes bring roots from fallen trees to create shade for their eggs;
- Colonial nesters: herd members often use a common nesting ground;

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Can be found foraging alone, but sometimes gather in great numbers;
- Often join herds of smaller herbivores, thus combining the alertness of small animals with *Iguanodon’s* might;
- Habitually walk on two legs when being curious, trying to get a better view of their surroundings or when greeting one another;
- *Iguanodon* is usually a calm and collected animal, keeping its nerve even in the face of danger;
- Often selflessly defend their family and herd members;
- Migratory and like to wander and explore.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 3 mature individuals in a group, called a cohort;
- A group of *Iguanodons* counts as a *giant* target;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called iglets;
- Several groups may travel together as a herd.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Welcome any herbivores into the group;
- Can adopt any baby land herbivores.

## ⚡ Aggression
- Can challenge animals of large, huge and giant size;
- Friendly to other herbivores in its personal space for the most part;
- Does not tolerate carnivores anywhere close;
- Males are prone to bullfight each other to show strength.


## 👹 Deviant profile *the Paladin*;
- The disdain that *Iguanodon* instinctively feels for carnivores is very well known. In some individuals it goes so far that they strive to prevent any predator attacks that they witness. When seeing a hunt unfold, they rush to the defence of their fellow herbivores, even if they gain nothing from it. No herbivores blood shall be spilled under their watch!
- These altruistic individuals are known as *Paladins*;
- A *Paladin* can interfere with an ongoing conflict as a 3rd party to defend any herbivores __below giant size__ from any carnivores;
- It cannot join the group of the defending herbivores until the hunt is over;
- A *Paladin* can only follow this profile when solo. If the *Paladin* joins any herbivore group, it can’t act upon this profile until it is alone again.

## ☂️ Survival tips
- Instead of crouching, *Iguanodon* can assume a bipedal posture that allows it to use different attacks, grants increased turning and stamina recovery, but slows it down;
- Learn how to quickly switch stances to use different attacks! Some attacks will switch your stance automatically;
- A build focused on a bipedal mode is defence-oriented, allowing *Iguanodon* to stand its ground against all but giant carnivores and force them to eat spikes to the face;
- A build focused on the quadrupedal stance is more mobile and offensive, allowing *Iguanodon* to actively charge down opponents and trade blows to its rear with leg kicks;
- Common nickname: iggy;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)

## 🦌 Roleplaying inspirations
- Sable antelopes often confront their predators head on and actively fight them;
- The noble and proud nature of large deers like elk, red deer and sambar;
- The deviant profile is based upon real behaviour some large ungulates rarely exhibit, disrupting predator attacks on other species.
