# Yunnanosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *Y. youngi*;

🦏 **Size category**: huge;
🕑 **Passive growth time**: 3 hours;
🔷 **Group size**: 3

## 🍽️ Diets
- **Herbivore**: leaves, fruit;
- **Forager**: flowers, nuts, roots, fruit.

## 🇨🇳 In real life
*Yunnanosaurus* was an early long-necked dinosaur that lived during the beginning of the Jurassic in what is now Yunnan, China. It was a more primitive relative of the famous giant sauropods, but unlike them, *Yunnanosaurus* walked on only 2 legs. The arms of *Yunnanosaurus* bore huge hooked claws likely used for defense and foraging.
There existed several different species of *Yunnanosaurus* with a massive size difference between them. *Yunnanosaurus youngi* was by far the largest.

## In the Garden
In the Garden, *Yunnanosaurus* is an odd barrel-bodied creature from a forgotten ancient lineage. It belongs to “prosauropods”, the precursors of the long-necked sauropods.
To spot this bizarre, lumbering, primordial creature waddling through a forest is a strange sight. But in spite of its funny appearance, *Yunnanosaurus* is a dangerous and unpredictable animal. The slightest irritation can trigger *Yunnanosaurus* to go into blind territorial aggression and tear the intruder apart: what happens inside the prosauropod’s tiny head is an enigma, and sometimes it lets intrusive thoughts win.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- **Woodland**: *Yunnanosaurus* prefers the safety of shaded forests.

## 🌿 Roleplaying guidelines
- **Sexual dimorphism**: males often possess a ridge of dermal spikes along the back that females lack. This is not a hard rule, but merely a statistical curiosity;
- Stand upright to intimidate and assert dominance;
- Also stand upright to greet one another and perform courtship display;
- Generally noisy, talkative animals and communicate with bellowing calls, bobbing their heads and showing off their dewlaps;
- Often drum their bellies as a threat display, both for predators and rivals;
- When congregating in large numbers, often challenge one-another for a duel, brutally slamming, necking and clawing. Such violence is most common among males, but not exclusive to them;
- *Yunnanosaurus* generally has a mean, stubborn and irritable character. It is not a type of herbivore to passively tolerate any nuisance in its personal space.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have up to 3 individuals in a group, called a slog or a marching band;
- A group of *Yunnanosaurus* counts as a *giant* target;
- Immature individuals don’t count towards the group limit;
- Grouped members can all defend each-other if attacked.

## 🧩 Mixed-species grouping
- Can group with any *small*-sized herbivores;
- **Sauropod parade**: can group with *Amargasaurus*, *Apatosaurus* and *Argentinosaurus*;
- Can include 1 deviant *Troubadour Lambeosaurus* in a group.

## 🪺 Reproduction
- **Nest materials**: rock, mud;
- Prefer to dig out simple nests in forest clearings;
- **Colonial nesters**: herd members prefer to nest in close proximity;
- Young *Yunnanosaurus* are called pea pods;
- *Yunnanosaurus* exhibit limited parental care, but may still protect their creche from danger;
- Mature quickly, but growth gradually slows down at later life stages.

## 🌳 Claiming food
- If under 75% hunger, may challenge any feeding herbivores away from a berry bush. Once the defeated party flees the vicinity of the bush, the contest is over, and the victor must eat up.

## 🚩 Claiming territory
- A *Yunnanosaurus* can claim a POI as its *Garden* by constructing a nest;
- While wandering through its *Garden*, *Yunnanosaurus* may challenge any terrestrial animal to leave the POI;
- While holding a territory, *Yunnanosaurus* claims all the berry bushes in it and can chase other herbivores away from them regardless of its hunger level;
- If the nest is fully destroyed, the territory is considered lost, and *Yunnanosaurus* cannot claim it for at least another 15 minutes;
- In general, *Yunnanosaurus* is very territorial and starts challenging other animals at the slightest irritation;
- Some individuals, especially males, are principled loners that display aggression towards other *Yunnanosaurus* encroaching on their *Gardens*.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Instead of crouching, *Yunnanosaurus* assumes an upright stance which allows it to use “*belly drum*”;
- *Yunnanosaurus* is quite speedy for its size. It is  important to know when to fight and when to flee!
- Body slams can leave you vulnerable for attacks against more nimble opponents;
- Upright stance makes you immune to knockback, and low stance makes you replenish stamina quicker;
- *Yunnanosaurus* is a very good swimmer;
- Common nickname: yunna;
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ (moderately challenging)
