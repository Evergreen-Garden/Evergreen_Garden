# Argentinosaurus

## 🔎 Known species
- *A. huinculensis*;
- *A. coria*;
- *A. titanus*;

🦕 **Size category**: colossal;
🌿 **Diet**: just about any plant matter;

## 🇦🇷 In real life
*Argentinosaurus*, a long-necked dinosaur known from the Late Cretaceous of Argentina, was possibly among the top largest land animals to ever live. It belonged to the Titanosauria clade - a group of highly successful Cretaceous sauropods that could reach enormous sizes even by sauropod standards. Life on land has never been this big before or after them.
Like most sauropods, *Argentinosaurus* was highly adapted to living at the limits of size possible for terrestrial animals. It had a highly pneumatised skeleton like modern birds, and a sophisticated way for breathing.
A limb bone of *Argentinosaurus* was first mistaken for a petrified log.

## In the Garden
*Argentinosaurus* is a mountain on the move. A citadel of flesh and bone. With each breath it inhales a windstorm. With each heartbeat it pumps away a river of blood. Each footstep it makes creates a small habitat. Holding its head far above everyone and everything, the titanosaur lives in a different world from any other animal of the Garden.
When they are hatched, *Argentinosaurus* young feed all kinds of predators. As they live, they create ecosystems, fertilising the land, clearing vegetation and digging canals. When they die, their bodies become blooms of life, feeding the earth for years. A life of even a single *Argentinosaurus* leaves a colossal footprint on the Garden. Look upon it with awe and trepidation, for it is the greatest of its beasts.


## ⛰️ Habitat
- Prefer to dig nests in wide open arid sandlots, dried lakebeds and salt flats;
- Colonial nesters: *Argentinosaurus* from all around come to use common nesting fields;
- Youngsters prefer obscured habitats like canyons, scrublands and dry forests;
- Adults prefer to roam open arid environments;

## 🦎 Roleplaying guidelines
- Sexual dimorphism: male specimens tend to have a row of dermal spikes along their spines, while females more often have smooth backs. This is not a hard rule, but merely a statistic;
- Often sing and communicate over long distances;
- Bellow at the moon on starry nights;
- Love to take baths in lakes and oasises, wallowing their enormous bodies to cool off;
- Mostly live alone and don’t have a social structure, but don’t mind the company of other *Argentinosaurus*, occasionally congregating en masse;
- Possess a calm, unhurried demeanor: mature *Argentinosaurus* live in slow motion;
- Like to observe the life of other animals;
- Sometimes accidentally step on small things;
- Occasionally visit marine coasts to wash off dust and take in more salt with coastal plants;
- Court one-another by singing gentle melodies and slowly waltzing like a senior couple;
- Youngsters may stick to adults for protection, but there is little parental bond present between them;
- Often travel in an entourage of small animals like *Bonapartenykus*.


## 💚 Grouping
- Can have 2 mature individuals in a group, called a pair;
- Can have as many immature individuals as fit in a group, called sauropodlets;
- A group of sauropodlets is called a creche;
- A herd of more than 2 mature *Argentinosaurus* is called a *pantheon*.

## 🧩 Mixed grouping
- Can group with any herbivores.

## ⚡ Conflicts
- *Argentinosaurus* can challenge animals of giant size and above;
- *Argentinosaurus* usually ignores smaller animals, but may still swing and stomp at lesser creatures in their personal space without warning - for it simply does not care. For a titanosaur all others are mere insects to be trampled under foot. You move out of the way or you become a pancake;
- Sometimes tensions arise among a pantheon, and *Argentinosaurus* may duel for resources and nesting spots using their necks;

## 🪦 Other interactions
- A dead *Argentinosaurus* is considered a **Whalefall**: its body may be simultaneously claimed by several groups of carnivores and provides food for entire ecosystems.


## 👹 Deviant profile *The Mad Titan*
- Some especially ancient *Argentinosaurus* can start to develop a really bad temper;
- Decades spent defending their territory makes them both ruthless and reckless;
- Such a *Mad Titan* patrols the lands in search of other *Argentinosaurus* to battle and bring down. It may attack others of its own kind without warning and with lethal intent;
- When titans battle, the land quakes;
- A *Mad Titan* does not act friendly with other animals;
- A *Mad Titan* cannot group with any other animals;
- Some *Mad Titans* can be over a century old.


## ☂️ Survival tips
- Young *Argentinosaurus* sauropodlets are incredibly slow, defenceless and  generally useless. The best way to survive is to hide away from populated areas!
- Mature *Argentinosaurus* are virtually immune to predation. Only pairs of highly experienced carcharodontosaurs may even pose a remote threat to the well-being of the colossus;
- Your most damaging weapon is your tail;
- You control the direction of your stomps with your camera;
- The masssive *Argentinosaurus* is the slowest animal in The Garden. Plan your journeys carefully!
- Avoid canyons, for it is easy to get stuck.
- Common nickname: argent, arg;
- Growing difficulty: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (very challenging)
- Survival difficulty: ⭐️ (easy)
